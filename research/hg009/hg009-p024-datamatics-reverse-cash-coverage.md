The middle stress row represents an **additional independent reserve of Rs 209.69 crore** in addition to the named acquisition payable, not a claim that the actual acquisition payable is double. The 16x terminal cash multiple is arbitrary and not proven as market implied value. No discounting, growth, distributions, minority claims or execution frictions are modeled. This is **not enterprise value, not intrinsic value, not forecast owner cash**, and this table cannot be used to rank for investment.

Earlier P023 tightened FY26 CFO less PPE, interest and lease principal to approximately **Rs 246.70 crore**, which is still not sustainable parent-attributable owner cash. It sits below the conditional 16x historical parity cash hurdle of Rs 266.10 crore, and below the zero-available-cash case Rs 292.95 crore. A modest illustrative gap does NOT establish fair value or margin of safety. At 12x it is materially further below. Reported Q1 margin growth is promising but not independently demonstrated to persist.
