## Stronger controls, not convenient benchmarks
Select the cohort without looking at future outcomes, price, recent growth or attractiveness. Use exact current Q2 and previous Q1 of FY27, prefer consolidated owner PAT. Prior quarter derived from H1 minus Q2 in the retained pipeline. Retain all identity/scope/source/clock fields. A 0.05cr ownership/PBT reconciliation gate is conservative and can block legitimate associates/rounding rather than prove bad accounting. Exclusion reasons are overlapping. Prior-year quarterly fields are largely absent in this source, so do not create fake seasonal data. Original XBRL semantics, e.g. zero owner PAT placeholders, may still fail; treat extracted forecasts as provisional inputs, do not retrofit the frozen packet if later source review discovers an error. Record a separately dated correction or invalid row, with both source-eligible and original denominator counts. Mechanical driver uses only verified-shape inputs, excludes sector flags, signed exceptional items or unstable tax/losses. Sector flags imported and expanded by named financial/holding-company exclusions, not a full verified industry classification. 38 driver forecasts, 62 abstentions; explicit reasons in JSON. This is an accounting comparator, not LLM intelligence.
