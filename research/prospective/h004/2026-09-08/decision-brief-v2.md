# H004 / current-market decision brief v2 — 2026-09-08

Status: **RESEARCH ONLY — LIVE CAPITAL DISABLED**

This brief is a breadth correction to `current-screen-v1.md`. It does not change frozen H004-R001. It separates three concepts that must not be mixed:

1. fresh pre-momentum information events,
2. older earnings gaps that remain fundamentally interesting but may be stale as H004 events,
3. already-repriced or non-qualifying names.

## Structural finding: event-age staleness

H004-R001 deliberately froze pre-momentum, earnings-quality and execution thresholds, but it did not freeze a maximum age for a Stage-1 information event. A July result can therefore remain on the watchlist in September if price thresholds remain satisfied.

That is a causal weakness. It is recorded here rather than retroactively changing H004-R001 after looking at current candidates. Historical replay must test the frozen rule as written. Any future event-expiry rule must be a separately versioned hypothesis/rule.

For practical current research, older events are marked `STALE_EVENT_RISK` rather than treated like fresh catalysts.

## Current two-lens ranking

A single rank is misleading. The research objective and the investment-quality objective are related but different.

These names have the strongest current combination of information change, insufficient/early price recognition and potential price elasticity. This is **not** a valuation ranking.

1. **DIACABS — Diamond Power Infrastructure**
   - strongest raw H004 earnings/catalyst setup,

   - but ~100x P/E and >150% six-month rerating make downside severe if execution disappoints.

2. **BDL — Bharat Dynamics**
   - real operating turnaround plus fresh Sep-7 defence-sector procurement catalyst,
   - but other income was ~62.5% of Q1 PBT and valuation is around 88x earnings,

3. **HLEGLAS — HLE Glascoat**

4. **GESHIP — Great Eastern Shipping**
   - strong earnings, active buyback and a fresh tanker/shipping macro regime,
   - much cheaper than the other high-convexity names,

5. **SHAREINDIA — Share India Securities**
   - exceptionally clean Q1 result and persistent information-to-price gap,
   - around 10x earnings,
   - event itself is older, so current H004 causal freshness is weaker even though risk/reward remains attractive.

## Stage-2 state at the checked Sep-8 morning snapshot

No top latent name was promoted to Stage 2 from the checked price data alone:

- DIACABS was roughly -0.7%,
- SHAREINDIA was roughly flat/slightly negative,
- DEEPAKNTR was roughly +0.3%,
- HLEGLAS was roughly +1.5%.

H004 requires at least two trigger conditions, so even a +2% price move alone would still be insufficient. The preferred trigger remains a modest recognition day plus abnormal volume/delivery or strong sector-relative performance, not a late 10-20% chase.

## Decision

The current research posture is therefore:

- **Best risk-adjusted latent names:** SHAREINDIA, GESHIP, BSE.
- **Highest explosive but high-risk setup:** DIACABS.
- **Fresh sector/catalyst setup with quality warning:** BDL.
- **Fresh special-situation catalyst:** HLEGLAS.
- **Fundamental cyclical value outside clean current H004:** NALCO.
- **Do not chase / already repriced:** PTCIL, GE Vernova T&D, Solar Industries, Aarti Pharmalabs.
- **Do not trust headline earnings without adjustment:** Dixon Technologies.

No name is considered an H004 executable paper entry until Stage-2 confirmation is observed under the frozen rule.
