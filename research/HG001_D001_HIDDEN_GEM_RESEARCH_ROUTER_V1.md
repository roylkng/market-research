## Purpose

Join independent evidence planes across the 2,319-name SS001 market universe to route
scarce deep-research and LLM effort.

HG001-D001 is not a stock score and does not predict returns.

## Frozen source planes

- SS001-I001 small-sum liquidity/capacity context;
- EI001-S001 earnings-inflection router;
- HA001-D001 asset/capacity anomaly router;
- GF001-D002 governance/ownership fact plane;
- SS002-D001-P2 current special-situation event spine.

Every source is point-in-time and outcome-blind.

## Independent opportunity lanes

A company can activate zero to three opportunity lanes.

### EARNINGS_INFLECTION

Active when EI001-S001 state is:

- STRONG_INFLECTION; or
- SUPPORTED_INFLECTION.

### ASSET_OR_CAPACITY_ANOMALY

Active when HA001-D001 contains at least one frozen opportunity flag:

- LIQUID_ASSET_HEAVY;
- INVESTMENT_HOLDING_HEAVY;
- INVESTMENT_PROPERTY_MATERIAL;
- CWIP_CAPACITY_INFLECTION.

### CURRENT_SPECIAL_SITUATION

Active when SS002-D001-P2 has at least one
`CURRENT_INVESTABLE_IDENTITY` event for the symbol.

The number of active opportunity lanes is descriptive only.

## Governance red-team context

GF001 is never treated as an upside signal.

Frozen governance caution flags:

- PROMOTER_PLEDGE_PRESENT;
- PROMOTER_NDU_PRESENT;
- PROMOTER_OTHER_ENCUMBRANCE_PRESENT;
- PROMOTER_REDUCTION_2PP when adjacent-quarter promoter ownership delta <= -2.0 pp;
- GOVERNANCE_SOURCE_INCOMPLETE when latest CORE_READY evidence is unavailable.

Any governance caution routes the company through a mandatory governance red-team before
an investable thesis may be sealed.

No company is automatically rejected solely by these flags in D001.

## Asset caution context

Retain HA001 cautions:

- INTANGIBLE_HEAVY_CAUTION;
- WORKING_CAPITAL_HEAVY_CAUTION.

They do not erase opportunity flags.

## Liquidity context

Carry SS001-I001 liquidity band and execution surfaces.

Liquidity does not alter opportunity-lane count or exclude research.

## Neglect context

Being outside U001 is descriptive only and does not add an opportunity lane.

## Priority ordering for research capacity only

Within each route, deterministic display order is:

1. active opportunity lane count descending;
2. governance caution count ascending;
3. asset caution count ascending;
4. symbol ascending.

Liquidity, U001 membership and market price do not affect ordering.

This ordering allocates research attention; it is not expected-return ranking.

## Scientific boundary

- blend signals into a numeric alpha score;
- infer intrinsic value;
- estimate completion probability;
- predict returns;
- exclude a company because it is small or illiquid;
- create ADO/PF001 eligibility;
- authorize live capital.
