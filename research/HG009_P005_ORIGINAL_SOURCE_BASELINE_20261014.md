# HG009 P005: Source-only comparability audit before NSE return labels

**Freeze date:** October 14, 2026. **Source time:** original HG001 full-market October 5, 2026, before experimental cohort entry.

**Status:** baseline confounder diagnostics, not an investment strategy, return ranking, model or investment approval.

## First-principles problem

HG009 selected 100 companies with two or three flagged opportunity lanes, and two 100-company comparison groups with one and zero opportunity lanes. Matching was **only historical cash-market traded-value bands** plus exclusion from the separate U001 cohort and source governance/asset cautions. This does not establish similar companies by growth stage, size, industry or financial-reporting regime.

If the higher-lane group subsequently outperforms, at least four incompatible explanations remain:

1. Opportunity indicators identified genuine mispriced business growth and corporate-event payoffs.
2. Selected companies were already growing faster, for which investors paid different starting valuations.
3. Sector, capitalization, borrowing and accounting differences explain the future return spread.
4. Observational price-index and market prices differ from executable after-cost portfolio performance.

The P005 audit measures **prior earnings and accounting baseline differences** before opening October 15+ return labels. It makes no winner forecast.

- current PBT margin %, YoY margin change in percentage points, finance-cost YoY %;

Every statistic retains the **100-company denominator** and exact observed/missing counts. Undefined EPS and revenue must not be imputed to justify an alpha claim. The mean is susceptible to base effects, so report median alongside it and preserve extremes.

## What this does not prove

No industry, market capitalization, enterprise value, financial versus industrial accounting normalization, current spread or bid/ask execution capacity, future return, model probability, fraud finding or fair value is inferred.

Exact matching on coarse historical liquidity bands does not make firms exchangeable. P005 must not be fed into the already-frozen 100-name selection, nor treated as a new pre-October-14 selection method. It is interpretation context only.

Future scientific validation needs source-backed sector/size/industry controls, appropriate bank/NBFC accounting, corporate actions/dividends, actual costs and independently frozen later cohorts. No repeatable LLM-generated alpha can be claimed from one 300-name cross-section.
