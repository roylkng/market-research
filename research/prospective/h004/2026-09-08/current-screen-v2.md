## Historical falsification status

H004-HR001 remains intentionally uncomfortable. The small out-of-design manual challenge catches Entero Healthcare and Bodal Chemicals but misses/rejects Huhtamaki India, Oriental Aromatics and VTM. Naive winner-only recall is therefore about 2/5 = 40%, below the frozen >=50% gate.

That is **not** a valid gate estimate because the sample omits the full eligible universe, false positives and exact 20-session labels. It is nevertheless a warning. Thresholds will not be relaxed to improve this number.

If the broad point-in-time replay remains below the frozen recall gate, H004 fails. Flow-driven moves without public information anchors belong in a separately frozen anomaly experiment rather than being retrofitted into H004.

## Bottom line

The current best H004 question is not "which stock is rising?" It is:

> Which company has had a material, high-quality information change that has not yet been adequately reflected in price, and then begins to show the first executable evidence of recognition?

On the evidence available at this intraday snapshot, ADVAIT, DEEPAKFERT, GESHIP, SHAREINDIA and DIACABS are the five highest-priority names for that question, with DIACABS carrying by far the largest valuation/rerating warning.
