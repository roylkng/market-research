## Objective

Prospectively confirm whether the frozen T005/T006 ten-feature NSE stock-futures
family adds 5-session cross-sectional information when the decision is made only
after the official same-session FO bhavcopy is actually published.

T012 exists because T006's 18:30 IST source gate is operationally incompatible
with the observed NSE FO publication schedule, while T011 showed that simply
lagging futures information by one session does not preserve the historical edge.

T012 does not modify T006 or reinterpret T011.

## Decision sources

For decision session D:

### Cash/delivery gate

SC001 must contain a same-session attempt with:

    eligible_before_cutoff = true

under SC001's original 18:30 IST contract.

This ensures exact current-session UDiFF cash and delivery bytes already existed
well before the late T012 decision.

### Futures gate

SC002 must contain a same-session attempt where:

- futures.status = READY;
- actual captured_at_utc <= frozen T012 cutoff timestamp for D.

SC002's original eligible_before_cutoff field is NOT used because it refers to
T006's immutable 18:30 cutoff.

The earliest READY attempt satisfying T012's frozen cutoff is authoritative.
