# HG009 P027: evidence-gated response to the first negative NSE experiment

**Audit date:** 21 October 2026, before the 22 October H5 market close.  
**Program:** systematic discovery of mispriced Indian equities and prospective, cost-adjusted investability, not an attractive stock-screening presentation.  
**Decision:** NO SUPPORTED INVESTABLE ALPHA at the present evidence standard. This does **not** mean that future alpha has been statistically disproved.

## What actually happened

The original 100 HG009 selected companies and two 100-company control arms were source-frozen October 14, selected from an October 5 source-only full NSE router. The first Oct 15 source-return diagnostics are a **single day of original unadjusted NSE stock price changes**, not three hundred independent tests.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Selected, 2 or 3 opportunity lanes | 99/100 | -1.410336% | 1 |
| One-lane source-matched control | 99/100 | -1.268997% | 1 |
| Zero-lane source-matched control | 100/100 | -1.076299% | 0 |

The selected-minus-one-lane difference is **-0.141339 percentage points** and selected-minus-zero-lane **-0.334037 pp**, conditional on observed NSE EQ sources. The NSE Nifty 500 **price** index gained +0.5738% in the same session. The relative index difference is not a portfolio hedge P&L, nor proof that a strategy can be shorted or traded at official open/close.

P025 was formulated on October 21 **after seeing Oct 15**. A posthoc revenue-growth-bucket adjustment kept both comparisons negative: **-0.220359 pp** versus one-lane and **-0.188974 pp** versus zero-lane. This is a diagnostic sensitivity, NOT an original preregistered prediction, and cannot justify creating/reweighting a buy portfolio.

## Why we should not optimize on these returns

P026 reconstructed October 16 NSE industry source observations, but from a **Nifty Total Market index subset** of only approximately 750 stocks, not the entire original 2,319 NSE universe. Exact sector source was found for only **142/300** original frozen identities: selected 46, one-lane 43, zero-lane 53. Original source day was October 16, **after** the October 15 entry. These labels cannot be backdated into the Oct 14 experimental design.

Only twenty source-industry complete selected/one/zero triplets can exist under that source. That is a ceiling on an exploratory matched subset, not twenty independent economic exposures or a valid sector-size-controlled alpha result.

The original issuer P008 ten dossiers and four deep-value L001 cases also report **zero** audited-normalized, minority/dilution-reconciled, probability-calibrated, after-cost valuations. For ACE, FY26 CFO-minus-PPE grew mostly because of **lower capital expenditure**, not a commensurate increase in CFO. Gross account balances, acquisition proceeds and holdings do not automatically become shareholders' liquid distributable cash.

### Market-mechanics confounder uncovered

India's NSE **Closing Auction Session** applies in phase one to derivative-eligible stocks. NSE's official CAS documentation and the SEBI review published **October 15, 2026** describe changes to cash-market closing price mechanics and derivative settlement, with the revised operational arrangements aimed at December 7. Sources:

- https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session
- https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=1&smid=0SEBI&ssid=7

CAS eligibility by security, relevant effective date, and cash-market close methodology must be independently source-bound before a **new** experimental entry, and not guessed from stock names or inferred from losing H1 performance. The first HG009 H1 close already uses the original exchange-published value but its cash price is not necessarily an executable auction fill. The first-day negative gap could also be explained by sector, liquidity, size, beta and portfolio-weight differences.

## Freeze scientific admission criteria for the next independent experiment

The **current 300 companies and their original H1/H5 labels are immutable**. No rescue by changing sample, sector labels, weighting, cutoff or model on the basis of H1/H5.

A genuinely **new** future cohort requires, before its first market entry:

1. Fully archived original as-of NSE issuer/ISIN identity and original cash-price sources, including actual retrieval UTC. **At least 95% complete sector source across the new cohort, and 90% within each arm**. Otherwise fail the full-cohort inferential gate and retain the unmatched names as missing, not substitute.
2. Independently dated **sector, industry, float-adjusted size, liquidity, pre-entry price momentum/beta, and financial-versus-industrial accounting regime**. A complete index subset is not sufficient if it excludes many NSE SMEs. Source audited business-segment revenue under NSE's industry policy, using an explicit UNKNOWN if no reliable document exists.
3. True matched or source-weighted comparator cohorts with declared weights and measurable standardized mean differences. Any weak-overlap strata and effective sample-size loss must be reported, not hidden.
4. Predeclared next-cohort market entry, fixed subsequent **60/120/252 trading-session** outcomes and original NSE corporate actions, dividend entitlements, issuer splits, rights, and ISIN replacements. Obtain **official 2027 exchange holiday calendar** before 2027-specific dates are frozen.
5. **Execution feasibility**: independent original spread/depth, capped participation, impact/taxes/brokerage and bid-ask asymmetry, including missing/suspended/delisted names. A trade-free market-return diagnostic cannot establish a tradable portfolio.
6. **Statistical independence**: multiple separately frozen future decision times, no calendar-contiguous H1/H5 overlap counted as independent trials, sector and accounting-model attribution, uncertainty under serial correlation, and multiple-comparisons correction. A single year with 100 symbols is **not 100 independent strategy track records**.
7. Parallel bottom-up owner-cash valuation work is separately scored. Each company needs original consolidated source custody, parent-attributable normalized earnings, capital reinvestment, minority/lease/debt and latest fully diluted shares, cash-access restrictions and valuation downside. The issuer P008 DCF is blocked until these conditions are actually present.

A future trial may pass *research admission* only after all required inputs are genuinely timestamped **before its entry**. This P027 audit cannot activate that future trial by fiat.

## No premature promotion to investment decisions

Until documented shareholder owner cash, valuation probability bounds, corporate-action share basis, and realistic transaction costs are credible, **no company from these experiments is a verified bargain**. The real payoff target is not winning a one-day screen but finding repeatably underpriced *long-run distributable owner cash*.
