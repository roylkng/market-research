# Historical decision reconstruction and current confidence update

Observed: 17 November 2026. **RETROSPECTIVE_RECONSTRUCTION_NOT_BLIND_BACKTEST. No live-capital approval.** This extends the discovery work in PR #620, without rewriting previous judgments or frozen H/PF/HG/AE experiments.

## Result and validity

Six annual checkpoints in the two already-selected candidates produce three plausible historical BUY_CANDIDATE judgments, two useful waits and one important missed recovery. These are reconstructed judgments, NOT predictions recorded in those years. They cannot establish an accuracy rate or strategy alpha.

A local protocol selected 16 November 2022/2023/2024 cutoffs, next-session opening reference prices and one-calendar-year horizons before detailed outcome lookup. No numerical purchase rule or verdict was frozen then. The model may already know outcomes, and outcomes were visible before the final judgments. The two surviving companies were selected by the current shortlist, not from a contemporaneous investable universe. Three dates for the same issuer are not independent samples. Historical BUY_CANDIDATE means the examined economics support considering entry, subject to remaining governance/implementation diligence, not completed capital eligibility.

## The miss must change the process, not the old verdict

Datamatics2024 is a false negative for a static screen. By October2025 H1 owner EPS rose14.54 to19.22, about32%, while the opening-reference price gain was67%. Both business recovery and valuation expansion mattered. Do not rewrite the November2024 wait into a successful buy.

The15May2025 issuer release, published after that day's close, reported Q4 revenue growth20.5% and EBITDA growth15.3%. This illustrates an evidence event that should force a new review. No newly invented May trading rule or associated alpha is asserted. A WAIT requires a specific review date or material-evidence trigger. Deterioration may route a solvent business into a recovery queue rather than an indefinite rejection.

## Research changes and evaluation limits

- Retain a recovery queue with explicit reopening evidence and review dates. Score opportunity cost of waits as well as avoided losses.
- Separate operating cash, sustaining investment, growth capex, acquisitions/earnouts and distributions.
- Keep earnings growth, valuation change and sector-cycle effects distinct. Do not assume all returns came from stock-picking skill.
- Treat these six seen examples as a research learning set. Changed rules require a separate forward challenger, including rejected names, failures/delistings, full corporate actions, path risk, costs and sector/simple-rule baselines. No frozen experiment is revised.

No model weights were retrained. A local standard-library evaluator validated six date-ordered observations and four mathematical assertions. It verifies arithmetic only.
