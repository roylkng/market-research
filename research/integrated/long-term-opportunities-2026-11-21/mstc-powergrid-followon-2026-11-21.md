## MSTC: digital auction economics worth investigation, headline operating cash unusable for valuation

Original issuer [12 Nov Q2/H1 FY27 signed filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MSTCLTD_12112026174610_SE782.pdf), consolidated balance sheet/cash flow and legal notes, and original [19 Nov filed transcript of 13 Nov management call](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MSTCLTD_19112026162210_SE786.pdf), including printed pp9 and 11. Decisive original pages visually inspected via screenshot; no local PDF bytes or SHA in this increment. Price verified at 20 Nov ₹656.55 from two dated secondary market feeds, [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/mstc-ltd-share-price-2291.html) and [ICICI Direct](https://www.icicidirect.com/stocks/mstc-share-price), **not** an original NSE EOD capture.

**The secular case:** Contractual e-auctions for government, mineral, metal-scrap and industrial counterparties can benefit from digitization, embedded integrations, high turnover relative to fee receipts and low incremental physical capital. H1 operating revenue ₹204.2635cr versus ₹162.4322cr, +25.75%, and H1 group PAT ₹133.1385cr versus ₹89.8417cr. These are period-specific. Management says higher scrap price, iron-ore volume and episodic land/mineral sales contributed; recurring price/fee yield is not independently decomposed. Legacy BG-backed procurement/Marketing discontinued in FY27, reducing trading exposure. Customer renewal, government-policy dependence, tender pricing and competitors are not settled by historically high margins.

**RBI event not yet operating cash:** The 12 Nov filing records 11 Nov **in-principle** TReDS authorization, conditional on audits and final authorization. Management on 13 Nov described MSTC as a potential *fifth* platform facing other operators, and said revenues can only be sized after operation. EPR platform still lacks regulatory rollout; a prospective coal exchange could **compete with/replace** the present auction business. The coal-related stream is management-estimated at roughly 20–25% of mineral-related business. No speculative TReDS/EPR/coal-exchange profits are credited.

**Critical reconciliation:** H1 CFO ₹962.30cr is driven chiefly by temporarily unpaid financial liabilities, **not** recurring owner cash. H1 zero-WC after actual cash tax and selected capex is ₹78.23cr. On the FY26 − H1FY26 + H1FY27 convention, TTM pre-WC operating cash ₹258.0157cr less cash tax ₹43.2655cr and selected capex ₹22.0942cr yields a conditional **₹192.656cr core owner-cash proxy**, before any normalized growth working capital, acquisitions or financing changes. TTM received bank interest ₹85.0923cr is an **additional separate diagnostic**, excluded from this proxy and not capitalized together with a claimed cash balance. No assumption that H1 after-tax CFO can persist.

**Cash ownership and governance:** Sept gross bank cash/equivalents ₹1,164.85cr + other bank balances ₹1,106.88cr against other current financial liabilities **₹1,563.30cr**, borrowings ₹145.00cr and trade payables ₹130.94cr. CEO/CFO told analysts 13 Nov that a major September creditor-settlement cash spike was temporary and already normalized. These accounts cannot justify crediting ₹2,271.7cr to minority equity value. ₹143.6197cr of current loans concerns disputed Standard Chartered Bank 2008–09 receivables and is also disclosed as trade receivables; a 2017 interim DRT judgment was set aside in 2023, while other cases remain pending. The auditor emphasizes this without qualification. Do not double-count opposing receivable/loan entries or assume the claim is finally paid. Government ownership and management credibility need incentive/capital-outcome review, not character speculation.

**Shareholder-price bridge:** 7.04cr shares, no newly indicated dilution in interim EPS, ₹656.55 = about ₹4,622.11cr equity value. TTM group PAT ₹261.73cr gives **17.66× reported profit**. The ₹192.656cr core selected cash proxy is ₹27.37/share, a substantially less cheap **23.99×**. A no-treasury, 50%-cash-payout, constant-share five-year mechanism with 15× terminal cash and company cash growth 0/5/10/15% gives **−6.4/−1.8/+2.9/+7.6%** illustrative annual IRRs. At 10% growth and 18× exit, +6.4%; 15% and 18×, +11.3%. These are **assumptions not forecasts or required returns**, omit future funding, taxes and buying costs, and assign no unlicensed platform option value. They show that 17.7× profit is not clearly an intrinsic value discount.

**Decision and reopen:** WATCH_PRICE_AND_CASH_OWNERSHIP rather than BUY. Reconcile buyer/auction escrow deposits, payout liabilities and restricted banks; demonstrate retained fees and receivable collections excluding commodity-price effects; confirm final RBI approval and actual TReDS revenue/customer adoption; evaluate incumbent auction business erosion if coal exchanges start. Better documented durable cash may change decision at the same price. Lower price after franchise erosion requires a fresh valuation.

This is **not** a claim that normalized distributable owner cash is negative forever. Most capital is growth investment in long-lived commissioned transmission that must be matched with future tariff receipts and debt finance. It **does** mean capital availability and project ROIC cannot be inferred from earnings or dividends alone. September borrowings ₹151,207cr, CWIP ₹55,419cr, work-in-hand ₹2.20 lakh crore **including CWIP**, 89% tariff-based competitive bidding (TBCB), not a blanket regulated asset-base return. Do not add CWIP to backlog again.

The 11 Nov original LoI quotes annual charges **₹243.34cr** for the Humnabad BOOT project and cites 11 TBCB FY27 successes with **₹8,106.97cr combined annual quoted charges**. These are not realized FY27 revenue or profit. Capex, concession execution, funding, completion and operating performance determine incremental returns.

19 Nov issuer corporate archive confirms an **executed** CTUIL SPA. Original 1 Nov issuer approval states initial **₹73.46cr** consideration from ₹76.85cr NAV minus ₹3.39cr specified liabilities, plus a final true-up. The 19 Nov signed PDF timed out, so actual closing/cash receipt/final value not verified. Some secondary extracts misread ₹3.46cr, contradicted by visually inspected original 1 Nov approval. Relative to the ~₹2.5 lakh crore company, this is not a material new capital-return catalyst.

At ₹269, reported rolling EPS ₹17.42 gives about **15.44×**. FY26 announced dividend ₹9/share is a historical reference, not secured future dividend; the FY27 ₹3 interim had a **6 Nov record date**, so 20 Nov buyers are not entitled to it. Five-year hypothetical **₹9 annual dividends, unchanged shares, 15× exit P/E** plus EPS growth at 3%, 5%, 8% gives roughly **5.6%, 7.5%, 10.3% annual IRRs**. For 8% growth and a 12×/18× exit, approximately 5.9%/14.1%. Funding and tariff assumptions are unproven. This is not a trade price or projected outcome. **Decision WATCH_PROJECT_RETURNS_AND_FUNDING**, pending original project tariff concession/commissioning and borrowing/return bridge, potentially promotable at same price if verified.
