## Case A: Crizac, INE0S4R01014. New source-proven operating and accounting deterioration

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations | **₹102.6653cr** | ₹162.2515cr | **−36.72%** | ₹201.2132cr |
| Pretax profit | **₹40.1328cr** | ₹64.8166cr | **−38.08%** | ₹64.8391cr |
| Group net profit after tax | **₹36.2327cr** | ₹48.3354cr | **−25.04%** | ₹46.1656cr |

**Derived H1 revenue declined 18.27%**, ₹303.8785cr from ₹371.7868cr, by reconciling the current Q1 ₹201.2132cr and Q2 ₹102.6653cr. **Source conflict, unresolved:** the same mirrored BSE result transcriptions print H1 ₹303.0785cr, which is ₹0.80cr lower than Q1+Q2. This arithmetic identity is provisional and the original signed 12.8 MB PDF must be inspected before H1 source admission. H1 consolidated PAT fell **12.48%**, ₹82.3983cr from ₹94.1479cr. The Q2 revenue decline was also **48.98% sequentially**, but inter-quarter student-intake seasonality could be meaningful; the **−36.72% same-quarter YoY** is the stronger comparison. No causal link to UK visa applications, admissions or partner cancellations has been established from the announcement.

### Why the PAT headline *understates* the deterioration

- The company-authored exchange **press release** states rounded Q2 PAT of **₹369 million**, versus the filed consolidated group's **₹362.327 million**. This is an unresolved attribution/scope difference. It is **not** additional profit and cannot be added to the group total.
- Consolidated Q2 PBT **fell 38.08%** but PAT fell just 25.04%. Mechanical quarterly effective tax rate was **9.72%** vs **25.43%** in year-earlier Q2.
- The original signed-note transcription records a **₹12.7010cr deferred-tax asset recognized for accumulated tax losses in Crizac Ltd UK during H1**, equivalent to **15.41% of H1 net profit**, supported by management projections of future taxable profit. This is an *accounting asset*, **not cash remitted to the Indian parent**. Exact Q2 versus Q1 attribution of this H1 tax benefit and any country-specific current tax are still not fully proven.
- Effective 1 Apr 2026, depreciation/amortization was changed **WDV → straight-line**, lowering recognized D&A by **₹1.4288cr Q2 / ₹2.6808cr H1** relative to the former method. Prospective estimate treatment and accounting effect are disclosed, not a cash uplift.
- Auditors' other-matters excerpt reports two foreign subsidiaries with aggregate **Q2 PAT loss ~₹5.0966cr**, **H1 loss ~₹9.5955cr**, in results they reviewed via other auditors. It would be **incorrect to divide subsidiary revenue by group sales and assign its loss share** without intercompany eliminations and appropriate consolidated reconciliation.
- Group **H1 after-tax operating cash flow ₹126.9221cr**, above H1 PAT ₹82.3983cr, is a valid *counterargument against an immediate cash-flow-collapse thesis*. But there is no verified current **parent-available FCFE** (acquisition spend, escrow, minority rights, growth reinvestment, recurring FX, taxes, capex and dividends all matter).
- Edument Consultancy became a subsidiary effective 30 Sept on acquired **5,337 CCPS (₹64.36 lakh) + 2,250 CCDs (₹59.38 lakh)**, making ownership and convertible/consolidation accounting real. The company also disclosed an **£742,378 cash agreement** for UK Inova Consultancy. No additional fictitious shareholder dilution or acquisition profit is assumed.

### Shareholder returns, valuation and risk

The issuer approved **₹4/share FY27 interim dividend**, record **6 Nov**, payment by **27 Nov 2026**. ₹4 × **17,49,82,500 shares** = **₹69.993cr cash dividend commitment**, **55.15% of H1 group operating cash** and about 85% of H1 group PAT. A genuine fresh declared payout, not an arbitrage at an already expired ex-date. Payout sustainability should be judged against the current lower revenue, group cash sources and parent liquidity, not historical high dividend yield.

The **30-Oct 15:29 NSE ₹138.01** quote times the dividend-reconciled 174,982,500-share denominator gives **₹2,414.93cr reference cap** (before any as-yet unverified subsequent legal dilution). FY26 PAT ₹219.1806cr + this H1 ₹82.3983cr − prior H1 ₹94.1479cr = **₹207.431cr mechanical trailing group PAT**, **11.64×**. Removing only the H1 ₹12.701cr noncash DTA as an *illustrative one-item sensitivity*, without attempting an exact tax-normalized model, raises the multiple to ~**12.40×**. Neither multiple proves cheapness, nor is the DTA entire quarter's tax benefit.

**Scenario downside lens (not a target):** At unchanged ₹2,414.93cr equity capitalization, sustainable annual *attributable* earnings of ₹150cr would mean ~**16.10×**, while ₹125cr means ~**19.32×**. Those are illustrative denominators, **not predictions**, and the published group PAT is not necessarily equal to shareholder-attributable profit.

**Decision:** Material new **negative thesis test**, independent of OPEN002. **INVESTIGATED / RESEARCH_ONLY / NO BUY**. Next authenticated data: original large PDF byte hash and all cash tables, group-vs-parent cash rights, actual UK intake volumes and renewals, Edument/UK amortisation/earnouts, tax asset realizability, FY27 H2 margin trend, management call **2 Nov**. There is no claim an FY27 dividend will repeat.

## Case B: Oswal Pumps, INE0BYP01024. Genuine new order, but the '6× P/E' trap

**Signed 30-Oct issuer notice:** MSEDCL communicated an **additional 10,000 off-grid DC solar water pumps** to be designed/manufactured/supplied/installed/tested/commissioned under **PM-KUSUM B Magel Tyala**. Stated consideration **₹235.92cr *including GST***, execution within **60 days from the operative order/notice**, **5-year warranty, repair, maintenance and remote-monitoring system**. The ₹235.92cr is **gross future billing at most, not consolidated revenue already recognized, not a new standalone recurring FY27 EBIT, not ₹235.92cr owner cash**. The state-issued counterpart work order and milestone/guarantee deductions need independent verification.

At verified **29-Oct 2026 NSE ₹209.95** and approx **₹2,392cr** secondary market capitalization, this gross order is **9.86% of equity value**, **before removing GST and paying any warranty and execution costs**. ### Real balance-sheet trap: FY26 standalone trade receivables

Separately, original audited FY26 **standalone** trade receivables (net of allowance) were **₹1,101.929cr**, up from **₹615.094cr** a year prior (**+79.15%**). Their carrying value alone approaches 46% of Oct29 quoted **group-equity** capitalization. These are **standalone** debtors, and may overlap group customers, so do not mix them numerically with consolidated CFO or double count.

The issuer's receivable note says **₹98.362cr gross** has experienced significant increased credit risk, with **₹23.873cr allowance for expected credit loss** deducted across debtors. It describes ordinary trade credit typically **0–120 days** and states trade receivables are **hypothecated to secure borrowings**, a material lender claim over future collections. Receivables older than a year carry more risk than an advertised receivable-day ratio suggests.

If **10%** of standalone March net receivables were hypothetically impaired, gross pre-tax book/earnings hit would be **₹110.193cr**, **4.61% of ~₹2,392cr** equity value **before taxes or lender effects**. This is **not an expected-credit-loss forecast** or proof debtors are bad. A larger haircut or funding stress also affects working capital and ability to execute new awards.

**Important counterevidence:** FY26 group CFO improved from a more negative ₹142cr in FY25. Management reports Q4FY26 positive CFO of ~₹170.6cr and April26 collections ~₹116.4cr from nodal agencies. This supports some ability to collect, but **April cash cannot be moved retrospectively into FY26 CFO**. It must be verified against bank receipts and offset by fresh FY27 receivables.

**Earmarked money:** Annual report lists **₹271.34cr unutilized IPO proceeds** earmarked for manufacturing/solar expansion and general-corporate purposes, predominantly in deposits. Treat neither as excess shareholder cash nor deduct it mechanically against equity market value. FY26 group net debt reference **₹134.6cr** is a separate measure. Planned solar module expansion to 1,570 MW and long warranty obligations require *incremental investment*.

**Decision:** **NEW OUTSIDE-SHORTLIST DISCOVERY → INVESTIGATED → NOT INVESTMENT-READY.** Follow Q2FY27 results (market calendars indicative ~6 Nov, check issuer board notice), 30 Sep **consolidated and parent** aged debtors, FY27 actual state collection evidence and bill discounting, capex outlays versus budget, warranty provisions, contingent performance guarantees, MSEDCL work order, earnings attributable to minorities and dilution. A low nominal P/E is not a low-risk investor cash yield.
