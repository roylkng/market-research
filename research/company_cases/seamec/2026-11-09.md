# SEAMEC: wait at the 9 November 2026 reference price

## Decision

**No fresh purchase at the ₹1,526.60 official NSE close on 9 November 2026.** Retain the company for price-sensitive research. This is an assisted underwriting judgment, not a sell instruction for an existing holding or a claim about the next price move.

The company has real fleet growth and contracted work. The apparent 11.6x trailing earnings multiple nevertheless overstates how cheap a repeatable earnings stream is. The latest profit includes an asset-sale gain, working-capital release supports cash flow, and the reported net-cash snapshot precedes a major acquisition. The conservative case does not meet the working five-year 15% annual price-return hurdle.

EPS itself is not added across periods, and the half-year is not annualized. These are cross-document reported amounts, not independently restatement-reconciled owner earnings.

## The earnings bridge changes the decision

Q2 FY27 pretax profit is **₹48.58 crore = ₹6.15 crore before exceptional items + ₹42.43 crore exceptional gain**. The gain is 87.3% of pretax profit. The current results note attributes it to sale of Seamec Gallant for ₹88.31 crore net of commission.

The remaining ₹6.15 crore still includes ₹23.72 crore other income. Excluding both items gives a **−₹17.57 crore pretax diagnostic**, not a normalized after-tax profit. The disclosed ₹12.50 crore Samudra Prabha repair cost is not automatically added back. Maintenance is part of this asset-intensive business, and one seasonal quarter should not be annualized.

| Trailing-earnings sensitivity | Reference EPS | Reference P/E |
|---|---:|---:|
| Remove only the vessel-sale gain | ₹114.93–119.10 | 12.82–13.28x |
| Remove the gain and all other income | ₹90.80–101.00 | 15.11–16.81x |

The ranges assume 0% or 25% tax benefit on the removed pretax amount. These are illustrative assumptions, not the actual tax on the disposal. The annual policy applies tonnage-based taxation to shipping income. Other income includes recurring interest and variable investment/other items, so removing all of it is a deliberately stricter sensitivity, not a verified normalization correction.

Definitions also matter. The May presentation's approximately ₹1,000 crore revenue figure includes other income. FY26 operating revenue is ₹952.46 crore. Use operating EBITDA excluding other income consistently: FY26 ₹399.67 crore, H1 FY26 ₹104.72 crore and H1 FY27 ₹162.18 crore. H1 operating revenue grew 60.6% and EBITDA 54.9%, while the margin moved from 34.0% to 32.8%.

## Cash and capital are not free extras

H1 operating cash is ₹303.70 crore. The disclosed inventory, receivable/prepayment, payable and provision movements contribute ₹139.68 crore. Before those movements, the cash diagnostic is ₹164.02 crore. Specified cash PPE purchases are ₹154.27 crore, interest in financing cash ₹8.14 crore and lease payments ₹0.99 crore. The residual is ₹140.30 crore as reported, but ₹0.62 crore before the working-capital contribution. Neither is normalized maintenance free cash flow because purchases include growth spending.

There is an unresolved source reconciliation: the results note reports ₹42.43 crore of exceptional sale profit while the cash-flow schedule deducts ₹27.31 crore as gain on fixed assets. The ₹15.12 crore difference is not explained here. No source figure is silently corrected and no wrongdoing is inferred.

The September presentation's ₹227.40 crore net-cash figure predates Anant's takeover on 14 October and on-hire status on 21 October. Management's disclosed acquisition programme was about USD 70 million. The 21 October related-party charter to HAL is about USD 55 million over 1,225 days, excluding GST and subject to HAL's ONGC charter. Gross contract revenue is not lifetime investment return. Future charters, residual value, operating costs, dry-docking and debt service matter.

CRISIL's 13 October assessment reaffirmed A+/Stable and A1, while explicitly expecting fleet expansion to move net cash to net debt. Its forecasts are not realized balances. The annual report also identifies deposits pledged against overdraft facilities. This case adds no separate surplus-cash bonus to an earnings valuation that already includes income from financial assets.

## Valuation scenarios, not probability-weighted forecasts

All cases use five years. Dividends, taxes, transaction costs, slippage and new dilution are excluded.

| Case | Starting recurring EPS assumption | EPS growth/year | Exit P/E | Year-five price | Price CAGR at ₹1,526.60 | Entry meeting 15% hurdle |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Stress | ₹80 | 0% | 10x | ₹800 | −12.1% | ₹398 |
| Conservative base | ₹100 | 12% | 12x | ₹2,115 | 6.7% | ₹1,051 |
| Optimistic | ₹120 | 18% | 15x | ₹4,118 | 22.0% | ₹2,047 |
| Generous cross-check | ₹115 | 15% | 12x | ₹2,776 | 12.7% | ₹1,380 |

The conservative EPS assumption is informed by the stricter earnings sensitivity. Growth and exit multiples are underwriting assumptions, not empirically calibrated predictions. The stress case implies 47.6% terminal price downside, not a maximum possible loss. At the current price, the same 12% growth / 12x exit assumptions require roughly ₹145 of sustainable starting EPS to meet the hurdle.

**Reconsider near ₹1,050 under the conservative case, or when recurring earnings and financing evidence justify a stronger case.** Even the more generous ₹115 starting EPS / 15% growth case requires about ₹1,380. These are conditional research thresholds, not certified intrinsic values or automatic buy levels.

## The award and the updated operating evidence

The 7 November PRP-X award specifies USD 26.14 million for SEAMEC's own scope, excluding GST, even though the legal recipient is a consortium. The 8 November filing confirms Princess departed Mumbai at 07:06 to commence the project. This updates mobilization, not acceptance of completed work, recognized profit or cash collected.

At hypothetical ₹85–100/USD, 10% after-tax cash margin, 12-month receipt, 15% discounting, full baseline incrementality and no additional funding, the finite cash value is ₹19.32–22.73 crore. That is about ₹7.60–8.94 per reference share, or 0.50–0.59% of reference equity value. The award is operationally useful, but by itself does not justify a major equity rerating. No P/E multiplier is applied to a one-off contract, and a contract already in the base case is not added twice.

## Evidence that could change this decision

The key evidence is post-Anant debt and available cash, recurring earnings after finance and maintenance costs, accepted billable deployment and collections, the sale-gain/cash-flow reconciliation, and a qualifying price with dilution/liquidity rechecked. Growth can make the optimistic scenario plausible, but an asset-sale gain cannot establish it.
