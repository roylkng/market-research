# H013 two-window historical challenge result

Recorded: 2026-09-08

Status: **HISTORICAL CHALLENGE PASS. PROSPECTIVE EVIDENCE NOT YET ESTABLISHED. LIVE CAPITAL DISABLED.**

H013 was frozen before its risk-adjusted score was calculated. It was tested without learned parameters on two disjoint, source-audited full-market windows using retained official NSE EQ prices, Nifty 500 index snapshots and corporate-action records.

## Frozen signal

For each active monthly cohort:

`risk_adjusted_momentum = (close[t-5] / close[t-125] - 1) / (sample_std(120 daily log returns) * sqrt(120))`

The market regime had to satisfy both Nifty 500 close > 120-session SMA and positive Nifty 500 prior-60-session return. Top 10% of sufficiently liquid eligible EQ names were selected, entered next session open and exited on the 60th holding-session close.

## Older challenge: 2024-12 through 2025-06 decisions

Only April, May and June 2025 were active under the frozen regime rule.

Across **310 selected observations**:

- mean Nifty 500 excess: **+6.03 pp**
- median Nifty 500 excess: **+1.44 pp**
- selected-stock beat rate: **54.84%**
- active-cohort positive rate: **100%**
- raw 120/5 momentum comparator mean excess: +5.46 pp
- prior-60 comparator mean excess: +3.60 pp
- prior-20 comparator mean excess: +2.82 pp
- full eligible cohort mean excess: +3.24 pp
- matched-random one-sided empirical p-value: **0.0010**
- maximum single-symbol share of aggregate positive gross selected return: **5.64%**

Every frozen per-window gate passed.

## Later challenge: 2025-10 through 2026-05 decisions

October, November and December 2025 were active. January through May 2026 were regime-off.

Across **303 selected observations**:

- median Nifty 500 excess: **+4.13 pp**
- selected-stock beat rate: **62.71%**
- active-cohort positive rate: **100%**

- prior-60 comparator mean excess: +7.36 pp
- prior-20 comparator mean excess: -2.28 pp
- full eligible cohort mean excess: -3.49 pp
- matched-random one-sided empirical p-value: **0.00010**

Every frozen per-window gate passed.

## Combined frozen gates

Across **613 selected observations**:

- mean Nifty 500 excess: **+8.25 pp**
- median Nifty 500 excess: **+3.04 pp**
- selected-stock Nifty 500 beat rate: **58.73%**
- neither challenge window had negative mean excess.

All combined H013 gates passed. The stronger `PROMISING` label required >=60% combined beat rate, so H013 is classified **HISTORICAL PASS**, not `PROMISING` under the exact preregistration.

## Interpretation

This is the first hypothesis in the current sequence to convert the positive-tail behavior of raw momentum into positive median selected-stock excess in both historical windows while beating its raw-momentum comparators and matched random selections.

The evidence supports continuing with H013. It does **not** establish a live trading strategy because:

- both windows are historical,
- transaction costs, taxes and slippage are not yet included,
- historical source reconstruction is not prospective evidence,
- the project still requires a prospective paper phase before promotion,
- a further earlier-period independent robustness replay is frozen separately and remains desirable.

No H013 parameter may be changed based on these results.
