# Independent review of Datamatics valuation | 18 November 2026

Reviewed `work/datamatics/decision_inputs.json`, `calculate.py`, the underwriting note and original rendered H1 consolidated profit/cash-flow pages. This is a focused independent check of material accounting and valuation choices, not a second full company audit.

## Material correction made before publication

The preliminary tax bridge used total P&L tax, inadvertently benefiting from a deferred-tax credit. Original statements show trailing current-tax expense of **₹88.37 crore**, cash tax paid **₹63.52 crore**, and therefore a **₹24.85 crore** normalization reserve. The preliminary ₹12.78 crore reserve was too small by the **₹12.07 crore deferred-tax credit**. The author corrected the inputs and report. The resulting operating-owner cash proxy is **₹254.99 crore**, before the optional treasury-income estimate. Including the conservative ₹16.17 crore treasury estimate gives **₹271.16 crore**, supporting the rounded ₹270 crore working assumption.

## Checks passed

- TTM cash-flow arithmetic correctly adds FY26 and H1FY27, then subtracts H1FY26. Original FY26 CFO is ₹323.99 crore. An OCR rendering of ₹323.49 is wrong.
- Reported capex, lease principal and cash interest are deducted. Minority cash remains an explicit proxy, not a falsely audited owner amount.
- Treasury interest/dividends are outside CFO in investing cash flow. Including their income while adding **no separate net-cash value** avoids the major cash double-counting risk. Debt is not subtracted twice from an equity-cash valuation.
- TNQ's acquisition cash is treated separately from operating cash. No additional 20% earnings step-up or free future acquisition growth is manufactured.
- Independently reproduced five-year scenario values at a 15% discount rate: **₹309.82, ₹590.37 and ₹901.68** for the proposed conservative, working and upside assumptions.
- The user's required return is not assumed to be universally 15%. The report shows 10%, 12% and 15% sensitivities.

## Remaining valuation sensitivity, not another reason to postpone a decision

The disclosed TNQ term loan amortizes. If existing financial assets fund principal, the treasury-income pool can shrink. Interest savings may offset this, but the report does not model the matched maturity/yield schedule. Showing an operating-only **₹255 crore** starting-cash sensitivity is a cleaner robustness check than assuming the same treasury income persists without explanation. At the same 8% growth and 12× terminal multiple, the 15%-discounted value is about **₹558**, versus **₹590** for ₹270 crore cash. The approximately ₹600 review line should remain conditional and rounded.

The working valuation derives about **68% of present value from its terminal component** at a 15% discount rate. Its 8% future owner-cash growth is a scenario assumption that must be funded after operating costs, capex and working capital. Annual retained cash cannot simultaneously finance that growth and be valued again as free surplus. The report explicitly recognizes this condition.

**Review conclusion:** current-price WATCH_PRICE is supported by the stated assumptions. The valuation is a decision aid with visible sensitivities, not certified intrinsic value, a calibrated expected return or an automatic buy order. The corrected tax bridge materially improves its integrity. No reviewer edits were made to the author's files.
