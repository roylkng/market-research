## First-principles correction to RR001

RR001 is useful for breadth, but its components measure different things:

- short score rewards current price recognition and trading activity;
- mid score combines price recognition with current consensus growth and value;
- long score combines current consensus growth and annual EPS yield.

Therefore a raw high score can be a trap:

- low P/E may reflect peak-cycle commodity earnings rather than durable value;
- strong short momentum may coexist with weak fundamental growth;
- high forecast growth may be offset by leverage or acquisition risk;
- current consensus levels are not the same as positive earnings revisions.

DR001 uses the radar only to allocate analyst attention. It does not alter or
reinterpret RR001 scores.
