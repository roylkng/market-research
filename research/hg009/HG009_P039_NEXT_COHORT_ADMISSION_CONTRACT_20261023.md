## Decision
Do not promote the original HG009 five-session performance to investable alpha. Original 300 identities, results, missing issuer prices, and source evidence remain unchanged. A new prospective experiment must be source complete, balanced before outcomes, and economical after costs. No security recommendations, portfolio entries, or capital approval are implied by this document.

## Non-negotiable source-admission gates
1. Construct a **new** frozen candidate and comparator population with exact exchange, symbol, ISIN, segment and original as-of membership. Duplicate ISINs, renames, inactive equities and missing financial regime must be explained, not silently dropped.
2. Before the new entry-time cutoff, retain original classification and size/float evidence for **at least 95% overall and 90% in every 100-issuer arm**. For a 300-issuer, three-by-100 design this means >=285/300 and >=90/100 each. These thresholds are predeclared and cannot be lowered after outcomes.
3. Official exchange/index one-level industry is NOT equivalent to verified four-level issuer classification. Keep source depth and accounting regime as distinct attributes. UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN. The 142 post-October-15 classifications from the original 755-row index subset are useful *source-discovery leads*, never October 15 pre-entry features.
4. Each admitted feature retains: exact original URL, source type, exchange/issuer publication time when available, **actual first-seen UTC**, raw byte SHA-256, issuer identity, accounting period, original quoted field/value and parsing rule. An exchange publication time alone does not establish first observation by the strategy.
5. Pre-entry eligibility uses actual first-seen UTC <= the cohort freeze cutoff. Unknown or later timestamps fail closed. Separate first observed from publication time; both must be represented.

## Balance diagnostics to freeze before looking at next-cohort returns
Match on verified industry/accounting regime, free-float-cap log scale, median prior cash-traded value, realized volatility/beta and existing 252-session momentum. Each covariate requires its original pre-entry observed cutoff and explicit missing denominator. Define calipers, weights and matching algorithm in a subsequent immutable trial registration **before opening any next-cohort price outcomes**. Report per-arm counts, overlap, effective sample size, unmatched issuer identities, raw and weighted standardized mean differences, source completeness and sector concentration. A future protocol must specify a numeric balance tolerance a priori. Do not choose that tolerance by inspecting outcome differences.

## Return and execution evidence
- Distinguish original 1/5-session diagnostic from genuinely independent future 60/120/252-session trading-day follow-up. Same-day or nested horizons are correlated, not independent trials.
- Preserve original corporate action entitlements including dividends, splits, rights, demergers, mergers, delistings, and financial issuer special cases. A close-to-close price ratio is not a total shareholder return.
- Preserve distinct continuous trading price, closing auction final price, derivative expiry VWAP and actual executable quote. Version exchange-clock rules by effective date; the December 7, 2026 closing auction transition is **forward-only** (issue #521). Special November 8 Muhurat session is explicit, not weekend-filtered.
- Report executable participation/capacity, spread, depth, brokerage, taxes, market impact and failed fills; no zero-cost alpha promotion.
- Estimate uncertainty by **independent entry date**, controlling clustered/serially correlated exposures; apply multiple-testing correction. A cross-section of 100 equities entered simultaneously is not 100 independent market experiments.
- Benchmark against cash, broad index total return, sector/size matched comparators, and plain value/quality/momentum baselines. A 100%-cash book beating a falling price index is not stock-selection skill.
