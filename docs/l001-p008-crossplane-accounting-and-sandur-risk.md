The **FY26 issuer annual report** and Karnataka High Court case W.P. 20531/2025 (judgment 30 April 2026) state that two material forest clearances expire **5 and 10 December 2026**. The underlying mining leases have a separate term through 31 December 2033. The High Court upheld requirements for compensatory afforestation. The April 30 court references two 2025 demand notices totaling approximately **₹131.25cr**, while the FY26 annual report references a later **₹139.05cr** total demanded for compensatory afforestation. These should **not** be simply added or quietly equated: reconcile timing and scope with original later correspondence. The report says legal/afforestation steps were ongoing. The latest Q1 original filing reflects mining as a substantial operating earnings contributor, so treating the December 2026 forest-clearance timeline as immaterial would not be prudent.
