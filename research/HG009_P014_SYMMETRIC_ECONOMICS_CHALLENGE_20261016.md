**Research date:** October 16, 2026. **Selection:** original ten P008 cases, fixed before October 15. **Market anchor:** official NSE October 15, 2026 EQ prices only. **Live capital:** disabled.

## Why revise the method

The first genuine HG009 H1 market source did **not** support a claim that selected companies already outperform controls. The observed-only mean October 15 price change was -1.4103% for the selected 99 priceable securities, -1.2690% for the one-signal 99, and -1.0763% for the zero-signal 100, while the Nifty500 price index gained +0.5738%. This is a **one-day unadjusted price diagnostic**, not a multi-year investment outcome and not a randomly controlled model test.

The screen still performs one valuable service: it finds different ways a company's fundamentals *might* inflect. But fundamental screening is not proof of mispricing. The operating/business research must now work backwards from **future parent-equity cash** and current price, not simply celebrate an EPS catalyst.

Because this review occurs **after** October 15 prices were opened, it is explicitly a **post-entry accounting review**. It cannot retroactively promote itself into a preregistered trading signal or rewrite the original 100+100+100 universe.

**These are not equivalent alternatives for owner FCF.** Buying financial securities or placing cash in other long-term financial assets reduces bank cash, but may create assets shareholders still own. Conversely, a capitalized project may use up cash today yet have a future income stream. All investing activity needs *balance-sheet and actual asset realizability reconciliation*. Subtracting every investment as a permanent expense would be as misleading as calling CFO minus PPE distributable profit.

## Correcting PIXTRANS accounting asymmetry

The initial review took current-quarter standalone PBT ₹60.8168cr, removed current quarter ₹25.2949cr FVTPL gain, then compared the resulting ₹35.5219cr to the **unadjusted** previous-year PBT ₹30.9666cr. That produced +14.71%, a comparison using inconsistent adjustment rules.

P011 retrieved original NSE Q1 FY26 and FY27 standalone quarterly disclosures. Removing *all other income* from both quarters produces current PBT after other-income removal **₹33.5056cr** versus previous **₹16.7799cr**, or **+99.68%**. This is a more symmetric comparison, not proof of +99.68% recurring operating profit. It can still mix financing expenses, accounting presentation changes, taxes, working capital and group subsidiaries. It does demonstrate why the earlier +14.71% figure cannot be treated as a decisive bearish adjustment.

## Higher-leverage model requirements

**Discovery** → **source custody and temporal validity** → **symmetric accounting quality** → **normalize owner cash and capital deployment** → **parent diluted equity bridge and source-backed bear/base/bull** → **cost-and-impact challenged opportunity** → **independent future-cohort performance**.

Failure of any gate means an explicit missingness or conditional uncertainty. Never translate "clean accounting" or "three independent signals" directly into a 50% expected return. The sample already differs from controls on baseline earnings and likely size/industry. Future alpha needs genuine risk-neutral out-of-sample comparisons and credible survivorship handling.
