## Operating hypothesis

Assume continuing finished-dosage and complex-generics throughput supports modest revenue growth, but persistent input/logistics pressure and peptide losses limit the profit increase. The base allows financing benefit from the stronger cash position while preserving meaningful financing costs. It is deliberately below the seasonal additive and YoY-ratio forecasts because those do not encode current margin pressure.

EBIT is after depreciation and excludes other income. Owner PAT = (revenue × EBIT margin + other income − finance costs) × (1 − tax rate) − NCI. Associates and new exceptional items are zero assumptions. Scenarios are uncalibrated sensitivities with no assigned probabilities.

- **Revenue:** 1625 cr, 4.2% above Q2, lower than the prior-year Q2-to-Q3 revenue increase of 7.0%. No unapproved first-to-file launch windfall is included.
- **EBIT margin:** 15.75% versus Q2 approximately 16.26% and Q1 approximately 17.56%. It embeds continuing peptide losses and input-cost pass-through lag. No separate addback of peptide losses.
- **Other Income:** 10 cr near Q2. Warrant proceeds support liquidity, not operating revenue.
- **Finance Cost:** 18 cr versus Q2 20.974 cr, allowing modest debt-cost benefit. Net cash does not eliminate gross borrowings or leases.
- **Tax Rate:** 25.5%, close to Q2 approximately 25.30%.
- **NCI:** Zero, confirmed in official NSE owner/NCI rows for current and prior periods.

No exceptional item in the four selected quarters. The prior 9M exceptional expense 25.911 cr belongs to an earlier quarter and is not a December addback. The issuer describes December 2025 labour-code impact as immaterial without a separate amount. Ongoing remediation and peptide losses are retained as operating economics.

## Decisive unknowns

- Gagillapur October 2026 inspection EIR remained awaited in the latest checked earnings deck. Management confidence is not regulatory clearance.
- Timing and magnitude of customer price pass-through, freight/input prices and currency effects remain uncertain.
- Peptide/CDMO EBITDA loss was 15.2 cr in Q2 versus 12.4 cr in Q1. New pipeline value and breakeven timing are not credited to December.
- Warrant proceeds and higher absolute PAT do not imply matching EPS growth. The target is absolute owner PAT, not EPS.
- Prior signed PDF was captured from a mirror. Exact prior owner PAT and zero NCI were separately corroborated in the original exchange integrated filing.
