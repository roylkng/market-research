- **Employee and overhead expense:** 15.2cr employees and 6cr other expense. Allow current higher payroll to persist, with seasonal overhead above current Q2 5.12cr.
- **Fair-value gain:** 0. No directional forecast. The downside assumes a 3cr loss and favorable case a 3cr gain.

- **Tax:** 0.25. Applied to own PBT only. No tax benefit is recognized in the adverse loss scenario.

## What would invalidate the base

- Fee income remaining near 36cr despite seasonal expectations would compress the base profit sharply.
- A change in the subsidiary earnings mix can move owner PAT even with unchanged group PAT.
- Fair-value movements can overwhelm the modest operating forecast.
