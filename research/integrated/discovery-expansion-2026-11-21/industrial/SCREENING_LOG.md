# Industrial discovery screening log

**Run:** 21 November 2026. **Scope:** four selected fresh industrial issuers. **Price basis:** exact EQ / ISIN row in the original 20 November NSE bhavcopy. These are reference closes, not assumed fills. This is neither a census of Indian equities nor a completed valuation of each issuer.

The lane tests a specific mechanism: recurring demand for small but operationally necessary manufactured products. The screen then asks who captures the economics, what growth needs to be funded, and whether the stock price already requires the best outcome.
