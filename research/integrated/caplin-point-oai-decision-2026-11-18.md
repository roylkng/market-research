## Decisive new development

On **17 November 2026** Caplin Point Laboratories reported in its **original signed NSE announcement** that subsidiary Caplin Steriles' injectable and ophthalmic manufacturing facility at Gummidipoondi, inspected **13–21 August 2026**, received **10 observations**. FDA inspection classification **Official Action Indicated (OAI)** was communicated **16 November 2026** [C1]. The issuer calls observations procedural, states the facility is still manufacturing and supplying, and does not expect a *current* material operating/financial impact. The last statement is an **issuer assessment**, not independently proven regulatory clearance. The PDF's first page and annexure were visually inspected on 18 November; original file bytes and SHA-256 **not** captured.

The **FDA's primary explanation** [F1] calls OAI an *unacceptable* CGMP state. It says pending approvals involving that facility and some certificates **may** be withheld; comprehensive corrective and preventive action generally needs verification, often including onsite follow-up. **No verified import alert, shipment halt, generic recall, FDA penalty, or quantified financial loss** was located. Do not infer those outcomes merely from OAI. Conversely, no-impact issuer language does not cancel future launch and customer-approval exposure. Facility-specific FDA dashboard entry, inspection report, product and ANDA list and remediation timing are outstanding.

**Post-disclosure price response:** secondary NSE-linked quote ₹2,085.30 on 18 Nov vs ₹2,247.10 on 17 Nov, -7.20% one day [P1]; versus 11 Nov secondary historical ₹2,520.30, -17.26% over the interval [P2]. Not all losses across the interval should be attributed causally to OAI. The correct decision question is what future owner cash was impaired and what portion the price already anticipates.

Keeping the mechanical current diluted EPS **₹90.41**, fixed share count and ignoring dividends, transaction costs and tax, a **flat** ₹2,085.30 share price in year 3 would require:
- With terminal P/E **18x**, year-3 EPS **₹115.85**, **8.62% annual EPS growth**;
- With terminal P/E **15x**, year-3 EPS **₹139.02**, **15.42% annual EPS growth**.

Multiples and horizons are hypothetical **SCENARIO_ASSUMPTIONS**, not endorsed fair multiples or forecasts. The listed business can also experience multiple expansion, contraction, regulatory delays, funding and dilution. No scenario provides supported 3-year owner free cash because maintenance/growth split, FDA financial bridge and capital claims are missing. Do not convert this conditional hurdle into a BUY_AT_PRICE threshold.
