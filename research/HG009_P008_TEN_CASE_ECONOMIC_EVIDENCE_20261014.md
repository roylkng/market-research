**Lease treatment matters.** For TRAVELFOOD, ₹123.70cr of lease principal appears in financing cash, along with ₹30.06cr of financing interest. Restricted assets, subsidiary cash, acquisition exposure and share claims require separate documents.

## Valuation engine and scientific limits

This calculator cannot supply real-company price targets with the current incomplete evidence.
