### Why the headline premium collapses when acceptance is measured

If a hypothetical investor already held shares, all were tendered, and **3.15% of that holding** was accepted at ₹1,000 with the unaccepted shares left at ₹810.10 **for illustration only**, the mechanical gross accepted-share excess over the old market value is (0.0315 × (1000/810.10 - 1) = 0.7384\%\) of holding value. The original special-holder tender allocation can differ from this idealized flat rate, and the market price of remaining shares may drop after payout. Do not call this an all-in return; taxes/fees, tender eligibility, tender acceptance by category, shareholder approval and next executable share price are unknown.
