# H008 design-check result

Recorded: 2026-09-08

Status: **PRIMARY MODEL REJECTED. APRIL-MAY 60-SESSION VALIDATION UNOPENED. LIVE CAPITAL DISABLED.**

H008 fit its preregistered Huber model on 959 October-December 2025 result events and evaluated the fixed model on 1,093 January-February 2026 events. No April-May 60-session target was computed because the H008 model failed its continuation gate.

Primary top-10% H008 model result on the January-February design check:

- selected: 110
- median Nifty 500 60-session excess: **-1.34 pp**
- mean excess: **-0.39 pp**
- Nifty 500 beat rate: **44.55%**
- median raw 60-session return: **-3.02%**

H008 is therefore rejected.

## Mechanism discovered in a preregistered baseline

The H008 specification froze several matched-count baselines before the 60-session target was opened. One of them, the unchanged H005 accounting-growth composite, was materially stronger in the January-February design check:

- selected: 110
- median Nifty 500 excess: **+9.67 pp**
- mean excess: **+13.45 pp**
- Nifty 500 beat rate: **73.64%**
- median raw stock return: **+7.24%**
- excess >= +5 pp: **59.09%**

Other design-check baselines were also directionally positive but weaker:

- prior-20-session momentum median excess: +4.42 pp;
- first-session tape median excess: +3.75 pp;
- prior-60-session relative momentum median excess: +2.30 pp.

This is design evidence, not validation. The accounting baseline was observed because it had been preregistered as a comparison, so promoting that mechanism requires a new hypothesis. The April-May 2026 60-session target remains unopened and can serve as the one-shot forward historical test for that new frozen signal.
