## Five-year valuation, assumptions disclosed, no probabilities or targets

**Reproducible illustrative cash ledger**, NOT a validated expected-return model: begin with TTM selected residual F0 = ₹630.759cr, let F_t = F0 × (1+g)^t for t=1..5, distribute 50% of F_t annually and accumulate the remaining 50% without crediting reinvestment returns. At year five add **12× or 14× F5** terminal cash plus **₹400cr** of unencumbered opening treasury *assumed* after liquidity/funding reserves. Divide all benefits by a fixed 9.355061cr illustrative fully diluted shares. IRR solves `price = Σ[0.5 F_t/shares /(1+IRR)^t] + [multiple×F5 + 400 + Σ0.5 F_t]/shares/(1+IRR)^5`. This **does not** charge future trust pre-funding, extra director option compensation, RegEdge cash spending, corporate taxes to investors, trading costs or new share grants. It is **optimistic**, especially for a fixed-growth assumption not justified by customer-level collected cash.

Conditional entry sensitivities with **unchanged** company economics: at 6% FCF growth and 12× exit, price at 8% / 10% / 12% annual personal return preferences is approximately **₹1,063 / ₹975 / ₹896**; under 6% and 14×, **₹1,185 / ₹1,087 / ₹999**. Those figures are *discounted scenario reference prices, not price targets, guarantees or automatic purchases*. They are invalid if business economics deteriorate or the new compensation regime changes shareholder claims. Higher-quality collected growth and favorable owner-alignment evidence can also reopen the case at a higher price without a rigid fixed threshold.

**Alternative hypotheses / moat and sector:** Large financial-service clients may value eClerx's domain delivery, audit trails, skilled teams and integration ability, but buyers may internalize AI workflows and reprice labor-intensive contracts. The disclosed ~60% top-ten customer concentration, 23.4% staff attrition and decline in deal wins constrain the durability claim. Issuer awards/biographies or claimed GenAI adoption are not independent reputation evidence. The prior buyback and technology capex demonstrate capital deployment, not its after-tax return. A board remuneration cap that can rise when options are exercised deserves independent minority-owner scrutiny. **Do not infer misconduct from a requested vote.**

**Disposition / falsifiers:** `PASS_CURRENT_PRICE` at the 19 Nov original exchange close, `WATCH_PRICE_AND_INCENTIVE_EVIDENCE` as new research holding queue. Reopen specifically for (a) independently evidenced 10%+ *organic*, after-investment, after-employee-claim owner cash growth with neutral FX and retained client budgets, (b) independent documentation of award economics, final director remuneration vote and no material incremental dilution/cash leakage, (c) materially different offered price accompanied by a refreshed current NSE/BSE official close, or (d) an independently sourced collection/capex/contract economics shift.
