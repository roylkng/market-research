## Stronger economic realization test than a static gross-cash screen

- Unlike **Just Dial** (OPEN007 and OPEN008), Maharashtra Seamless actually transferred cash to common shareholders: **₹134.00 crore dividend paid in FY2026, same in FY2025**, or approximately **₹10/share** based on exactly 133,999,252 FY26 ordinary shares. At the completed **26-Oct-2026 close ₹533.40**, trailing paid dividend yield **1.87%**. Historical dividends are not forward guaranteed.
- It has a real original **7-Oct-2026 NSE board-approved proposed demerger** (appointed date 1 October) of two manufacturing/power/rig undertakings into separate, *to-be-listed* wholly owned subsidiaries. Company original filing https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MAHSEAMLES_07102026172556_MSLIntimationDemerger.pdf (8 pages, Annexure pp3 and 5 visually checked).
- Undertaking 1, Mangaon seamless pipes 125,000 MTPA plus Beed solar 10 MW, FY26 **₹793cr turnover, 16.98%**; resulting **MSL Seamless Tubes Ltd (MSTL)**.
- Undertaking 2, Telangana Narketpally 200,000 MTPA plus Rajasthan 25MW solar and Jindal Explorer rig, FY26 **₹693cr, 14.84%**; resulting **United Seamless Ltd (USL)**.
- Remainder FY26 operational turnover **₹3185cr, 68.18%**, total demerger disclosure **₹4671cr** vs separate audited group revenue ₹4674.34cr. The denominators and undertaking-specific margins are **NOT identical audited segment financial statements**.
- If legally completed, every **five original MSL shares** will remain and entitle holder to **one MSTL and one USL** fully paid ₹5 share, subject to fractions and eventual exchange listing. Original filing expressly says **value-neutral** exchange. It does **not** create a 40% bonus payout. NCLT, shareholder/creditor/regulator approvals and record date still unresolved. Asset, borrowing, investment portfolio and contingent liability allocation to resulting entities is **not** yet separately published. Execution timelines cannot be invented.

## Decision versus Just Dial, BLS and GESHIP

- **Just Dial:** gross financial assets slightly exceed equity market value, but longstanding promoter capital-retention, 2021 ₹2164.88cr preferential proceeds earmarked for growth and historical nil dividends make the cash unlock uncertain. OPEN008 time-discount tests undermine free-cash arbitrage.
- **MAHSEAMLES:** **real dividend** and an official corporate action are more tangible than a passive asset discount. Yet *investment-mark volatility*, falling revenue and earnings, anomalous FY26 working-capital cash releases and demerger allocation/approval risks preclude a BUY.
- **BLS/IndiaMART:** have service/business recurrence but customer prepayments, tenders, treasury gains and cash ownership require separate analysis. They are not dominated by Maharashtra Seamless based on nominal ₹10.19 P/E.
- **GESHIP:** historic persistent NAV discount plus peak shipping TCE and company-estimated vessel value warrant a conditional watch price, not an instant NAV discount play.

**Relative finding: high-quality source-backed EVENT WATCH**, not high-conviction investment. Original Oct7 board demerger announcement is a **testable future milestone**; proposed separation does not itself generate free shareholder cash. Without each resulting entity's normalized CFO/capex and assigned liabilities/portfolio, no legal sum-of-parts or intrinsically underwritten current share price can be computed.
