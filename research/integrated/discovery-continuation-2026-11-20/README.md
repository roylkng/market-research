## The four findings that changed the economics

### CMS: the cash question is now specific and priced

The audited FY26 capital-addition and payment-timing bridge explains ₹410.378cr against ₹409.268cr reported cash investment, with a retained ₹1.110cr residual. The broad annual discrepancy is substantially explained. It is not a reason to keep the entire case indefinitely unfinished.

The important new deduction is customer financing. Trailing cash after selected PPE, leases and interest is ₹206.731cr. Combining the operating and customer-loan cash-flow lines reduces that to **₹143.802cr** before separate acquisitions. Receivables converted into loans are not cash collected from a customer. H1's negative ₹8.992cr stock-compensation adjustment also cannot become a positive earnings addback.

The funded model uses a more demanding cash-investment budget than management's guidance. Approximately ₹200cr starting investment supports the 10.8–12.5% working range. A ₹250cr starting budget growing with sales cuts the result to **6.1%**. A combined margin, investment, exit-multiple and customer-financing stress gives **−1.1%**. The cash budget is economically decisive. One credible issuer-origin bridge can resolve it. There is no arbitrary requirement to wait two more quarters. [Complete CMS case](cms/cms_review_2026-11-20.md).

### R Systems: real cash, with real claims on that cash

Cash after all reported PPE/software investment, full leases and financing interest is **₹240.615cr for the twelve months to June 2026**. It is not a September cash-flow figure. The model repays the ₹275cr acquisition debt on its dated maturities, funds operating liquidity and includes existing incentive-plan capacity, Velotio conversion shares and scenario-based Novigo stock consideration.

Novigo's disclosed additional stock formula is eight times average FY26/FY27 EBITDA less the ₹400cr upfront payment. The final exchange ratio is unpublished. Modeling a range is sufficient for a research judgment. Treating that uncertainty as an automatic veto would recreate the old conversion problem.

At ₹242.50, the working case is 9.6–10.6%. At ₹210–220, the author convention produces approximately **14.0–12.9%**. Evidence supporting 8% organic growth and a 19.5% cash EBITDA margin produces **13.1% at the current price**, without raising the multiple. The issuer attributes much of recent EBITDA improvement to FX, so headline growth is not proof of that route. Joint moderate stress gives **2.0%**. Independent review also increased the assumed cost of a future compensation cycle. [Complete R Systems case](fresh/README.md), [independent challenge](independent/rsystems/rsystems_independent_review.md).

### Medi Assist: seasonality improves the diagnosis, tax normalization limits the value

The earlier ₹1.522cr half-year cash residual was a bounded diagnostic, not a perpetual estimate. The full trailing bridge now gives **₹84.198cr** after software/PPE investment, leases and nonlease interest. However, trailing cash tax is unusually low and reported profit includes a deferred-tax acquisition adjustment. Neither is a permanent cash windfall.

The bottom-up working model starts at about ₹89.7cr operating owner cash and reaches ₹131.1cr in year five, after normal tax and investment. The result is **3.5%** at the current price. Releasing the conservative cash reserves and immediately monetizing the tax asset still raises it only to **5.4%**. This is a priced pass, not an unfinished source hunt. The earlier rejection of a new post-deadline buyback entry remains intact. [Complete Medi Assist case](mediassist/mediassist_review_2026-11-20.md).

### Accelya: a past dividend is a claim to test

The declared FY26 ₹80 dividend implies an 8.2% historical yield at the reference price. Actual FY26 cash dividends were ₹126.87cr against ₹99.16cr operating cash flow. After reported PPE/software investment and financing-classified leases, the selected residual was ₹44.45cr. Lease restructuring makes that historical residual unsuitable as a perpetual run rate.

The forward model normalizes leases and investment. Independent review also challenged the draft's repeated ₹15cr annual working-capital and contract-funding charge against only modest sales growth. The revised central assumption is ₹5cr, with ₹15cr retained as a stress and zero as a favorable alternative. This is an analyst normalization assumption, not newly observed cash.

The revision raises recurring first-year owner cash to **₹105.79cr, about ₹70.88 per share**, before separate legacy funding. The already committed final dividend is reserved from opening treasury. Q1 revenue growth is about 1.1% and the operating margin is lower than the prior-year quarter.

The revised working 12× exit yields **5.9%**, a 14× exit gives **8.6%**, and retaining the recalculated current owner-cash multiple gives **8.3%**. Combining the ₹5cr funding assumption with lower financing lease cash and a 14× exit gives about **9.6%**. This is a plausible income comparison with conditional economics, not a categorical rejection of the business. The historical dividend alone still does not establish sustainable income. The draft 3.5% central result is superseded and retained only in the independent revision record. [Complete Accelya case](accelya/accelya_review_2026-11-20.md).

## A surveillance miss corrected, without rewriting history

Cosmo's state GST search disclosure was published on 18 November around 20:09 IST. The retained routing record saw it before the consolidated decision register was authored that night. Our earlier Cosmo note and the 19 November handoff omitted it. That was a material omission in the handoff.

The original notice says an inspection/search was in progress at the Aurangabad units. At that time the company reported no communicated violation or allegation and no material operational impact. This is an event requiring investigation, not a quantified tax liability or a finding of wrongdoing. The correction is dated 20 November and preserves the earlier record. The old ₹650–700 conditional range cannot automatically trigger promotion without checking the event's scope and economic consequences. Final legal closure is not an arbitrary prerequisite if the risk can otherwise be bounded. [Original notice and chronology](cosmo_event/cosmo_event_review_2026-11-20.md).

The practical change is a closing reconciliation: before publishing a consolidated handoff or promoting a company, compare the existing official announcement routing since the last cutoff and read the material original filings. Routing metadata is a discovery aid, not accepted financial evidence. This changes the assisted research workflow, not the frozen source-acceptance rules.
