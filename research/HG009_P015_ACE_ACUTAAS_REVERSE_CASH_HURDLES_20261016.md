### Economic distinction

ACE's ₹324.01cr proxy is positive, but ₹13,764cr cross-date capitalization implies a high **42.48× proxy**. A business can compound and remain a poor investment if the starting price already assumes strong future growth. A valid underwritten case needs inventory/receivable cycle reversal, cash value of ₹560.26cr FY26 current investments without double counting other income, return on capacity additions, group/minority reconciliation and actual October diluted shares.

ACUTAAS's FY26 CFO minus actual PPE cash spending was **negative**. That does not prove a poor business because its capacity may be expanding and old FY26 cash output can understate normalized future cash. But an investment thesis depends on independently verifying commissioned capacity, order conversion, incremental after-tax ROIC, working-capital build, productive PPE and actual future dilution. **High future growth is a hypothesis to test, not something to assume because spending is high.**
