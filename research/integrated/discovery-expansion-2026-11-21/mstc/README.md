# MSTC: cash ownership, accounting correction and a completed price case

Research opened 21 November 2026 IST. Financial anchor 30 September 2026. Official NSE EQ close on 20 November: **₹656.55**, ISIN INE255X01014. All company amounts are ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.

## Decision

**PASS_AT_CURRENT_PRICE. CONDITIONAL_REOPEN_AROUND_500_OR_BETTER_CORE_ECONOMICS.**

MSTC has a valuable auction franchise and several possible new businesses. At ₹656.55, the bounded working case produces a **5.1% annualized five-year equity return**, including dividends and retained cash. The adverse case is **−10.6%**, the favorable case **17.4%**. These are scenarios, not assigned probabilities or a confidence interval.

At **₹500**, unchanged working economics produce **11.2%**, while the adverse case remains **−5.4%**. At today's price, increasing sustained revenue growth from 8% to 15% while holding other working assumptions fixed produces **10.2%**. These are two concrete ways the decision can improve. A lower share price alone cannot cure a deterioration in the underlying auction franchise or a materially larger legal claim.

The watch is now a completed economic assessment. A complete contract-by-contract cash certificate is not required to reject the present price. Positive promotion should depend on the economics after conservative cash ownership and liability treatment, rather than waiting for every uncertainty to disappear.

3. **Older CAG observations were missed in the earlier assessment.** The FY26 annual report already contained them before the watch was written. 4. **The valuation now follows all retained cash.** The earlier table used 50% distributions without an explicit retained-cash roll-forward. That made the treatment of the undistributed half ambiguous. On the identical old 10% growth and 15× terminal assumptions, crediting the retained half once raises the illustrative IRR from 2.91% to 5.41%, assuming no additional uses. This is a funding-treatment clarification, not an IRR arithmetic error. The new model below separately funds capital spending, working capital and platform costs, then accumulates every rupee retained and values it once at exit. The targeted [legacy check](../legacy-cash-audit/README.md) found that eClerx and CAMS already account for their cash correctly.
5. **Float earnings are separated from float principal.** Customer principal is excluded from equity cash. A bounded amount of interest that the business may earn on non-interest-bearing deposits is included separately and can be set to zero.

## 1. The business has an economic engine

MSTC earns fees for auction infrastructure, execution and related services. Its embedded relationships with government bodies and principals can support repeat transaction activity. The core opportunity over several years is more activity moving through established electronic channels, combined with additional services to existing clients. Management described new-client onboarding and assistance to principals in improving auction design in the 13 November call. These are issuer claims. We did not obtain independent customer retention or market-share confirmation. [M2]

H1 revenue rose from ₹162.43 crore to ₹204.26 crore. Trailing revenue is ₹411.49 crore. Trailing group PAT is ₹261.73 crore, implying about **17.7× reported earnings** at the current ₹4,622.11 crore equity value. Reported earnings include substantial interest income. The operating franchise and the bank balance therefore need separate valuation. [M1, M3]

The company has discontinued the bank-guarantee-backed marketing business from FY27. This reduces an earlier risk channel and changes period comparability. It does not eliminate legacy claims. The Government of India held 64.75% at the FY26 reporting date. Capital allocation and shareholder access to surplus cash remain relevant even when the operations generate cash. [M1 notes 5–6, M3 share-capital note]

The residual gives no credit to receivables, court claims or JV value. It is not a legal upper bound or an audited distribution amount. Other statutory liabilities, noncurrent obligations, restricted deposits, working liquidity and contingent outcomes still matter. [M1 p12]

The annual note provides a useful ownership map: March EMD/security deposits were ₹477.50 crore and customer deposits ₹205.68 crore. Interest accrued on borrowings of ₹78.89 crore was already included in other financial liabilities. March margin deposits were ₹29.00 crore. Some September categories lack an updated detailed note. [M3 pp217, 221]

The model credits ₹150/250/350 crore of usable opening treasury in adverse/base/favorable cases. These are analyst assumptions below the cash-only residual. As a reconciliation, deducting the reported other current and noncurrent liabilities/provisions, the earlier margin-deposit amount, a ₹14.71 crore reserve for disputed old-remittance treatment and a ₹75 crore operating buffer leaves approximately ₹262 crore. The base ₹250 crore is therefore a bounded cash-only allowance, not an assertion that management can distribute it immediately. Some reserves may prove conservative and some contingent obligations may require more.

The recognized ₹145.00 crore borrowing and the accrued interest already included in financial liabilities are deducted **once**. They are not paid again in forecast cash. Recoveries from corresponding disputed receivables are assigned zero value. Any additional unreserved claim is a separate stress.

H1 CFO of ₹962.30 crore contains ₹784.97 crore from trade payables and other financial-liability changes. Management attributed the September cash spike to creditor-payment timing and said cash had already normalized. Its reference to a normal ₹900–1,000 crore cash-and-equivalents balance was not a declaration of unrestricted shareholder cash. [M1 p13, M2]

The cash proxy already excludes the ₹14.71 crore liability writeback through the original reconciliation. Forecast taxes should not permanently inherit unusually favorable cash-tax timing. Using a 25.17% tax assumption and depreciation of 3% of sales produces ₹61.84 crore of normalized tax and about **₹174.08 crore** of core cash before interest at unchanged historical capital payments. [M1 p13, M3 cash flow, model]

Issuer legacy tender terms explicitly say some EMD/security balances bear no interest. Current Global EMD instructions describe bidder ledgers and requested refunds. This supports the possibility of float income while confirming refundable principal. It does not establish the average investable balance or income rights across every current contract. [M5, M6]

The base assumes ₹30 crore of after-tax float income, growing 3% annually. At the assumed 5.5% yield and 25.17% tax, this implies about ₹729 crore of average eligible non-interest-bearing float. The adverse/favorable income assumptions imply about ₹364/972 crore. March EMD plus customer deposits were ₹683.18 crore. Neither that date's balance nor September's temporary spike verifies the average investable amount or contractual income rights. Interest on the much smaller assumed shareholder treasury is calculated separately. Eliminating float income reduces the base five-year return from **5.1% to 2.3%**. This is a material assumption and is exposed in the calculator.

## 6. Long-term opportunity and counter-thesis

**Auction-network extension:** Better utilization of the existing principal/bidder network can produce fee growth with modest incremental capital. The favorable scenario requires that this occurs without margin compression or cash collection deterioration.

**Coal and mineral exchanges:** Management expects several years before full operation and is evaluating participation. Migration from auctions could also erode existing fees. A new exchange should therefore be evaluated on net incremental earnings after cannibalization, capital and competition. [M2]

**TReDS and EPR:** RBI's published status as of 16 November lists MSTC with in-principle authorization in the category that cannot yet operate. The call explicitly conditions launch on final authorization. No later final approval was verified in this review. EPR launch also depends on stakeholder/regulatory action. These projects merit monitoring, while zero attributed revenue is a reasonable current valuation treatment. [M2, M4]

**Capital allocation:** The business can accumulate substantial owner cash, but government control, legacy liabilities and the board's handling of old remittances affect how readily that cash reaches shareholders. The concern is observable allocation and accounting quality. No allegation of misconduct is made.

## Actionable reopening

- Around ₹500 with the core franchise and liability assessment intact, the valuation merits a positive decision review. This is not an order or an allocation recommendation.
- At the current price, durable core growth closer to 15%, supported by fee revenue and collections rather than temporary other income, can make the working economics more attractive.
- A credible reconciliation of own cash, counterparty balances and float interest can alter value immediately. There is no arbitrary requirement to wait two quarters.
- The FY27 ECL method, resolution of old-remittance treatment and any material legal development should update the relevant cash assumption. They should not trigger an automatic rejection of every part of the business.
- Final TReDS approval alone is insufficient. Adoption, pricing, cash investment and net incremental profit are the investment evidence.

## Reproducibility and limits

Run `python3 calculate.py` in this directory. `inputs.json` separates reported facts from scenarios. `outputs.json` contains annual cash bridges and sensitivities. `source_manifest.json` records six captured originals with actual byte sizes and hashes. The shared price file retains the NSE EQ row and original CSV provenance.

This is a current fundamental valuation and a retrospective mechanism check. It is not a blind historical stock-selection test, a complete market screen, an audited forecast or evidence of investment alpha. Earlier dated judgments remain intact. No live capital, frozen experiment or source-acceptance protocol is changed.
