## Audited two-year cash bridge

| Consolidated ₹ crore | FY25 | FY26 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pretax profit | 549.12 | 556.71 | +1.38% |
| Operating cash flow | 411.98 | 417.27 | +1.28% |
| Property, plant and equipment cash spend | 221.00 | 93.26 | -57.80% |
| CFO minus PPE, NOT owner FCF | 190.98 | 324.01 | +69.66% |

The ₹133.03 crore growth in this simple cash proxy decomposes exactly into **₹5.29 crore additional CFO** and **₹127.74 crore less PPE spending**. Approximately **96.02%** of the apparent cash-proxy improvement was attributable to lower PPE spend. With FY25 PPE spending held constant, the hypothetical FY26 proxy is only ₹196.27 crore.

This is not necessarily poor economics. A project coming online after heavy FY25 capex could be valuable. The result instead shows that **the value creation depends on productive reinvestment and returns on capital**, rather than current evidence of accelerating cash generated from operations.

The separately retained original October 15 EQ close was ₹1,155.70. P015's cross-dated June reported basic-share count gives an **illustrative** equity capitalization of about ₹13,764.39 crore. Comparing that same reference with FY25 and FY26 CFO-less-PPE produces source-proxy yields of ~1.39% and ~2.35%. Neither is a verified owner cash yield, and October diluted equity remains unresolved.

At an arbitrary 16× cash multiple, achieving even one-time 50% gross share appreciation requires around ₹1,290.41 crore annual owner cash, compared with the ₹324.01 crore unnormalized FY26 proxy. Achieving 50% compound annual gross appreciation over three years would require about ₹2,903.43 crore owner cash at that multiple. These are mathematical **reverse hurdles**, not credible growth forecasts.
