## Source-evaluation boundary

This P011 acquisition proves at most the custody of original
exchange PDF bytes. It does **not** prove by itself:

- whether management actually used "2x EBITDA" in this source;
- the definition or period of that EBITDA numerator and
  actual historical carve-out earnings;
- the inclusion or exclusion of ₹550 crore purchase taxes,
  retained debt, operating liabilities and working capital
  from a true transaction enterprise value;
- actual delivered synergies, annual revenue escalation by
  customer, contract renewals, gross margins and maintenance capex;
- contractual minority rights and conversion price for Authum;
- legal BTA transfer effective date.

These are separate original-page semantic and future audited
financial diligence gates. No per-share price target or
probability-weighted future return is published.
