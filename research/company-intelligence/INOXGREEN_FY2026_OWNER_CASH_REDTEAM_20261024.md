2. **Main pre-tax vs segment:** consolidated PBT 157.81 versus segment bridge PBT 160.71, difference 2.90 crore. This also needs a reconciliation.

4. **Capital formation and dilution:** FY26 equity capital 401.49 crore versus earlier 367.02 crore shown. Filing does not by itself prove contemporaneous market cap, weighted average fully diluted shares or warrant overhang. Direct equity and other equity instrument proceeds are not operating cash.
5. **Cash restrictions:** ₹417.69 crore other bank balances, ₹550.35 crore noncurrent investments and ₹352.53 crore other noncurrent financial assets cannot be treated as free distributable cash without note-by-note encumbrance, ownership, tax and counterparty verification. Nominal cash equivalents ₹86.89 crore do not by themselves determine net cash.
6. **Investing economics:** large net investing outflow is not automatically business capex. Need acquisition/investment consideration breakdown, intercorporate deposits, mutual-fund movements and recurring maintenance capex, plus minority interests.
7. **Profitability:** large positive other income (₹145.20 crore) and substantial fair-value cash-flow reversal (-₹121.43 crore) demand recurring earnings separation. Neither item alone quantifies pure non-cash profit or taxes attributable to the parent.
