**Objective:** find a better company-economics and valuation situation than GESHIP, not justify previous selections. **No approved BUY, no capital, no expected annualized return.** This is ex-post 25-Oct diligence from FY2026 originals and 23-Oct quotes. It is **not** a new 15-Oct HG009 prospective signal, and candidate identity cannot be inserted into the frozen 100/300 original denominators. The original HG009 panel includes TRANSRAILL in its ZERO_LANE arm, not BLS.

## Method

1. Source dated NSE closing price rather than an undated quote.
2. Match original audited *consolidated*, year-ended 31-Mar-2026 **post-income-tax** CFO and PPE cash purchases. Never confuse cash generated before tax with after-tax CFO.
3. Use **parent-attributable earnings** for price/EPS and identify minority cash claims. Consolidated cash minus PPE is **not** verified parent owner cash. Do not add putative net cash without checking restrictions, deferred consideration, partial subsidiary ownership and legal cash transfer.
4. Disclose known adverse government, litigation, credit, contract and capital intensity facts, even if those make a lower multiple less attractive.
5. Four **hypothetical terminal value** cases over two years. Inputs are displayed and not calibrated forecast probabilities. `two-year-price = audited FY26 diluted EPS × (1 + one-time EPS shock) × (1 + assumed annual EPS growth)^2 × assumed terminal P/E`. No dividend, execution taxes, fees, refinancing, additional dilution or currency impact included. No scenario receives a probability.
