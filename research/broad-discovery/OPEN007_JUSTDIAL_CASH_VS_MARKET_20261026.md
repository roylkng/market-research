## The decisive problem: shareholder value realization

- The audited FY2026 company annual report **explicitly states no dividends declared after FY2014–15 through FY2025–26**. Its FY2026 AGM calendar shows no dividend payment date. A company can hold cash in low-risk funds for a decade while public shareholders cannot compel pro rata distribution.
- Promoter group owned approximately **74.14% at Sep2026**, Reliance Retail Ventures alone **63.83%**. It controls capital allocation. This is a strong strategic shareholder, **not a bank guarantee to minority shareholders**. Source: https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/45869/just-dial-ltd/shareholding/ and original FY26 annual ownership notes. Assess RIL/RRVL transactions, fund restrictions, intra-group services, potential related-party M&A, buybacks and realistic dividends from board resolutions. Do not infer a catalyst from balance sheet alone.
