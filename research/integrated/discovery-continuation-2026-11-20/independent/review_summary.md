# Independent checks and what they establish

The four company cases now have complete operating cash, ownership and terminal-value calculations. A second research agent or the integrating reviewer challenged the material economics. The independence here is between implementations and reviewers within this research session. It is not an external financial audit, independently adjudicated evidence, or out-of-sample investment validation.

## Corrections preserved rather than erased

Accelya's original 3.5% working result is retired as the central estimate. The original inputs and results remain in its independent-review folder so the change can be audited. The new ₹5cr funding assumption is a judgment about normalization, not a newly discovered cash receipt. The revision prevents an excessive conservative assumption from creating an artificial rejection.

R Systems' early cash bridge omitted ₹2.039cr intangible purchases. That was corrected before the final company model. Existing stock awards and acquisition consideration are not silently ignored, but neither are they deducted twice as both equity dilution and another cash liability.

CMS's negative stock-compensation adjustment is a credit in the source cash-flow bridge. It is not a positive earnings addback. The annual capitalization-to-cash difference is substantially reconciled with a residual retained. The exact interim capital-payment split is a specified economic uncertainty.

The Cosmo correction records an actual surveillance omission. The original 18 November search filing was available before the consolidated handoff. It does not establish a tax demand or misconduct. The dated correction preserves the earlier record and requires a final material-announcement reconciliation before future promotion.

## Comparison limits that remain

All modeled returns cover five forward years and use the same official reference-price date. Cash observation periods differ. CMS and Medi Assist have trailing September cash bridges, R Systems has a trailing June bridge, and Accelya's audited year ends in June with a September earnings update. The report does not imply four matching trailing cash periods.

The working ranges vary different assumptions. CMS varies recurring investment. R Systems varies terminal cash timing. Accelya varies the terminal multiple. Medi Assist displays one central path and retains separate sensitivity grids. These are not probability intervals or a common calibrated ranking model.

Source hashes establish custody and integrity of the files. Visual and text inspection supports the specific claims recorded in the notes, not every table in each report. Issuer assertions about customers, future growth or governance are not treated as independent corroboration. Missing source routes remain failed, not silently promoted to captured originals.

Future collections, renewal economics, capital allocation, sustainable margins and market exit multiples remain uncertain. Scenario losses are not maximum-loss bounds. No scenario probability, portfolio suitability determination, executed trade or demonstrated post-cost selection advantage has been created. The earlier historical audit's limitations remain unchanged.
