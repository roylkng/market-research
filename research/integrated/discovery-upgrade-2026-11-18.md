# Discovery review: priced decisions and a larger historical test

**18 November 2026. All current stock prices below are verified official NSE closes for that date. This is investment research, not a transaction or a change to portfolio policy.**

The goal is a repeatable discovery process that produces defensible, price-sensitive investments and learns from both bad purchases and missed opportunities. The earlier six-observation replay was insufficient. It selected only two surviving companies, reconstructed judgments after outcomes were visible and did not complete the current valuations. More confidence could not honestly be inferred from it.

This session completed two current company underwritings, three bounded additional priced reviews, a 196-issuer current-price attention review, a 300-identity frozen historical diagnostic and a follow-through check on four genuinely recorded prior decisions. These are different kinds of evidence. They must not be added together and called five validated buys or 300 independent investment tests.

## Datamatics is no longer waiting for an acquisition explanation

The initial 80% of TNQ cost ₹348.12cr. The last 20% cost ₹206.80cr and completed on 31 July 2026. Total consideration was ₹554.92cr before separately identified acquisition/financing costs. TNQ's audited FY2026 cash after capex and leases was about ₹100cr, or ₹78.45cr after removing favorable working-capital movements. That is 18.0% or 14.1% of purchase consideration, not an acquisition IRR. The marginal final 20% produced a much lower approximately 7.6% cash-return proxy at its purchase price.

The consolidated trailing owner-cash bridge deducts capex, leases, cash interest, a current-tax timing reserve, a provisional minority reserve and phantom compensation. It supports approximately ₹255cr of operating owner cash, or ₹270cr including a conservative treasury contribution. The model adds no separate net cash. An independent review corrected the preliminary tax normalization and required a lower cash sensitivity because loan amortization can consume treasury assets.

Both rows assume 8% annual growth in owner cash after necessary reinvestment and a 12-times final-year owner-cash multiple. The conservative case values are substantially lower, so ₹600 is not a protected downside price.

## The larger historical test weakens confidence in the old ranking

The repository already contained a stronger design than the six retrospective cases. On 14 October, before the 15 October entry, it froze 100 selected names and two 100-name comparison groups. This session calculated the previously specified 20-session endpoint through 12 November, preserving original identities and the earlier Muhurat-session amendment.

|---|---:|---:|
| Selected, two or three opportunity lanes | 98 / 100 | **−0.70%** |
| One-lane control | 98 / 100 | **+0.66%** |

| Nifty 500 price index, context only | Index | **+8.21%** |

The selected mean trails the one-lane comparison by **1.36 percentage points**. This provides no mean advantage for the old ranking rule on this endpoint. It does not prove that current company underwriting has no value.

The script actually reparsed 40 archived original stock/index files, verified their hashes, reconciled 5,623 archived OHLC rows and reproduced earlier H1/H5 means. The parent researcher independently reran the same script successfully against the repository checkout.

Six original identities remain null because of trading-series changes, a symbol rename and an absent row under the frozen rule. They are not silently dropped from the original 100 slots or treated as delistings. The table's means are conditional on the 98 complete observations in each arm. All 294 paths still lack full corporate-action/dividend clearance. Costs and executable fills are unmeasured. This is one common entry event, with turnover-band controls rather than sector/size/risk matching. It is a raw-price diagnostic, not an economic portfolio backtest or alpha estimate.

**Action:** the number of opportunity lanes remains a way to route research. It is not evidence that one share will outperform another. No frozen experiment, cohort, label or outcome ledger was rewritten.

## Broader discovery and the mistakes it exposed

All 196 issuers in the retained financial dataset were matched to fresh official prices. Twenty-one pass the initial attention filter. The financial snapshot remains dated 8 November, 192 issuers have comparable six-month periods, and the imported financial facts are unreviewed. This is not a full current census of all NSE/BSE opportunities.

The source reviews produced specific corrections:

- **Cosmo:** interest and dividend tags were swapped in the structured filing. The cash totals still balanced. The signed statement reduced the selected cash measure by ₹63.76cr. Hashing and total reconciliation did not catch financial meaning.
- **Sandesh:** the signed CFO's fair-value sign conflicts with the profit description and investment roll-forward. A hypothetical repair is disclosed as a hypothesis, not silently substituted for reported accounts.
- **CMS:** a noncash CWIP transfer cannot, on its own, reconcile a lower capex definition with the statutory cash paid. The case now asks for that exact bridge instead of an indefinite request for “more evidence.”
- **Datamatics:** a cash decline was acquisition deployment rather than an operating collapse. Current tax, minority attribution and financing had to be handled explicitly.
- **GESHIP:** normalizing FX/tax did not normalize the freight cycle or remove asset-sale gains. A NAV discount does not assure that the asset value or market discount remains constant.

The queue, current price identities, input hashes, period correction and primary-source overrides are retained in [the breadth review](discovery-upgrade-2026-11-18/breadth/README.md). Cosmo, Sandesh and CMS are additional bounded cases. They are not described as full independent diligence across every framework lens.

## Operational changes for the next run

1. Start from the priced decision register. Do not repeatedly reopen GESHIP or Datamatics merely because their names were previously prioritized.
2. A fresh company case needs source-semantic checks, cash attributable to owners, explicit downside and a dated price judgment. A missing nonmaterial culture metric must not postpone an otherwise supportable judgment indefinitely.
3. A WATCH must say whether price, business evidence or both can change it. Recheck at the same price when cash evidence improves. Price falls accompanied by business deterioration require a new model.
4. Keep the old signal, the current company theses and portfolio allocation separate. No stock-specific WATCH establishes an all-market cash recommendation. Broad-market price performance is opportunity-cost context, not a forward return forecast.
5. Preserve prior rejections, price references and the new decisions for follow-through. Review business evidence as it arrives and examine 30/90/180-day reference-price outcomes without claiming those horizons validate a five-year thesis.
6. Use the next research slots for unfinished or new names, including the existing N R Agarwal cash/funding case and fresh candidates outside the five completed reviews. Queue membership is not a return rank.

The existing daily NSE Opportunity Watch is the continuation mechanism. Its research prompt is updated in place after this register is published. Its schedule, enabled state, research-only restrictions and frozen experiment boundaries are preserved. Actual remote commit/PR and automation status are recorded in the session handoff and delivery record rather than assumed from local files.
