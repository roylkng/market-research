## Six overlapping review gates, never stock ratings

- CFO minus original PPE **and** intangible purchases is nonpositive.
- Original standard PPE purchase tag is zero while net investing cash is material.
- Original other-investing purchase category exceeds the absolute CFO-minus-PPE proxy.
- Absolute net investing source cash is larger than the crude proxy.
- Original net-investing cash tag is absent.
- Other original nonzero investing component tags require note reconciliation.

Do NOT add all concepts together: investing cash may contain gross purchases, proceeds, nested/net components and source label sign anomalies. An empty tag is unknown spending coverage, not guaranteed zero. A reported source cash outflow is not automatically sustaining expenditure.
