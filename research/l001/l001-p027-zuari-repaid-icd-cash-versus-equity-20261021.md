## Correct first-principles parent equity accounting

**For the lender ZUARIIND:**

Before repayment, a loan receivable is a parent-company asset. After principal repayment, cash or bank balance replaces it. **₹258.50 crore of principal repayment is NOT ₹258.50 crore of new equity wealth or recurring profit.** It may meaningfully improve liquidity and reduce lender/covenant risk. Only net new cash earnings, interest recovered, financing saved or a proven reversal of a previously impaired receivable could alter economic value. The parent cash receipt and loan-receivable movement have not yet been independently reconciled against parent bank statements and the September quarter original consolidated/standalone financials.

Do **not** add ₹258.50 crore to the holding company's five listed stakes or to its original NAV while keeping the old receivable on the balance sheet. Likewise do not reduce March net borrowings by the same cash without observing how ZUARIIND actually used the receipts. Pledges/encumbrances require matching to the specific creditor and debt, never a second arbitrary collateral deduction.

**For the borrower ZUARI:** Debt repayment reduces cash and the related liability. Reducing a loan balance is not itself a source of shareholder profit. ZUARI is the **fertilizer issuer**, NSE symbol ZUARI, not the Zuari Industries parent symbol ZUARIIND. The 2026-09-30 original filing does not establish ZUARIIND's current fully diluted capital, parent ownership of every five other listed stake, or the ultimate tax/distribution.
