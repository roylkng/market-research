## Lessons

- An NSE keyword label is a **document retrieval hint only**.
- Direct listed-equity offers must be separated from debt buybacks, subsidiary
  funding, other-entity allotments and procedural disclosures.
- A current EQ symbol match uses the security master **as of ingestion**;
  it is not point-in-time proof of issuer identity on an earlier date.
- Never convert a filing category into a positive return expectation without
  source-bound terms, legal stage, capital reconciliation, valuation and
  independent red-team review.
