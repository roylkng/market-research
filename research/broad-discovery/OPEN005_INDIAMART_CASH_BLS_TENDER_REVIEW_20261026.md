**Derived, source-specific economic stress checks:**

- FY26 consolidated CFO grew **11.39%** versus FY25, while operating profit before working capital rose only **4.13%**. Slower operating cash-production growth is a quality issue, not proof of insolvency.
- An FY26 **₹287.6cr contract-liability cash inflow** represented **41.43% of reported net operating cash**. It is customer prepayment inflow that also represents future service delivery. **It is not identical to a full refundable cash debt**, and using its entire movement as a permanent cost is not a normalized valuation either.
- The FY26 group's CFO ₹694.2 minus directly selected ₹7 PPE/intangible and ₹13.2 lease outflow = **₹674cr selected-use cash**, ~7.17% of the OCT26 12:52 market-cap observation. After FY26 ₹26.8cr deferred acquisition cash = ₹647.2cr; after FY26 ₹300cr shareholder dividends = ₹347.2cr **period residual before other investment/financing claims**. None is normalized parent-shareholder FCFE.
- If FY26 contract-liability cash inflow were **only at FY25's ₹237.7cr pace**, rather than ₹287.6cr, selected-use cash = **₹624.1cr** (~6.64% quote-implied equity value).
- Deliberately severe *zero additional contract-liability inflow* scenario: ₹694.2−₹287.6−₹7−₹13.2 = **₹386.4cr**, ~4.11% of referenced equity value. **Not a forecast, not a permissible normalization without obligations and recurring contract rollovers**.
- **Parent cash ownership is more direct than BLS:** standalone parent FY26 operating cash was **₹668.5cr = 96.30% of group CFO**, and standalone Q1 FY27 cash/investments ₹3,316cr were **93.33% of group gross ₹3,553cr**. Compare BLS standalone ₹62.28cr CFO versus ₹902.93cr group, but BLS additionally reports actual subsidiary dividends ₹102.44cr. Accounting statement perimeter differs. IndiaMART's parent cash is **not automatically unrestricted or fully distributable**, but upstream visibility is materially better.
- FY26 parent's CFO minus selected ₹1.4cr capex and ₹13.2cr lease payment = **₹653.9cr**; after ordinary ₹300cr dividend = ₹353.9cr before other parent investing, debt, capex obligations, tax reclaims or acquisition finance.
- June group cash/investments ₹3,553cr and deferred revenue ₹2,014cr **must remain separate facts**, NOT be subtracted as if all deferred income were payable debt, NOR treated as ₹3,553cr freely distributable excess cash. Need cost-to-fulfil, escrow/restrictions, noncontrolling interests, and actual repatriation audit.

**Valuation conclusion:** there is a credible, economically inspectable, asset-light marketplace cash business at an ~₹9,404cr dated intraday market capitalization, but the headline **7.17% selected-use cash proxy can look closer to 4.11% under a severe future-prepayment stress**. These are **not intrinsic-value ranges** or after-cost expected yields. Marketplace supplier retention and price increases determine which cash case is more representative; the next quarterly result is the falsifiable near-term gate. The new finance subsidiary could introduce risk, but a ₹5-lakh initial capital figure does not establish large risk-taking. No BUY approval.
