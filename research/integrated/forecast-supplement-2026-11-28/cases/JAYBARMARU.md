# Jay Bharat Maruti: prospective December 2026 PAT

**Status:** research forecast, consolidated PAT attributable to owners, INR crore. Recorded 2026-11-27T19:46:43.127227Z.

**Base 29.81cr. Adverse 16.44cr. Favorable 43.48cr.** These are scenarios without assigned probabilities.

Separate manufacturing EBIT, recognized government incentives, other income and after-tax joint-venture contribution. Strong Q2 revenue does not justify extrapolating every income component at the same growth rate. The base allows modest manufacturing growth while normalizing other income.

## Assumptions and arithmetic

The accompanying JSON stores each signed component, tax rate, after-tax associate profit and NCI. The evaluator reproduces all values.

- **Manufacturing EBIT:** 820cr revenue x 2.5 percent margin = 20.5cr. Revenue above Q2 ex-incentive 793.6281cr and margin moderately above derived 2.21 percent. These are judgmental assumptions, not company guidance.
- **Government incentive:** 29. Near Q2 recognition, below Q1 and prior-year quarters. Timing and eligibility remain uncertain.
- **Other income and financing:** 1cr other income and 12cr financing cost. Do not repeat the full Q2 other-income increase.
- **Tax and joint venture:** 25.17 percent tax on own PBT, then 1cr JV profit. Approximation to the new tax regime. JV profit is already after tax and is not taxed twice.

## What would invalidate the base

- Government incentive recognition below 22cr or delayed recognition would undermine the downside case.
- Other income remaining above 5cr would make the base conservative.
- Manufacturing volume growth without margin improvement would reduce the value of the revenue increase.
