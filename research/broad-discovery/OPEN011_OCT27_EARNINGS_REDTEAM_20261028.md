## INDIAMART: quarter economics good on the surface, deteriorating network activity underneath

All following are consolidated **Q2 FY27** unless stated. Issuer said:
- Revenue from operations **₹429cr, +10% YoY**; group EBITDA **₹159cr, +23% YoY**, **37% reported EBITDA margin**. Parent/standalone ₹389cr sales / ₹156cr EBITDA.
- Group after-tax operating cash **₹135cr, +19% YoY**, standalone parent **₹124cr, +14% YoY**. These are **period-specific company-reported amounts**; not audited normalized owner FCFE after sustaining product development and leases.
- Customer collections **₹443cr, +9% YoY** and Q2 consolidated deferred revenue **₹2,029cr, +16% YoY**. These deposits fund service obligations; they are neither entirely refundable debts nor 100% cash free to shareholders.
- Paying supplier count **approximately 217,000**, **net loss 615 this quarter** and **~2% YoY reduction**. A rounded end-count cannot be combined with the exact -615 to invent an exact June share/customer denominator. Silver-tier churn remains elevated. Platinum/Gold tiers are approximately half of paying customers but ~75% of revenue and retained better, creating price/mix sensitivity.
- Unique business enquiries approximately **27mn, down 14% YoY**. Management attributed some engagement pressure to AI-search/data-scraping changes and increased its efforts on supplier data protection. Engagement weakness may precede supplier churn. Earlier Q1 already lost ~1,850 paying suppliers; not proof of statistically independent monthly attrition.
- Revenue per paying supplier annualized **₹72,000, +10% YoY**, meaning revenue growth currently depends disproportionately on realization/upgrade mix versus new merchant volume.
- Group quarterly **other income −₹8cr** due in part to bond mark-to-market losses; management expects recovery, not guaranteed. It must not be called recurring marketplace negative operating income.
- IndiaMART reported **₹3,234cr cash and treasury** Sept30. ### Explicit margin and cash-access valuation falsification

The stock's completed 28-Oct NSE close was approximately **₹1,572.70** and market-cap provider showed **~₹9,469cr**. Do not imply a verified executable size or current fully diluted original equity proof.

Management's long-run **EBITDA margin guidance remains 33%**, versus the unusually strong **37% actually reported** in Q2. Annualizing Q2 sales at *unchanged ₹429cr/quarter* is a **static stress**, NOT a FY27 forecast:
- At 33% margin, hypothetical annual core EBITDA = **₹566.28cr**.
- At 37% margin, hypothetical annual core EBITDA = **₹634.92cr** (versus using the actual rounded ₹159cr×4 = ₹636cr).
- The full September ₹3,234cr reported cash/treasury deducted from the approximate equity cap leaves **₹6,235cr residual**, but is **not economic EV**: we have not resolved all liabilities, lease obligations, tax on treasury gains, minority rights, historical use-of-proceeds/restricted money or the real cost of fulfilling ₹2,029cr deferred-revenue obligations.

The access fractions are **accounting/reinvestment sensitivities**, not actual legally restricted amounts or probabilistic stock-price targets. Treating all ₹3,234cr as shareholder cash is a mistake; treating none as economic shareholder value is also unjustified. Especially avoid subtracting *both* all deferred revenue as if payable tomorrow and also the costs to deliver contracted services.

**Decision: DEEP DILIGENCE BUT NO BUY.** This is a higher-quality operating cash source than many industrial examples, but without supplier count stabilizing and unique inquiries growing, a supposedly cheap adjusted multiple may represent an eroding network-effect franchise.
