## Critical economic interpretation

One Q1 shows improved profitability and reported net cash, supporting an *investigation* of recurring operating margin expansion. However a release's cash figure and a contingent-liability fair-value change do not resolve shareholder cash ownership. There is no evidence here of a market pricing error. Nor does a +43.5% Q1 owner PAT mean FY27 PAT will rise by that percentage: seasonality, acquisition consolidation and base effects require control.
