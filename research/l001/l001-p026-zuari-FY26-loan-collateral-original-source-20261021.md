# L001 P026: Zuari's listed investee shares are not necessarily free collateral

**Discovery:** October 21, 2026. **Source period:** year ended March 31, 2026. **No buy recommendation or attributable equity valuation.**

## The next decisive uncertainty

P025 revalidated a hypothetical **₹473.87 crore** Oct19 stake-mark gap when subtracting stale March net borrowings and old basic-share cap. A further **12.59%** haircut on listed investee marks erases that already fragile arithmetic gap. The FY26 annual report now identifies another direct constraint: a number of Zuari Industries and subsidiary financing instruments were secured by **Chambal Fertilisers shares** and other pledged assets.

The original issuer [FY26 annual report](https://www.zuariindustries.in/storage/uploads/images/1787553470.pdf) was submitted August 24, 2026, with detailed 31 March 2026 borrowing notes.

These are **report-period observations, not October 21 liabilities**. The number of distinct shares pledged must be reconciled with lien ledgers before aggregating or subtracting them from equity NAV. Some facilities may share collateral or have been refinanced. Pledged assets are not an extra deduction **on top of an already correctly netted secured debt**. Rather, collateral can constrain distributability and require creditor consent, affecting discount, realization timing, tax and capital allocation.

### Why the finance mismatch matters

The source-pinned issuer Q1 FY27 presentation in P025 showed consolidated segment EBITDA ₹8.05 crore versus quarterly finance costs ₹61.54 crore. The ratio 13.08% is a red flag to investigate, **not an annual cash debt-service ratio**, particularly because sugar, agricultural cycles and property handovers are seasonal. Operating cash, parent dividends from affiliates, actual cash taxes, matured obligations and pledged collateral release are not independently reconciled.

A broad SOTP model that marks shares and assumes they can simply be sold to shareholders would miss this economic constraint.
