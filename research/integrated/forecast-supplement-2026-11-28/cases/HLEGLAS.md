## Operating hypothesis

Assume partial conversion of existing equipment orders and a modest improvement in operating mix. Q2 profit recovery is not extrapolated at its sequential growth rate. December FY26 was weak even before its exceptional costs, so repeating that quarter mechanically conflicts with the current backlog evidence. This is a testable operating hypothesis, not proof of a seasonal break.

| Favorable | 410 | 10.00% | 2.5 | 8.5 | 25.0% | 2.3 | 23.95 |

EBIT is after depreciation and excludes other income. Owner PAT = (revenue × EBIT margin + other income − finance costs) × (1 − tax rate) − NCI. Associates and new exceptional items are zero assumptions. Scenarios are uncalibrated sensitivities with no assigned probabilities.

- **Revenue:** 365 cr is 5.3% above current Q2. It assumes some execution of the September backlog. No specific December revenue is assigned to the November 10 order.
- **EBIT margin:** 8.3% versus approximately 7.72% current Q2 and 3.67% Q1. Modest utilization and mix improvement, well below extrapolating the sequential PAT jump.
- **Other Income:** 2.3 cr, near current Q2 2.3255 cr.
- **Finance Cost:** 8.5 cr, near Q2 8.3901 cr. No immediate deleveraging assumed.
- **Tax Rate:** 25% is an analyst group-tax assumption. Q1 effective tax was unstable and prior December showed a tax credit, so a mechanical recent-tax extrapolation is inappropriate.
- **NCI:** 1.8 cr, near Q2 1.7127 cr. Subsidiary earnings remain partly attributable to minority shareholders.

## Normalization and source corrections

Main adjustments remain zero because the signed statements disclose group pretax costs but not their after-tax owner allocation. Prior Q2 transaction costs 3.0752 cr and prior Q3 transaction costs 1.11 cr plus labour-code expense 2.0673 cr are documented. A separate, explicitly assumed normalization is below.

Current owner PAT agrees with frozen extraction. Headline group PAT 16.0197 cr is not the target 14.3070 cr.

Separately assuming 25% tax benefit and 100% owner allocation gives addbacks 2.3064 cr/2.3830 cr for prior Q2/Q3, and an adjusted additive forecast of 5.23 cr. These allocations are assumptions, not disclosed facts.

## Decisive unknowns

- Backlog is not a quarterly revenue schedule. Management cited a 4-7 month cycle and roughly 210-220 cr of the September backlog for delivery over about three years.
- Kinam orders and customer acceptance are lumpy. The USD 42-48m November order extends through November 2028 and commencement conditions are not public.
- Tax credits, losses in overseas businesses and NCI can move owner PAT independently of operating profit.
- Exact after-tax owner allocation of historical acquisition and labour-code costs is not disclosed.
