## Why another gate matters

Our initial HG002 28-name outside-U001 universe contains five particularly
relevant transaction/asset catalyst cases: ANANTRAJ, DEVX, INOXGREEN, NPST
and SAMBHV. HG005-D003 converted their narratives into numerical reverse
hurdles or sensitivities, *not* expected returns.

Eleven newer reports now have original exchange/issuer/credit source evidence
that materially changes the interpretation of their gross headline values.
Without a combined source state, it is easy to quote a 50% theoretical
equity-equivalent uplift while missing rent, dilution, outside funding,
interest subordination, noncore income and untransferred cash.

This protocol pins the **exact 11 existing original research JSON Git blobs**
and reconstructs the five old HG005 companies without introducing stocks,
weights, revised price forecasts or post-hoc tested selection.

## Why these findings are not 50% CAGR opportunities yet

The numerator of an investment payoff is not a management headline or
a gross enterprise-value sensitivity. The following must separately
clear for each candidate:

1. An actual executable price and correct current fully diluted listed
   share denominator, corporate-action adjusted if necessary.
2. Case-specific normalized recurring free cash generation, not gross
   asset spending, management multiples, escrow/FDR balances or
   nonrecurring income.
3. Post-transaction actual consolidated net debt, third-party funding
   rights, share dilution, lease liabilities and minority attributable
   equity economics.
4. Legal transaction stage as of each original exchange publication
   cutoff. Proposals, investor presentations and QIP uses are not
   completed earnings.
5. Comparable downside, business survival and a prospectively
   calibrated completion probability based on sufficient stage/family
   historical survivor outcomes.
6. Transaction frictions and a realistic margin of safety.

HG006 has **zero** publishable case-specific completion probability
surfaces. No current source warrants a 50%-uplift stock return forecast
or a buy recommendation.
