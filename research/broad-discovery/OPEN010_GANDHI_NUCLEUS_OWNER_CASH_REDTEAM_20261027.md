# OPEN010 | True shareholder distributions: Gandhi Special Tubes vs Nucleus Software

**Research time:** 27 October 2026 morning IST. **Last completed NSE quote session: Monday 26 October 2026.** This is an **open-market post-hoc diligence** study, independent from and not modifying HG009, H002 or H003 original prospective symbol/ISIN or outcome ledgers. Issuer originals and SEBI buyback statement were inspected visually. **Raw original PDF bytes and first retrieval SHA receipts are NOT archived in this repository. NO BUY / NO LIVE CAPITAL.**

## Executive conclusion

**Critical denominator/capital-allocation correction:** Gandhi Special Tubes accepted and later extinguished **868,100 original shares at ₹900 each**, leaving **11,283,900** shares versus 12,152,000 before the completed buyback. The SEBI post-buyback offer advertisement shows **8,44,704 accepted shares belonged to the promoter group, 97.30% of the repurchase**. Public databases quoting the older share count overstate Oct26 equity market cap by ₹72.41cr at the same observed NSE close. A legal buyback is *not* a riskless future tender opportunity, and a 97.3%-promoter participation buyback does not prove that minority investors received proportionately the buyback cash. It is also not proof of wrongdoing: the open tender's actual valid applications and SEBI allocation rules, including the small-shareholder reservation, are original documented facts.

For Nucleus, audited profits and after-tax operating cash are **declining**, and the cash/investment mix contains significant noncurrent assets. Historical dividends and the completed 2024 buyback establish that capital has reached shareholders in the past, but they do not independently establish present undervaluation.

Q2 effective diluted denominator, Q1 gains and post-buyback investments should be updated from **new original results** rather than extrapolating March balances.

**Provisional conclusion:** Quality-industrial and shareholder-capital-allocation *watch*, **not a high-conviction pick**. Promoter receipt concentration and marked investment income weaken simplistic low P/E / strong-buyback conclusions. Evaluate true order book, customers and recurring manufacturing PAT and minority-friendly distributions before an entry-price calculation.

## What this adds beyond the prior 24-name investigation

The initial issuer universe expanded beyond the old ORIGINAL-10 and OPEN003 23-name review with Just Dial, Maharashtra Seamless, Gandhi and Nucleus. This is not an exhaustive evaluation of all 2,300+ NSE stocks; we are open to unreviewed companies. False-confidence traps specifically corrected here:

- **OLD SHARE COUNT**: post-repurchase outstanding must be used for market cap; company share register becomes the point-in-time authority.
- **PROMOTER-CASH DOMINATED BUYBACK**: a completed buyback can be proportionately realized nearly entirely by controlling shareholders while still lawfully increasing public ownership percentage.
- **PORTFOLIO-DRIVEN PAT**: original Q1 Gandhi fair-value gain alone equals ~26% of its PBT; Nucleus Q1 profits fell despite FY26 software platform claims.
- **AS-OF BALANCES**: March financial assets cannot be netted unchanged against October prices after a completed repurchase.
- **HISTORICAL OFFER PRICE**: an expired ₹900 tender does not provide present holders an arbitrage floor.
