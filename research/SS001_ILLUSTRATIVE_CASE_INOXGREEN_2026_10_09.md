## Why this is a useful research candidate, but not yet a hidden gem

The documents disclose a material potential **operating-services expansion**
with stated revenue history and a bounded proposed purchase consideration,
plus a separate scheme-related shareholder entitlement.

However, neither a large O&M portfolio nor a low apparent purchase-price/revenue
relationship proves shareholder value. We still lack:

1. **Completion and structure:** executed agreements, exact closing/effective
   date, approved consideration, liabilities and assets transferred.
2. **Operating economics:** normalized O&M EBITDA, contractual margins,
   customer concentration, renewal rates, working capital and free cash flow.
3. **Funding and capital allocation:** cash/debt financing, impact on standalone
   and consolidated earnings, acquisition ROIC.
4. **Scheme/share identity:** post-demerger INOXGREEN and IRSL issued share counts,
   class/ISIN continuity, listing status and actual shareholder entitlements.
5. **Valuation:** as-of price, verified market cap, current net debt and defensible
   downside/base/upside cases. **None is supplied by this source review.**
6. **Later-source check:** any announcements after the frozen 2026-10-01 cutoff
   belong to a separately dated update and cannot silently repair this snapshot.

## Key inference safeguard

The two threads show why keyword-only classifiers are insufficient:

- The `Demerger` action is not equivalent to a demonstrated INOXGREEN issued-share
  increase.
- An NCLT-approved resolution plan is not equivalent to an executed acquisition.
- INOXGREEN's prospective O&M business and INEL's IPP interest are different
  entities/assets.

**Research routing:** advance only to source-complete transaction-thread review.
Do not assign a probability, expected return, market capitalization, buy/sell/hold,
ADO/PF001 eligibility or live-capital permission.

**Outcome flags:** return outcomes unopened; capital/share clearance false;
portfolio eligibility false; live capital false.
