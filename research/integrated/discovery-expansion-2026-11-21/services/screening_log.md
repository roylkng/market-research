# Services, software and platforms discovery screen

**Scope:** Six new current-evidence screens, one full integrated company case and one additional compact funded recovery valuation. This is a bounded extension of discovery, not an all-market screen. Previously completed CMS, R Systems, eClerx, Accelya, Medi Assist, CAMS and Datamatics cases were not repeated. All prices below are original NSE EQ closes on 20 November 2026 with the matching ISIN retained in `screen_prices_2026-11-20.json`.

The selection principle was ownership of recurring customer workflows, defensible intellectual property, cash conversion and the price paid for future cash per share. A drawdown, a low nominal share price, an AI product launch and a low accounting P/E are not sufficient selection criteria.

## Why these were the two completed cases

NEWGEN offered a way to test the strongest durable-workflow argument against actual recurring cash and a less demanding reported multiple. The investigation then exposed collection and normalization issues that matter more than a lower quote. Its full case retains the positive subscription-transition argument and a concrete lower-price route.

NUCLEUS was added beyond the initial four names because its treasury was large relative to current equity value. It received a bounded original-source follow-through rather than being placed on a vague watchlist. The result is a complete economic answer to that lead: substantial financial assets exist, but a purchase at ₹611.45 still needs operating recovery. The negative cash residual was not treated as a permanent terminal loss. The model explicitly funds a partial recovery and shows what the current price requires.

MPS is the strongest positive current operating comparison in the remaining screen. This file does not conclude it is inferior simply because NEWGEN was selected first, or because MPS's nominal share price is larger. Its acquisition funding, contingent consideration, client concentration, capitalized content/software and cash per share need one joined valuation before assigning an investment ranking. Its one-day traded volume of 6,169 shares also means the daily close is not an assumed executable size.

## Source and claim discipline

All six prices come from the retained original NSE file. NEWGEN and NUCLEUS current cash and balance-sheet tables were visually inspected from captured originals. MPS signed result and call, REDINGTON exchange release and EMUDHRA deck were inspected as original PDF text for the screening claims above. INTELLECT is explicitly primary-text inspection without archived PDF bytes. The source manifest records exact URLs, retrieved UTC timestamps, byte sizes, hashes, failed downloads, wrong-document routing corrections and actual inspection limits.

Screening conclusions do not replace full cases. No stock return has been presented as a blind historical prediction. No hypothetical revenue or margin is promoted to a fact, no universal return threshold is imposed and no portfolio allocation or brokerage instruction is created.
