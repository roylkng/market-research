**Cash-quality mechanism:** The large H1 CFO improvement is not equivalent to a similarly large increase in recurring pre-working-capital cash. The RHP shows pre-working-capital cash generation of approximately ₹6,426.89 crore in current H1 versus ₹6,041.76 crore prior H1. Collections, liabilities and inventory movements explain much of the reported CFO increase. Rebuild after-tax, maintenance-versus-growth capital expenditures, stripping/restoration/environmental cash, subsidiary rights, restricted cash, future taxation and ongoing working capital before an owner-cash valuation.

**Customer-credit concentration:** The RHP reports delayed balances from Andhra Pradesh Power Generation Corporation Ltd of **₹1,929.88 crore** and Andhra Pradesh Power Development Co Ltd of **₹502.16 crore** at 2026-09-30, total **₹2,432.04 crore** against total trade receivables **₹3,767.78 crore** (approximately **64.55%**). No expected-credit-loss provision was taken against these particular state utility balances based on management's judgment. They are **not established losses**, but collection timing materially affects the apparent cash quality.

## CAG-disputed levy: an especially important non-additive risk

In the RHP's disclosure of **CAG supplementary audit observations and management responses** (printed pp. 55-56), CAG says Coal India's legal department had estimated a **₹31,227.80 crore possible outflow** in respect of MCL in 2024-25 connected to the Odisha ORISED mineral-land-tax / MADA matter. CAG criticizes the lack of separately quantified contingent-liability disclosure. Management replies that the underlying ORISED Act was struck down by Odisha High Court in 2005, no new enforceable law/quantified demand has arisen, and an obligating event is not established.

**Do not** call ₹31,227.80 crore a payment due or proven impairment. **Do not** add it mechanically to ₹12,513.29 crore stated contingent liabilities. The relationship between these scopes and legal predicates requires original legal orders, government demands, audit committee/accounting treatment, and counsel opinion. CAG's observation is real. The liability's crystallization is **unproven**. This gap materially weakens a simple 8.4x earnings inference without proving dishonesty.

## Minority governance and incentives

As of the **2026-11-13 RHP**, MCL's board had **no independent director**, although the issuer says the stated listing requirements would require six including a woman director. Ministry of Coal appoints directors and decides their terms; the audit committee cannot choose the CAG-appointed statutory auditor. The RHP states MCL **requested SEBI exemptions dated 2026-08-31** concerning board/committee composition and independent director price-band approval. The RHP does **not** establish approval of those requests. Government ownership may pursue public energy security and divestment alongside outside-shareholder returns. Neither this structure nor a disclosure discrepancy alone establishes malfeasance.

**Strongest counter-thesis:** A low absolute earnings multiple may represent genuine long-cycle cash production and franchise-scale mining with a stable state-backed customer base. Counterevidence to the bear case would be timely government-utility collections, substantiated all-in cost/capex/closure cash, independently adjudicated tax and levy exposure, minority governance remediation, and credible payout policy. The contra-bull case is that H1 CFO is a temporary working-capital benefit, a significant share of receivables sits with delayed government payers, coal's terminal duration may be overestimated and outside owners receive no IPO proceeds.

## Decision effect and falsifiers (2026-11-16)

**New:** The announced price band makes the IPO an immediate research decision; the 13 Nov original prospectus gives previously unadmitted **CAG ₹31,227.80 crore possible levy controversy**, **₹12,513.29 crore disclosed contingent amounts**, **₹2,432.04 crore government-payer delays**, and a **missing independent board**. These are material economically and for minority rights.

**Decision:** `WAIT_FOR_PRICE_OR_EVIDENCE / RESEARCH_ONLY_NOT_BUY`. Do not infer guaranteed pricing advantage from FY26 trailing P/E, IPO grey-market premiums, management dividends before listing, or proceeds from the 10% OFS. A new actual exchange-authenticated price is required **after** listing for any capital-ready claim, alongside original price-band instrument and a normal-cycle per-share valuation.

**Highest-value next documents:** independent CAG/SEBI/exchange original disclosure and statutory legal-risk resolution, original H1 receivable aging/collections and mine cash-cost split, original governance exemption outcome; retrieve original RHP bytes/hash and verify page visuals. Then produce a conservative per-share downside based on legal outcomes, tax, sustaining cash expenditure and dilution rather than assigning ungrounded probabilities.
