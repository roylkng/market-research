# 27 November 2026: convergence and external-method challenge

## Decision
Research quality is improving; incremental return-selection skill is not demonstrated. The 10 November operating review already specified separate hypothesis, analyst, allocation and outcome layers. Do not construct another agent architecture simply to restate that design. The existing main blocker is discovery efficiency and validation of the incremental value of qualitative analysis, not generating more company reports. Keep independent economic judgment and broad discovery, but prioritize discrepancies in expectations and evidence over headline positivity.

## External comparison
BlackRock's published systematic approach converts language data into measurable signals and tests them; Two Sigma combines independent forecasts, costs and risk; Capital Group emphasizes years of firsthand industry/company research and debate; AQR publishes quality/value/momentum-related research. Qlib/RD-Agent offer reusable experiment/data/model infrastructure and agent-assisted R&D. FinRobot offers deterministic valuation operators plus LLM research/reporting. AlphaSense offers evidence-linked retrieval/workflows. These are public descriptions and research/product claims, not a verified independent reproduction or disclosure of proprietary alpha. Trendlyne's own help warns that current constituents are used throughout its index-universe backtest, so do not use that path as a point-in-time historical proof.

Kim/Muhn/Nikolaev's 2024 study reports earnings direction skill on anonymized statements, not a transferable Indian-stock alpha guarantee. FINSABER finds weaker performance for broad long-run LLM trading tests than narrow examples. Dou/Fu's 2026 working paper reports AUC 67.88% for earnings direction with post-model-release outcomes, not 67.88% accuracy or an investor return. The May 2026 Agentic Trading survey finds sparse comparable time splits, cost models and survivorship handling among 19 closed-loop studies. FinSearchComp tests realistic analyst retrieval and reasoning (not returns). Build the narrow task benchmark and the incremental economic outcome test separately, not another unconstrained price-prediction prompt.

NSE's Nifty500 Multifactor MQVLv 50 combines momentum, quality, value and low volatility. Sep30 2026 original fact-sheet text gives launch Feb6 2025 versus base Apr1 2005; 5y TRI CAGR 12.07% includes back-calculated prelaunch history, 1y TRI -5.04%. Neither is a forward expected return nor after-cost investable track record. Screenshot attempt failed; no source bytes or hashes captured. SPIVA India Mid-Year 2026 reports 85% five-year large-cap and 55% mid/small-cap active fund underperformance against respective benchmarks. These are a practical warning, not evidence stock picking is impossible.
