**Sector/customer economics and thesis:** engineered pumps can have installed-base repair/replacement demand and qualification friction, but no independent customer renewal, division contribution or maintained gross margin evidence is in this bounded read. Energy investments could diversify revenue, but until funding, utilization, margins and external customer collections are measurable, do not give a terminal premium. Related-party terms, succession and capital-outcome history require further original checks. Lack of evidence is not an integrity accusation.

**Reverse price hurdle only**, before dividends, dilution or financing complications: at 31.86× TTM reported PAT today, for the stock *price* to appreciate 10% annually for five years with a hypothetical **18×** exit P/E, parent-attributable PAT would need to grow **23.31% per year**. At 20× exit it needs **20.74%**, at 25× **15.47%**. A 10% PAT path with 18× exit implies approximately **−1.9% annual price appreciation**. Not forecasts, not a constant enterprise-value model, and actual owner cash is currently far weaker than the PAT denominator. No standing entry floor is justified by the evidence.

**Decision: WATCH_CASH_CONVERSION_AND_PRICE, not BUY_AT_PRICE.** Evidence capable of reopening at same price includes sustained conversion of invoices to cash, operating-plus-energy unit economics after depreciation and financing, reconciliation of the ambiguous investing uses, stable diluted shares and cash debt servicing. Deterioration alongside a lower share price does not satisfy the condition.

## 2. MPS LTD: excellent headline margin, much less distributable cash

**Reverse cash-price diagnostic, not forecast**: for a five-year 10% *price-only* outcome with a 20× terminal cash multiple, starting at ₹3,668.90cr market equity capitalization, fifth-year attributable distributable cash must reach about **₹295.45cr**. From provisional ₹92.34cr trailing cash that implies ~**26.2% company cash growth** per year. From a hypothetically normalized ₹195.56cr annualization of current H1 selected cash, it implies ~**8.6%**, showing why seasonal collections and historical funding claims decisively govern the investment case. Neither 20× exit nor the cash normalization is underwritten and neither model includes interim distributions, future acquisitions, or share dilution.

**Management/capital allocation:** November results proposed an independent director's second term **subject to shareholder special resolution**. This is not completed reappointment. Existing acquisition and earnout obligations, goodwill, FY26 profitability affected by Liberate adjustments and the operating contrast between research/education/corporate segments are more important than management's headline group EBITDA. No misconduct assertion. The business has embedded publisher workflows, with potential AI productivity gains that might be captured by customers instead of MPS. Need verified client retention, acquisition cohort cash, and margins by segment for a purchase-quality forecast.

**Decision: WATCH_OWNER_CASH_AND_ACQUISITION_ROIC, not BUY_AT_PRICE.** Reopen at the 20 Nov price if original FY26 cash conversion is reconciled and continued collections support a sustainably higher cash run rate with disciplined acquisition outlays and limited dilution. A changed price with intact economics can also reopen immediately. Do not impose an arbitrary calendar wait.
