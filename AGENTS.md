# Repository working instructions

## Assisted investment research

The user's objective is timely, evidence-backed Indian investment decisions and a repeatable research capability. Do not replace that objective with a sequence of infrastructure projects, test counts, generic index recommendations, or unsupported stock tips.

Before continuing investment research, read:

1. `research/integrated/README.md` for the current handoff and unresolved work.
2. `docs/integrated-investment-framework-v1.md` for the full decision framework.
3. `research/integrated/decision-journal.md` and the relevant dated company assessment.
4. The relevant existing operating contract, experiment specification and decision register. In particular, preserve `docs/analyst-fund-manager-operating-system-v1.md`, `docs/investment-approach-review-2026-11-10.md` and all frozen H/PF/HG/AE protocols.

Assess material interactions among market/sector conditions, structural demand, competitive response, leadership, integrity, team depth, stakeholder reputation, capital allocation, financial quality, expectations in the price, timing, liquidity and portfolio alternatives. A full company note must state coverage and the decisive unknowns. It must not imply every factor was verified merely because headings exist.

Leadership claims require evidence of decisions, incentives and outcomes, especially in difficult periods. A biography, award, large order or famous promoter is not a management-quality score. Integrity concerns are non-compensating review gates, not deductions that high growth can offset. Distinguish a source discrepancy from an allegation of wrongdoing.

Keep facts, attributed issuer claims, analyst inferences, scenario assumptions and unresolved questions separate. Preserve source dates, observation dates, reporting scope, units and limitations. Verify changing public facts before calling them current. Never turn prior assistant prose, a source URL, a bibliography or an imported report hash into proof that original bytes were verified.

Do not let stock-specific WAIT judgments silently become an all-market cash recommendation. Compare company exposure with diversified exposure, alternatives and cash, without changing portfolio policy or inventing the user's asset allocation. A past rally is neither proof of future returns nor a reason to ignore broad-market developments.

A historical analogy needs contemporaneous evidence, alternative explanations, an unsuccessful or weakening comparison, and an early observable signal. Famous survivors do not constitute an unbiased backtest. New leadership observations must not modify frozen H003 features, labels or cutoffs.

## Persistent handoff and completion

For each substantive research session, update the relevant tracked case or create a dated new assessment, append material changes to `research/integrated/decision-journal.md`, and update the handoff in `research/integrated/README.md`. Record what changed, why it matters, sources, limitations, current disposition and the next decisive check. A no-change result needs a note only when the verification itself is material. Do not create empty commits.

Preserve old decisions and reasoning. Correct them with a dated superseding entry, not hindsight edits. Read the current branch and open PRs before writing. Use a focused branch/PR and inspect the exact diff. Report the actual commit/PR status. A branch is not a merge, a green extraction run is not semantic validation, and a passing test is not investment alpha.

Publish supported conclusions company by company. A blocked document or local tool must block only the claims depending on it. Do not rerun an entire acquisition workflow just to recover a lost narrative. Do not claim local execution, downloaded bytes, screenshots, tests or hashes unless actually obtained.

These are researcher/agent working instructions, not a new scheduled job or automatic fact-acceptance mechanism. Follow the existing repository's research-only and live-capital restrictions. Do not rewrite frozen experiments, execute broker actions, alter schedules or provision paid services under this documentation update.
