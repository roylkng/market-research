## FDC: final letter resolves a misleading headline premium

**Original signed issuer filing:** FDC NSE letter dated **16 Nov 2026**, including 61-page letter of offer [F-O]. Cover and page 1 visually inspected during 17 Nov source session. Source status: **ORIGINAL_ISSUER_PDF_TEXT_AND_SCREENSHOT_CHECKED**, no original-byte download or SHA custody. Prior buyback board approval on **27 Oct 2026** is historical, not a new authorization.

- Tender **2,400,000 shares × ₹500 = ₹120 crore**, **1.47%** of pre-buyback shares. **Record date 13 Nov 2026 has passed**. Offer opens 19 Nov and closes 26 Nov. Settlements stated by 3 Dec, not assumed executed.
- Letter-of-offer category ratio, approximate before rounding: **small shareholder 4/57 = 7.01827%**, **general category 10/773 = 1.29366%**. Page 1 LOF states precise indicative 1.293754483% in text; use authoritative explicit ratio rather than overprecise calculator result. Small shareholder classification uses **≤537 shares as on record date** under the disclosed ₹2 lakh threshold and ₹372.10 official NSE record-day close in the offer. This does NOT mean that all current owners qualify as small holders.
- **Promoter holding ~69.66% and intends to tender**, so promoter abstention cannot be assumed. Small reserved portion 360,000 shares; general 2,040,000 shares. Final acceptance of extra shares cannot be preclaimed.
- At **16 Nov pre-letter close ₹379.40**, ₹500 is **31.787% nominal premium per accepted share**, but a hypothetical entitled small holder who bought at that *historical* price would capture only **2.231% gross of position value** at 4/57 acceptance. General fraction 10/773 would be **~0.411%** gross. Both are illustrative, not executable trades, and exclude price adjustment, eligibility timing, tender rounding, fees, tax or extra acceptance.
- Cash funding in offer described as internal accrual and/or liquidation of investments, not automatically cash already segregated or proved recurring FCF. A **new purchase after 13 Nov does not convey 13 Nov tender rights**. Gross premium must therefore not be used as an arbitrage signal.
- No independently verified current owner CFO less sustaining capex, obligations, full diluted-share bridge or downside valuation. Standalone valuation remains **WATCH_FUNDAMENTAL_ONLY**, not mechanically attractive because of buyback.

**Decision-changing evidence:** For existing eligible owners, actual registrar entitlement, valid tender and finalized acceptance. For new capital, audited owner cash through cycle, capex, dilution and a new dated close giving defensible downside. Do not conflate the two.
