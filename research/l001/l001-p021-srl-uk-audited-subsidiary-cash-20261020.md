Against this **GBP-denominated** historical purchase consideration:

- £2.060217m of **mixed-period 14-month CFO** is **2.05%** of £100.5m, NOT annual post-acquisition return on invested capital.

**Central accounting trap:** a GBP £100.5m *parent purchase price / indefinite-life intangible* cannot be compared with the subsidiary's UK FRS 102 share-capital book value as if the latter recognized the acquisition price.
