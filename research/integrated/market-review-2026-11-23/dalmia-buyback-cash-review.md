## Primary new catalyst: signed 23 November buyback approval

[Original signed NSE PDF, 23 November](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/DALBHARAT_23112026113131_outcomeBMbb23112026n.pdf), opening text and critical PDF pages visually inspected this session. **No original-byte SHA custody claimed.**

- Up to **₹700 crore** via **open-market stock-exchange purchase**, **at prices not exceeding ₹1,850/share**. This is **not tender** and does not create an entitlement to sell at ₹1,850. The limit is a maximum, not an offer to purchase each ordinary shareholder's position at the cap. Promoters and controlling persons are excluded.
- At full ₹700 crore deployment at the maximum ₹1,850 price, **3,783,783 shares**, about **2.02%** of 187,565,953 existing shares, would be retired. Purchases below the cap may retire more shares for the same outlay. Buyback outlay is **2.38%** of the indicated equity value using the secondary close, not a cash dividend yield. The superficial **18.14% cap-to-close premium** is *not an investable arbitrage return*.
- Required minimum deployment stated in filing: **₹525 crore** (75% of cap), with at least **₹280 crore** (40%) in the first half of the 66-working-day buyback window after opening. The public announcement with implementation timetable is due within two working days. The program is approved, not yet executed. Fees and tax are additional to cap.
- Promoter shares **104,731,548 (55.84%)**, pro-forma **56.99%** if full 3,783,783-share retirement at the cap. Their ownership percentage rises mechanically despite nonparticipation. An open-market buyback can improve continuing owners' per-share claim *if* the paid price is below realizable long-run value after cost of cash/borrowing. That value is not demonstrated here.

## Causal long-term thesis and its countercase

**Positive mechanism (not underwritten):** Cement capacity, distribution density, logistics savings and commissioned acquired assets may improve unit costs and asset utilization if housing/infrastructure paid volumes grow. Buybacks conducted below owner value while funding reliable growth could increase continuing owners' cash/share. Strong ratings and liquid investments could help the financing bridge.

**Countercase:** Regional cement is cyclical and capital-intensive. Competitors may expand capacity ahead of demand. The issuer's Q2 EBITDA/tonne decreased while acquisition borrowing increased. Spending and acquisition integration must generate cash after sustaining assets, debt service, environmental obligations and expansion costs before an open-market buyback can be deemed value creating. Buyback approval alone does not prove owner-aligned management or attractive prospective returns.

**Leadership and stakeholder evidence limits:** Approval of repurchase rather than pro-rata dividend reveals an allocation choice, but neither effective incentives nor customer loyalty, technical team depth, succession, plant-level capital outcome, customer contracts, minority treatment record or supplier terms were fully reviewed. No adverse integrity conclusion is implied from low cash, acquisition or an impairment. Competitive environment and capex over a full cement cycle remain to be underwritten.

## Decision, falsifiers, follow-through

- **PASS_BUYBACK_ARBITRAGE:** no standing purchase entitlement at ₹1,850. Do not buy for an assumed 18.14% spread.
- **WATCH_CAPITAL_ALLOCATION:** company remains a *new, material* capital-return/cyclical research case, without BUY_AT_PRICE or a fictional price target. The most important question is whether the board's ₹525–700cr cash return is funded without impairing liquidity, capacity execution or shareholder returns on its much larger investment base.
- **Upgrade evidence:** subsequent original public announcement, actual exchange buyback execution/average price/shares retired, debt/cash funding, **signed** H1 cash-flow page reconciliation, FY27–FY28 commissioning and acquired-capacity EBITDA per tonne, post-maintenance cash and debt roll-forward, treasury access and diluted shares. An improvement in sustainable cash/share at the same market price could reopen the positive case.
- **Reject/falsify:** continuing weak EBITDA/t while gross debt and finance burden rise, project funding crowds out necessary maintenance, buyback effectively funded by costly new debt with inferior reinvestment returns, or material share dilution offsets the retirement. A lower price with worse underlying economics is not a buy trigger.
- **Publication/measurement:** board outcome before reported 23 Nov BSE close, but the *official* exchange close file was not retained this run. Do not assert execution or measured prospective returns. November 24 is an NSE equity holiday according to already retained original circular. Future outcomes remain null.
- **Portfolio comparison:** cash and diversified indices are independent choices. One cement-sector pass cannot authorize all-market cash or index buying.
