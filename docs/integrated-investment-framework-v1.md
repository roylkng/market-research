# Integrated investment framework v1

Adopted for assisted research: 15 November 2026.
Scope: a decision method and persistent research contract, not a trained return model, a new hypothesis namespace, a complete market scan or permission to deploy capital.

## Objective and relationship to existing work

Find durable business improvement that the offered price does not adequately reflect, before the opportunity becomes obvious, while avoiding permanent loss and accounting for alternatives. A useful outcome can be BUY_CANDIDATE_FOR_POLICY_REVIEW, WAIT_FOR_PRICE_OR_EVIDENCE, PASS_WITH_REASON or EVIDENCE_BLOCKED. These are descriptive research dispositions, not a change to existing programmatic Analyst Decision Object enums or portfolio authority.

This document extends the practical decision-first review and the analyst/fund-manager operating contract. It does not replace them. The existing scientific H-series, portfolio policies, frozen cohorts, point-in-time labels and outcome ledgers remain untouched. Especially, the richer qualitative leadership review is NOT a change to H003's frozen management-delivery experiment.

The object of analysis is the interaction:

    Market and sector conditions
             +
    Customer need and industry structure
             +
    Leadership, integrity and organisational capability
             |
             v
    Durable customer preference and capital allocation
             |
             v
    Incremental after-tax OWNER cash per diluted share
             |
             v
    Expectations already in the offered price
             |
             v
    Downside, timing, liquidity and portfolio alternative
             |
             v
    Dated research decision -> new evidence -> attributed outcomes

No individual factor is sufficient. A growing industry can attract enough supply to destroy returns. A capable CEO can overpay for assets. A good company can be overpriced. A cheap company can have unacceptable governance. A WAIT stock case does not imply avoiding the whole market.

## Ten connected lenses

| Lens | Decision question | Evidence to seek | How it can alter the case |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Market, sector and opportunity cost | What is changing across the investable market, and what alternative are we declining? | Dated broad/sector total returns, breadth, earnings revisions, rates, inflation, oil, FX, liquidity and fund-flow data. Separate price from total returns and observed flows from causal explanations. | Financing costs, discount rates, customer demand, concentration and the cost of waiting. A one-month rise alone decides none of these. |
| 2. Customer need and growth runway | Why must customers spend, when, and where is the actual bottleneck? | Customer budgets, utilization, repeat purchase behaviour, supply lead times, standards and purchasing economics. Distinguish policy announcements from funded demand. | Volume, duration, price elasticity and realistic market penetration. Do not apply total-addressable-market growth directly to company revenue. |
| 3. Competition and value capture | Why will this supplier retain economic profit as demand attracts rivals? | Qualification, switching costs, distribution, technical yields, service, substitution, new capacity, buyer power and the availability of substitutes. | Market share, pricing, gross margin and the duration of excess returns. Distinguish a temporary shortage from a durable advantage. |
| 4. Leadership and strategic judgment | Does management repeatedly choose and execute valuable problems? | A multi-year promise/action/outcome record, product choices, capital commitments, forecast misses, disclosed corrective actions and behaviour in downturns. | Execution range and reinvestment choices. The leader's name or tenure alone receives no valuation premium. |
| 5. Integrity, incentives and minority alignment | Who captures the value, and what happens when honesty is costly? | Auditor observations, related-party terms, pledges, remuneration and its metrics, dilution, capital raising, capital returns, disclosure timing and documented regulatory findings. | May block a purchase until a material concern is resolved. Evidence missing is not proof of misconduct, and integrity cannot be averaged away with growth. |
| 6. Team, culture and succession | Can the institution execute without heroic intervention by one individual? | Second-line accountability, technical/finance continuity, turnover explanations, internal promotions, safety/quality response, delegation and handover milestones. | Key-person risk, operating resilience and capacity to scale. An organisation chart proves named functions, not effective delegation. |
| 7. Stakeholder reputation | Who demonstrably prefers the business and why? | Separate customers, employees, suppliers/lenders/partners and equity investors. Seek renewals, repeat qualification, complaints resolved, staff retention, payment conduct and counterparty corroboration. | Demand durability, acquisition costs, talent, working-capital terms and credibility. Publicity, awards and anonymous reviews are leads, not decisive evidence. |
| 8. Capital allocation and owner economics | What does the next rupee retained earn for each outside shareholder? | Incremental returns through cycles, maintenance/growth capex, acquisition outcomes, recurring after-tax earnings, working capital, leases, restricted cash, minority interests, dilution and funding obligations. | Cash attributable to owners and financial survival. Orders, accounting profit and collected cash remain different. Use sector-appropriate models. |
| 9. Expectations and valuation | What must go right at today's price, and what is genuinely not already reflected? | Verified price/date/security, sustainable cash or earnings, dilution, reverse valuation, cycle/peer evidence and downside/base/upside scenarios. | Entry conditions and required operating milestones. Do not simultaneously assume high growth, peak margins and a premium exit without defending all three. |
| 10. Catalysts, timing and portfolio fit | What changes the decision, when can it be observed, and what can go wrong before then? | Dated approvals, commissioning, collections, customer decisions, succession, liquidity, correlations, event gaps, debt maturities and invalidation triggers. | Research priority and risk limits. A reference close is not an executable fill, and a three-year thesis is not validated by a few good weeks. |

All ten lenses must be addressed in a full case, but depth follows materiality. Each receives SUPPORTED, MIXED, UNKNOWN or ADVERSE_EVIDENCE with an explanation. These are coverage descriptors, not an ordinal score. Identify the three facts most capable of changing the decision and research those first.

## Turn qualitative evidence into falsifiable economics

A qualitative observation should follow this chain:

    Observation -> proposed mechanism -> leading indicator -> financial consequence
                -> counterevidence -> required price or decision change

Examples are hypotheses, not automatic forecasts:

- Repeat qualification by demanding customers could indicate switching friction. Check continuity and defect/return economics before assuming a longer margin advantage.
- Management says it prioritizes returns over size. Compare prior projects' cash returns, acquisitions, rejected bids and capital returned, not the slogan.
- A succession may reduce founder dependence. Look for retained technical/customer capability and clear authority before reducing the key-person risk range.
- A trusted brand could lower customer acquisition costs or support pricing. Verify repeat purchases without subsidies or loose credit before capitalizing that expectation.
- A changed collections policy could improve cash conversion but reduce sales. Model both effects rather than counting only the cash benefit.

Do not double count a leadership or reputation benefit by simultaneously raising revenue growth, margins, terminal duration and the exit multiple for the same observation. Show the incremental change relative to the old case and run the no-premium alternative.

## Leadership, values and promises ledger

For every material management commitment retain the speaker and role as verified at that time, original wording/source, publication and first-observed timestamps, quantitative or qualitative objective, deadline, controllable actions, capital at risk, subsequent result, explanation of any miss and our assessment. Unknown outcomes remain unknown. Preserve the initial promise when guidance changes.

Aim to cover several years including a difficult period, and at least three consequential decisions where source availability permits. Do not manufacture a numeric delivery rate from an opportunistically selected set. Separate skill from commodity prices, cheap funding, regulation, inherited assets, favourable timing and luck. Lack of access to interviews is a limitation, not a reason to invent culture evidence.

Values are tested by costly choices: bad-news disclosure, treatment of customers during defects, safety failures, employee/technical continuity, supplier payment, fair terms for minority owners and willingness to abandon weak projects. A single adverse event does not establish a person's character. Distinguish documented misconduct, unresolved evidence, business failure and allegations.

## Historical mechanisms without survivor bias

Use the retained history study as a source of mechanisms, not stock analogies or estimated win rates. For a proposed mechanism:

1. Define the pre-success decision date and use only evidence then available when reconstructing an investment judgment.
2. Separate the business's subsequent success from the return available at the original valuation.
3. Include a comparable exposed to the same tailwind that failed, stagnated or lost the advantage. Prefer a matched peer to an unrelated cautionary tale.
4. Test alternative explanations such as capital cycle, policy, customer concentration, inherited distribution and access to finance.
5. State the early observable signal and what would have falsified the mechanism before the eventual outcome.
6. Label retrospective work HISTORICAL_MECHANISM_STUDY. Never move known outcomes into prospective validation or relabel seen examples as independent holdouts.

Satyam is a useful integrity counterexample, not a matched economic peer for every successful company. A proper matched historical panel remains unfinished.

## Evidence and uncertainty contract

Every material claim needs: identifier, claim text, fact/issuer assertion/inference/assumption/question type, company/security and accounting scope, source publisher/type/URL, source period, publication date if known, observation date, original-byte capture status and hash only when actually obtained, contradictory sources, verification limits and the decision it affects.

Source status vocabulary:
- SOURCE_TEXT_CHECKED_THIS_SESSION: readable source text inspected, without implying retained original-byte custody or independent confirmation.
- IMPORTED_PRIOR_RESEARCH: a previous artifact or memo carries the claim. It has not been freshly verified merely because the artifact was read.
- INFERENCE or SCENARIO_ASSUMPTION: our reasoning, not a reported fact.
- UNKNOWN or CONFLICTED: explicit unresolved state, not a zero or a completed transaction.

A company page can establish what the company publishes about its team, not prove the team's effectiveness. A customer or vendor can corroborate a relationship while having a commercial interest. A regulatory finding must be described with its legal status, dates and scope.

Current price, index performance, office-holders, contract status, capital changes and market conditions require current verification. When unavailable, publish the old date and limit the affected decision. Never propagate a previous chat's market level, management title, profit figure or entry threshold as freshly audited truth.

## Required decision output

A full assisted case should contain, in this order:

- Dated action, intended horizon, verified price/reference limitations and strongest counter-thesis.
- What the market already appears to expect, what we think differs, and what would show us wrong.
- Coverage of the ten lenses with source and uncertainty labels. Explicit integrity, funding and identity gates.
- A leadership/capital-allocation timeline and stakeholder evidence, with what remains unknown.
- Per-share downside, working and upside economics. State dividends, tax, costs, dilution, terminal assumptions and timing. No unsupported probabilities.
- Price improvement OR evidence improvement that would change the action, not a permanent arbitrary entry number.
- Benchmark/alternative and opportunity cost. Keep core-market participation separate from the satellite stock decision.
- One highest-value next evidence task and the reopen triggers.

Portfolio sizing still requires the relevant portfolio policy and actual user constraints. This framework grants no capital authority. Report supported company conclusions independently rather than withholding all work for one missing filing.

## Research productivity and future evaluation

Keep three scoreboards separate: critical-evidence quality, completed decision productivity, and prospective after-cost investment value. Track false negatives, cash drag and decision reversals caused by source errors as well as successful picks. Preserve failed models and comparison variants. Research priority does not equal expected return.

No new model benchmark, live market scan, automated approval, price alert, daily budget, storage migration or commercial service is created by this document. Data rights, independent labels, reliable inference, sustained source operations and portfolio validation remain distinct requirements.

## Update and resumption protocol

At session start read `research/integrated/README.md`, the journal, current relevant files and open PR state. Work from the existing question instead of restarting old experiments. Resolve the latest material disclosure before using an older valuation.

Before reporting a substantive completed update, persist the dated assessment and its evidence references, append the thesis delta and unresolved issues to the journal, and refresh the handoff. Keep old verdicts and correction history. Cite the real commit or PR state in the user-facing handoff. A material correction must identify what earlier statement or scenario it supersedes.

If a tool fails, persist available research through an available repository path and say exactly what was not verified. Do not claim future background work unless an actual authorized automation exists. Do not delete original evidence or edit frozen research to make a workflow green.
