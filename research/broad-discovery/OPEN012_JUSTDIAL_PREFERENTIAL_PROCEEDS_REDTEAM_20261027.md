- **Just Dial FY2026 audited annual report**, signed issuer report filed 8 August 2026 (original company investor portal https://www.justdial.com/cms/investor-relations/online_reports, company-signed searchable reproduction https://financialfilings.com/filings/just-dial-limited/annual-report/2026/52832446/ , Board Report "Utilisation of funds raised"). It specifically describes funds raised via the 2021 preferential allotment as being temporarily held in mutual funds and fixed deposits **pending intended utilization**, with no deviation from the original offer objects. The auditor says no use **during FY2026** other than temporary investment. **Neither statement alone proves how much of the original ₹2,164.88cr still remains unused on September 30, 2026 or that a specified rupee balance is legally locked.** Original complete offer-object clauses, actual monitoring-agency report and latest accounting need independent acquisition.

This is not merely market sentiment: the controlling shareholder's earlier capital contribution has a stated business use. It could be invested in growth rather than returned to minority holders, even when held in a liquid debt mutual fund today. **Do NOT subtract ₹2,164.88cr as debt or a financial liability.** Equity issuance is already included in the capital and book assets. Its opportunity cost lies in capital allocation, time value and returns on eventual deployment.
