## ACUTAAS economic falsification

The frozen P014 FY26 consolidated cash-flow packet reports CFO Rs 292.1736 crore and PPE expenditure Rs 327.9788 crore, implying CFO less all PPE of negative Rs 35.8052 crore. It does not represent normalized parent-attributable owner cash.

July 24, 2026 earnings-call transcript at https://earningsapi.io/transcripts/acutaas-chemicals-limited_acutaas_earnings_call_transcript_2026-07-24 quotes management as estimating approximately Rs 40–45 crore FY27 maintenance capex, and roughly Rs 50 crore FY27 carried-over electrolyte additive/pilot plant capex, with further land and R&D spending unannounced. These are management statements in a third-party transcript and **not independently audited FY26 maintenance PPE or original issuer transcript bytes**.

A deliberately optimistic historical CFO sensitivity, applying FY27 maintenance guidance to FY26 CFO while excluding other reinvestment obligations:

| Assumed maintenance PPE | FY26 historical CFO minus this amount (Rs cr) |
| --- | ---: |
| 40 | 252.17 |
| 45 | 247.17 |
| Actual FY26 all PPE 327.9788 | -35.81 |

None represents owner FCF. Mixing FY26 cash generation and FY27 prospective guidance is scenario arithmetic, not an actual comparable result.

Frozen October 15 illustrative *basic* market cap Rs 25,646.15 crore. At 16x annual attributable owner cash, value parity mathematically requires Rs 1,602.88 crore annually and a hypothetical +50% equity valuation requires Rs 2,404.33 crore, holding shares, claims and balance sheet unchanged. The 50% hurdle is about 9.73 times the optimistic Rs 247.17 crore sensitivity. This is not a forecast that cash will grow 9.73x.

The material inference is **cash spending classification changes FCF interpretation, but even a generous maintenance-only proxy leaves a very large valuation hurdle**. Neither high projected growth nor a cash-flow proxy suffices to claim mispricing.
