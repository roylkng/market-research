# SEAMEC: wait at the 9 November 2026 reference price

## Decision

**No fresh purchase at the INR 1,526.60 official NSE close on 9 November 2026.** Retain SEAMEC for price-sensitive research. This is an assisted underwriting judgment, not a sell instruction for an existing holding or a claim about the next price move.

Fleet growth and contracted work are real positives. However, the reported 11.6x trailing multiple includes nonrecurring profit, working-capital release supports cash flow, and the net-cash snapshot predates major fleet investment. The conservative case does not meet the working five-year 15% annual price-return hurdle.

EPS itself is not added, and the half-year is not annualized. Cross-document amounts are not independently restatement-reconciled.

## Earnings quality

Q2 FY27 pretax profit is **INR 48.58 crore = INR 6.15 crore before exceptional items + INR 42.43 crore exceptional gain**. The gain is 87.3% of pretax profit. The results note attributes it to disposal of Seamec Gallant for INR 88.31 crore net of commission.

The INR 6.15 crore still includes INR 23.72 crore other income. Excluding both produces a **negative INR 17.57 crore pretax diagnostic**, not normalized after-tax owner profit. Ordinary maintenance is not automatically added back. One seasonal quarter is not annualized.

| Trailing-earnings sensitivity | Reference EPS | Reference P/E |
|---|---:|---:|
| Remove vessel-sale gain only | INR 114.93-119.10 | 12.82-13.28x |
| Remove gain and all other income | INR 90.80-101.00 | 15.11-16.81x |

The ranges assume 0% or 25% tax benefit on removed pretax amounts and full parent attribution. These are assumptions, not the actual transaction tax. The November presentation identifies Gallant as owned by Seamec International FZE, and the audited investment note identifies that entity as wholly owned. The all-other-income case is deliberately stricter, because interest income can recur. It is not a reconciled parent-level normalization correction. The annual policy uses tonnage-based taxation for shipping income, not a universal 25% tax rate.

FY26 operating revenue is INR 952.46 crore, rather than a revenue measure including other income. Operating EBITDA excluding other income is INR 399.67 crore in FY26, INR 104.72 crore in H1 FY26 and INR 162.18 crore in H1 FY27. H1 operating revenue grew 60.6% and EBITDA grew 54.9%, while the operating margin moved from 34.0% to 32.8%. The growth case exists, but reported profit exaggerates its repeatable component.

## Cash conversion and unresolved source differences

H1 operating cash is INR 303.70 crore. Inventory, receivables/prepayments, payables and provisions contribute INR 139.68 crore. Before those movements, the cash diagnostic is INR 164.02 crore. Specified cash PPE purchases are INR 154.27 crore, financing cash interest INR 8.14 crore and lease payments INR 0.99 crore. The residual is INR 140.30 crore as reported, but **INR 0.62 crore before those working-capital movements**. This is not normalized maintenance free cash flow because purchases include growth spending.

Two discrepancies remain visible rather than being silently repaired:

1. The FY26 audited cash-flow PDF, page 171 / printed page 169, reports **INR 3.6804 crore lease payments**. The FY26 XML tags zero. This is a separate period from H1 FY27, where both sources support INR 0.99 crore. Annual lease-adjusted normalized owner cash is not claimed.
2. The Q2/H1 results note reports **INR 42.43 crore** exceptional sale profit, while the H1 cash-flow statement deducts **INR 27.31 crore** as gain on fixed assets. The **INR 15.12 crore** difference is not reconciled here.

These are source-reconciliation questions, not allegations of wrongdoing.

## Funding changes the valuation context

The September presentation's INR 227.40 crore net-cash snapshot predates Anant's takeover on 14 October and on-hire status on 21 October. The disclosed acquisition programme was approximately USD 70 million. The related-party charter to HAL is approximately USD 55 million over 1,225 days, excluding GST, and is subject to HAL's ONGC charter. Gross charter value is not lifetime investment return. Costs, future deployment, residual value, dry-docking and debt service matter.

CRISIL's 13 October assessment reaffirmed A+/Stable and A1 but expected fleet expansion to move the balance sheet from net cash to net debt. That is the agency's forward assessment, not a realized balance. Annual-report bank-deposit notes also identify pledged deposits. This case adds no separate surplus-cash bonus to an earnings valuation already containing financial-asset income.

Dividends, investor taxes, transaction costs, slippage and future dilution are excluded. No scenario probability is assigned.

The starting EPS assumption is informed by the earnings sensitivities. Growth and multiples are underwriting assumptions, not calibrated predictions. The stress case has 47.6% terminal-price downside, not a maximum possible loss. With 12% growth and a 12x exit, the reference price requires about **INR 145.19 of sustainable starting EPS** to earn 15% annually.

**Reconsider around INR 1,050 under the conservative case, or when recurring earnings and financing evidence justify a stronger case.** The more generous INR 115 starting EPS / 15% growth case yields an entry threshold of INR 1,380. These are conditional research thresholds, not certified intrinsic values or automatic buy prices.

## The operational update does not establish the stock's value

The 7 November PRP-X award specifies USD 26.14 million for SEAMEC's own scope, excluding GST, although the legal recipient is a consortium. The 8 November filing confirms Princess departed Mumbai at 07:06 to commence the project. This updates mobilization, not acceptance of completed work, recognized profit or collected cash.

At hypothetical INR 85-100/USD, a 10% after-tax cash margin, twelve-month receipt, 15% discounting, full baseline incrementality and no additional funding, finite cash value is INR 19.32-22.73 crore. That is about **INR 7.60-8.94 per reference share**, or **0.50-0.59% of reference equity value**. The award is useful operating work, but does not by itself justify a major equity rerating. It receives no recurring earnings multiple and is not added twice if already included in a base case.

## Evidence that would change the decision

Post-Anant debt and available cash, repeatable per-share earnings after finance and maintenance costs, accepted billable deployment and cash collections, both source reconciliations, and a qualifying price with dilution/liquidity rechecked. The bullish case needs stronger evidence. An asset-sale gain cannot establish it.
