## 4. APL Apollo: the transaction changes per-share economics

The original APL investor presentation, **PDF page 9, printed slide 8**, discloses **Rs28,000cr consideration**, **24.8x FY27 projected EBITDA**, and expected initial borrowing of **Rs280bn**, followed by planned long-term capitalization. The consideration divided by the stated multiple implies approximately Rs1,129cr projected acquired-business EBITDA, or a 4.03% EBITDA yield before financing, tax and reinvestment. This is derived arithmetic from management's projected figure, not independently validated recurring cash.

Tata's separate Rs15,510.6cr promoter-share purchase pays the seller. It does not fund APL's asset acquisition. The additional 26% open offer at Rs2,301.40 is conditional and limited in size, so it is not a guaranteed exit for every share. The announcement's expanded capital includes possible SAR issuance. Its 26.38% stake denominator differs from the release's approximately 26.6% current-capital figure.

**Disposition:** focused transaction underwriting. The decisive evidence is acquired cash generation, final financing and capitalization, any dilution, and resulting cash per existing shareholder. Do not assume the debt remains permanent or that unspecified future equity is free. The Detailed Public Statement is due on or before 1 December according to the original announcement. No BUY assessment or acceptance-probability estimate was established.

Primary sources: [APL presentation](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/APLAPOLLO_23112026081111_Investor_Presentation.pdf), [open-offer announcement](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/APLAPOLLO_23112026083014_Open.pdf), [Tata release](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/NIDHIFADNAVIS_23112026082123_BSENSEPRIR.pdf).
