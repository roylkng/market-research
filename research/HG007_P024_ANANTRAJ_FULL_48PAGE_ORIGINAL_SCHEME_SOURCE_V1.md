## Missing economic proof

but does not in itself quantify its value.

Independent page-level analysis must verify:

- specific transferred cloud/data-centre assets, property, licences,
  intellectual property, intercompany loans and receivables;
- which contingent liabilities, borrowings, guarantees, leases,
  employee benefits and taxes pass to Ashok Cloud;
- any allocation rule where real estate and cloud assets/debt overlap;
- actual legal effective date, future record-date issued shares,
  parent-retained newco crossholding and share class rights;
- cancellation, consolidation and intercompany capital eliminations;
- actual carve-out earnings, power/capex and operating free cash flow
  rather than generic management growth assumptions;
- exact court, shareholder, creditor and exchange approval state.

A catch-all liability transfer clause is not a balance sheet and
cannot be used as a quantitative cash/debt deduction.
