## First-principles result

The already-researched PNCINFRA FY26 **₹4,592.97cr consolidated after-tax CFO** was extensively explained in OPEN011 (merged PR #548) by **₹4,146.10cr cash release from trade receivables** and project monetisation. A recently signed issuer statement now confirms the precise danger of mistaking that large FY26 CFO for indefinitely repeatable owner cash:

**H1 FY27 group after-tax CFO is ₹−377.7677 crore**, while **H1 FY26 group CFO had been ₹+4,189.0558cr**. The 2025 comparator is itself heavily affected by asset/receivable unwinding, so the giant YoY swing is *not* a newly measured recurring deterioration rate. Critically, **parent standalone CFO improved from −₹404.3093cr to −₹127.6028cr**, yet is also negative. Both group and parent are burning operating cash this half-year. The reported Q2 consolidated profit fell **46.83% YoY**.

This is a material accounting/thesis **falsification event**, not a verified bargain at low P/E, P/B or market cap.
