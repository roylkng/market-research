## Operating hypothesis

Assume earnings stabilize around Q2 while already commissioned ACN and DME capacities ramp gradually. Reported consolidated volume fell year on year, so the revenue increase is not evidence of broad volume-led growth. The base retains Q2 operating margin rather than reverting to higher Q1 profitability or booking projects scheduled for later quarters.

EBIT is after depreciation and excludes other income. Owner PAT = (revenue × EBIT margin + other income − finance costs) × (1 − tax rate) − NCI. Associates and new exceptional items are zero assumptions. Scenarios are uncalibrated sensitivities with no assigned probabilities.

- **Other Income:** 4.5 cr near current Q2.
- **Finance Cost:** 1.6 cr versus Q2 1.3425 cr, allowing incremental working capital and ramp-up.

## Normalization and source corrections

Reported values retained. No separately reported exceptional item. Q1 includes earlier-year tax expense 0.4851 cr. Prior Q3 contains a 0.0832 cr group earlier-year tax benefit but parent standalone benefit is 0.0915 cr, so exact owner normalization is not imposed without subsidiary attribution. These are disclosed limitations, not assumed recurring charges.

Current owner PAT matches original extraction. Prior Q3 signed owner 31.4330 cr equals group 30.7569 cr less NCI(-0.6759 cr). Published EPS9.49 appears to use group PAT. The explicit owner row controls, and no earnings figure is reverse-engineered from EPS.

## Decisive unknowns

- DME customer acquisition and utilization timing are not quantified. Nameplate capacity is not sales.
- Unit-I brownfield completion is guided for end-FY27 and Unit-II for Q4FY27. No Q3 earnings uplift from those future completions is assumed.
- Product-level realizations, spreads, consolidated volume scope and exact segment mix are not sufficiently disclosed to forecast prices directly.
- Current board outcome records a SEBI warning-cum-advisory. Its merits were not investigated here. This is not a clean-governance conclusion.
