The issuer reports that on 9 October Vibhav Energy Private Limited (VEPL)
converted ₹150 crore of existing ICD liabilities into ₹10-face-value
ordinary equity securities:
- INOXGREEN: ₹50 crore existing ICD converted to 5 crore new VEPL shares.
- Authum: ₹100 crore existing ICD converted to 10 crore new VEPL shares.
- VEPL total paid-up equity rose from ₹250.01 crore to ₹400.01 crore.
- After both allotments, INOXGREEN owns 75% of VEPL and Authum 25%.
- VEPL has ceased to be wholly owned by Inox Green, but remains its
  subsidiary.
- Existing financial exposure was capitalized noncash. This does
  **not** represent ₹150 crore of new acquisition liquidity.

**Chronology:** the October 7 WWIL disclosure described conversions as
proposed, optional/future and documents the ₹550 crore payment while the
O&M business transfer remains conditional on the executed BTA. The new
October 9 issuer filing provides a *later* equity conversion milestone.
It cannot retrospectively convert October 7 proposed terms into actual
October 7 shares. The published news index shows the 9 October notice
around 23:43 IST, after NSE cash-market close. The source collector
does not independently attest that exchange broadcast time; treat
publication-time use as requiring a separately sourced timestamp.

Authum's own 10 October filing corroborates its ₹100 crore ICD conversion
and identifies additional consortium financing of WWIL assets. This
latter ₹350 crore financing must NOT be added to VEPL's original
₹550 crore O&M purchase to synthesize a fabricated ₹900 crore price;
the underlying asset/transaction boundaries differ.
