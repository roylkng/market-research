# N R Agarwal Industries: fund the new plant before capitalizing the earnings rebound

**Research date: 19 November 2026. Disposition: PASS_CURRENT_PRICE, with a conditional project and price watch.** Reference: official NSE 18 November close **₹618.80**, NRAIL / EQ / ISIN INE740D01017. Intended horizon is approximately five years. This supersedes the *unfinished-source status* of the 17 November preliminary review, without changing that historical judgment.

The main question is now answered: the original cash statements are available, and the earnings rebound has not yet created substantial post-investment owner cash. The next large project makes a simple 8× doubled-half-year P/E misleading. Our working project-and-funding model gives about **4.2% annual return at the reference price**, versus roughly **−20.1% / +25.1%** in downside / upside scenarios. These are explicit, uncalibrated scenarios, not return probabilities or price forecasts.

The strongest counterargument is credible: the new plant could operate earlier and earn much more than our conservative first-year contribution. At the same price, about **₹262cr new-plant EBITDA in year five**, instead of ₹180cr, reaches a modeled 12% return with other working assumptions fixed. Evidence supporting that outcome could reopen the case without a price fall.

## What was actually verified

Original signed H1 results, FY26 annual accounts, prior-H1 cash, September shareholding, July ICRA rationale and September AGM voting results were downloaded. Decisive cash, balance-sheet, lease and share-encumbrance pages were visually inspected. The official November 18 price ZIP and CSV hashes were independently rechecked against the retained source, then joined on symbol, ISIN and EQ series. This is a completed bounded underwriting review, not a forensic audit or verified customer interview programme.

Source financial statements use **₹lakh**, converted to ₹crore by dividing by 100. Accounting scope is standalone, the operative issuer scope. The company reported no subsidiary, associate or JV for FY26. [S1–S7]

## The decisive capital-allocation fact

The disclosed Unit VI expansion is approximately **₹1,500cr**, greater than today's equity capitalization. The July rating rationale describes roughly ₹227cr spent in FY26 and a further ₹23cr envisaged in FY27. September CWIP is ₹252.14cr. Our working model rounds the remaining project spending to ₹1,250cr. It does not treat the already-spent amount as a second new cash outflow. [S1,S3,S7]

The evidence does **not** justify treating the entire project as financed. ICRA's July view was that near-term liquidity was adequate, with approximately ₹62cr FY27 and ₹97cr FY28 repayments. The FY26 accounts show ₹64.68cr current term-plus-vehicle maturities at March. These are different scopes. ICRA's rated bank facilities of ₹1,137.57cr are not a sanction for all future project borrowing. The 2 September AGM approved borrowing and security authority up to ₹5,000cr, but shareholder authority is not a bank commitment. [S3,S7,S8]

The annual narrative itself gives **December 2029** on printed page 10 and **late 2030** on page 11. We retain both and give no meaningful new-plant EBITDA to the working case until model year five. Management's no-dilution intention and approximately ₹1,000cr future **long-term** debt ambition are issuer claims. They cannot be substituted for future total debt including working capital. The model computes its own financing requirement. [S3]

**Interest-budget uncertainty is material and explicitly tested.** The issuer has not supplied an itemized future project budget showing capitalized interest. Working ₹1,250cr is an assumption for future *non-interest* project cash, while borrowing interest is charged separately. If the headline budget already contains ₹150cr of future interest or other overlap, reducing non-interest spend to ₹1,100cr raises the working return only to **7.0%** and 12% present value to **₹493**, versus ₹433. This sensitivity avoids silently asserting that the whole headline budget is non-interest spending.

Debt rolls forward from actual September gross borrowings. Cash interest is 9% of average opening/closing borrowing and is solved together with debt. Taxes use positive post-lease EBITDA less physical depreciation and modeled cash interest. Cash interest is treated as deductible in this normalized calculation, which is not a statutory tax-return forecast. ₹2/share annual dividends are assumed, not promised. Surplus operating cash reduces debt, it is not also distributed as free cash. Funding availability and refinancing are assumptions. A financing failure can be worse than the modeled downside.

Terminal value is post-lease EBITDA × an explicit enterprise multiple minus resulting gross debt plus credited cash/current investments. The current equity portfolio is credited at 75% of September carrying value, cash at face value, total **₹16.02cr**. Noncurrent investments and other bank balances receive no extra value. Leases are already charged as cash rent and are not deducted again as terminal debt. Existing CWIP and the finished project are not added again to enterprise value.

The exit multiples are scenario choices, not independently established fair market multiples. The range deliberately does not award both a high-growth narrative and a premium rerating. At the same price, fixed-other-assumptions new-plant Y5 EBITDA thresholds are **₹239cr / ₹262cr / ₹301cr** for 10% / 12% / 15% returns. This one-factor test holds working capital and depreciation constant, so it is best read as a margin sensitivity. Higher volumes would generally require additional working capital and possibly maintenance spending.

A conditional research price around **₹430–475** corresponds to 12–10% working returns. It is not a standing order, price floor, personal hurdle or substitute for satisfactory project funding. A falling price accompanied by weaker margins requires remodeling.

## Management, integrity and stakeholder assessment

The material history is mixed. The prior large expansion was commissioned in 2024 and debottlenecked again in January 2026, followed by stronger FY26/H1 FY27 sales and operating cash. That is evidence of production execution. The old Unit IV was closed in July 2025 with an asset-discard loss. The next project is much larger and commits capital before the last expansion has demonstrated sustained debt reduction. Favorable paperboard demand alone cannot establish adequate incremental returns. [S1,S3,S4,S7]

The September shareholding statement reports **no pledge**, but **12,516,998 shares under non-disposal undertaking**, 99% of promoter holdings and 73.55% of total shares. This is disclosed financing encumbrance, not the same legal instrument as a pledged share and not a misconduct finding. The annual borrowing notes separately describe promoter undertakings and guarantees. [S3,S5]

FY26 key-management compensation was ₹8.975cr, versus total dividends ₹3.404cr. It is already expensed and must not be deducted twice. Related-party salary loans, director funding, rent and product dealings are disclosed. An unmodified audit and no reported lender default provide bounded reassurance, not forensic clearance. No independent economic review of related-party pricing or complete regulatory-history search was performed. [S3]

New operations/project leadership in October and a chief procurement officer effective 16 November add named functions. Their appointment documents establish roles and issuer-described experience, not demonstrated execution or succession quality. [S9,S10]

## Highest-value next evidence and reopening rules

**Next decisive item: the executed Unit VI financing and cash-spend plan**, including debt amount/rate/maturity, interest during construction, promoter/equity contribution, true commercial-start date and the assumed operating working capital. A large borrowing authorization is insufficient.

Reopen at the same price if this plan and subsequent operating evidence support the roughly ₹262cr new-plant Y5 EBITDA / funding combination needed for a 12% modeled return, or a materially better earlier-ramp case. Review FY27 original cash for actual debt reduction and inventory/receivable discipline. Deterioration means rebuild the case, not preserve the old threshold. If supplier-finance acceptance balances contract, charge the cash use once. FY26 disclosed ₹95.26cr acceptances in trade payables. A ₹50cr one-time runoff sensitivity lowers working 12% present value to **₹410** and modeled annual return to **3.1%**, without also adding the same amount to initial debt. [S3]

No fresh universal return hurdle, all-market cash allocation or live capital permission follows from this decision. Diversified equity exposure remains a separate portfolio-policy question. The completed case should now leave the generic evidence queue and reopen only for material price, funding or operating information.
