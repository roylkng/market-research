## Frozen H004 status remains unchanged

H004 Stage 1 predicts the *conditions that may precede repricing* using earnings inflection, turnaround or a quantified material catalyst. Stock-level momentum cannot create Stage 1.

Stage 2 is an execution confirmation only and requires at least two frozen recognition conditions. No threshold is changed in this brief.

## Market regime

The Sep-8 market is adverse: Indian benchmarks were lower during the morning while Brent crude was around $98/bbl amid Middle East risk. India imports about 85% of its crude. Most sectors were weak, while defence had relative support following roughly ₹1.10 lakh crore of DAC approvals.

This regime increases the information content of positive company-specific relative strength but increases false-breakout risk. It can help tanker/shipping economics and hurt crude/feedstock-sensitive manufacturers.
