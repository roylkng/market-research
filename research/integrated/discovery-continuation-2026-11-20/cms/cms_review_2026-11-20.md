# CMS Info Systems: current-price candidate, cash budget decides

**Decision date: 20 November 2026. Official reference: ₹204.60 on 19 November, NSE CMSINFO / EQ / INE925R01014. Five-year research horizon. Disposition: WATCH_CASH_CONVERSION, now a valuation-complete current-price conditional candidate.** This supersedes the *preliminary* status of the 18 November review while preserving its original record. Research valuation is complete. The recurring cash-conversion assumption remains material to investment admission. No broker instruction or portfolio sizing is authorized.

The strongest case for buying is now concrete: normalization to roughly ₹200cr annual cash investment supports **10.82–12.51% annualized working returns** at the current price, with cash taxes, all lease payments, working capital, dilution and retained cash included. This does not need the issuer's much lower ₹100–125cr FY27 capex guidance. FY25 already produced ₹265.97cr after selected investment and financing uses, so a ₹263cr first-year owner-cash scenario is not an unprecedented outcome. [S1, S2, MODEL]

With ₹250cr recurring cash investment growing with sales, the working return falls to **6.13%**. A credible joint stress gives **−1.07%**, even without financial distress. A low reported earnings multiple does not settle this question. These are conditional outcomes, not probabilities or forecasts. [MODEL]

## 3. Cash earnings, treasury and share count

H1 statutory EBITDA excluding all other income is **₹340.842cr**, calculated from PBT plus finance cost and depreciation, less other income. The cash-flow statement subtracts ₹8.992cr for stock-option compensation. Treating that negative adjustment as a nonrecurring credit gives **₹331.850cr**, or **25.84% of revenue**, before future share-based compensation. The gross H1 expense/reversal schedule is not disclosed, so the label of a reversal is an analytical interpretation of the signed adjustment. Annual disclosures establish that option lapses and reversals have occurred. Future dilution is modeled instead of a second cash charge for the same awards. [S1, S2 note39, MODEL]

Annual ATM useful life changed from seven to ten years effective **1 January 2026**, reducing FY26 depreciation by ₹4.765cr. That is the original note's date, which is more precise than the call's informal Q2/Q3 recollection. Our cash-investment forecast replaces accounting depreciation for valuation. Tax depreciation is a separate approximation. H1 deferred-tax credits are not treated as collected cash. [S2 note4, S4 printed17–18]

September cash ₹59.102cr plus current investments ₹205.309cr plus noncurrent investments ₹32.208cr total **₹296.619cr**. The model reserves ₹100cr for operating liquidity, credits ₹196.619cr as opening excess treasury and assumes zero treasury yield. It excludes other bank balances, restricted deposits, claims and customer loans. The presentation's ₹473cr net-cash definition is not substituted for this balance-sheet bridge. Charging all future lease principal and interest means the ₹267.405cr lease balance is not subtracted again. [S1, S2 notes6–8,13, S3, MODEL]

Original September AGM voting gives **160,063,415 issued shares**, consistent with September reported equity capital. Bounded searches through 20 November did not establish a later allotment. This is a dated denominator, not a certified absence-of-change proof. The March option schedule has 6,865,750 options at weighted exercise ₹308 and 291,249 at ₹198. A deliberately broad gross-exercise sensitivity adds all these shares and their exercise proceeds without pretending they all remain unexercised in November. The June tender retirement of 4,939,126 shares at ₹340 and related cash use are already reflected in September balances. H1 buyback cash including transaction costs was ₹169.988cr, alongside ₹40.016cr dividends. No new purchaser receives a retrospective buyback gain. [S1, S2]

## 6. Decision and next check

CMS deserves a place among the strongest **current-price conditional candidates**. It is no longer an unfinished retrieval task or a reverse-P/E sketch. The present evidence supports a plausible cash recovery and manageable financing. It does not yet establish which side of the economically consequential ₹200–250cr annual cash-investment range the business will occupy, especially once customer financing and collections are combined.

**Highest-value next evidence:** one issuer-origin cash schedule connecting the ₹149.442cr H1 PPE/intangible outflow to FSS settlement, capital advances, capital-creditor payments and current projects, alongside the old-receivable to customer-loan collection roll-forward. An existing disclosure, published IR clarification, FSS closing update or next detailed cash statement can settle this. Two arbitrary future quarters are not required.

**Same-price promotion route:** evidence supports approximately₹200cr all-in recurring investment, full leases near₹110cr, cash operating margin near25.5%, and moderate incremental working capital without repeated customer rescues. That would justify BUY_CANDIDATE_FOR_POLICY_REVIEW at a comparable price, subject to the existing research-only operating contract. It is not contingent on attaining15%.

**Price route:** a lower reference price improves the same frozen economics. It cannot rescue deteriorating collection quality. **Invalidation:** recurring investment approaches₹250–300cr without proportionate cash operating improvement, net customer financing repeatedly expands, old collectible balances age further, or fixed-fee margins fail to absorb wages/fuel and lease costs. Record the resulting reversal without rewriting this note.
