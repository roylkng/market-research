Because the subsidiary reporting period **starts before Sun TV gained control**, these figures are not automatically post-acquisition, fully annualized consolidated contribution. PAT is not operating cash, free cash, incremental owner cash or a franchise ROIC numerator.

Original consolidated Note 40(B), PDF page 226, separately reports the franchise indefinite-life right at **₹1,248.27 crore carrying value**, management discounted cash-flow impairment test using **9% discount rate**, and **no recognized impairment** at March 2026. A management impairment conclusion is not independent investment valuation, cash return or a stock-price floor.

### Transparent reverse cash hurdles, never predictions

P019 independently establishes initial franchise intangible **₹1,190.88 crore** from Note 5, versus separately disclosed GBP 100.5 million deal price equivalent **₹1,184.99 crore**. Exact ₹5.89 crore differences in valuation/transaction cash remain unreconciled. Historical subsidiary reporting-period PAT to initial franchise asset cost is **1.3713%**. Because numerator and denominator have different accounting and ownership clocks, this is *not* cash yield, true annualized ROIC or an annual earnings multiple that can be used as a stock target.

The following flat **annual owner-cash** amounts would be necessary merely to achieve selected *hypothetical* cash-yield hurdles on original historical acquisition cost, if annual owner cash could actually be identified:

| Hypothetical annual cash yield on acquisition cost | Annual owner cash required, ₹ crore |
|---:|---:|
| 5% | 59.54 |
| 9% | 107.18 |
| 12% | 142.91 |

The middle 9% is chosen for transparent comparison with the issuer's separate DCF discount-rate assumption, **not** because impairment analysis proved 9% is the required investor return or because franchise cash lasts perpetually. For scale only, ₹107.18 crore required annual cash is **6.56 times the reported-period ₹16.33 crore PAT**, which cannot be interpreted as a forecast growth rate. No period-specific post-acquisition cash flow is known.

### Economic proof still missing

1. Original month-by-month post-July28 earnings, actual operating cash and franchise financial distributions, not the subsidiary's mixed ownership reporting period.
2. The exact bank/consolidation schedule mapping initial ₹1,190.88 crore acquisition cash into group intangibles investing cash. Avoid adding the same deal twice.
3. Franchise rights fees, broadcasting/media rights, sponsorship, league payments, taxation, sustaining/replacement cash obligations, working capital and FX.
4. Independent downside appraisal of the issuer's 9% impairment valuation, original forecasts and rights life/terminal assumptions.
5. Recurring group content-rights spending and unrestricted parent available cash, excluding incidental investment activity and minority claims.
6. Current diluted parent share count, dated NSE quote, rights/dividend basis and actual execution costs.

P019's conditional **₹778.30 crore FY26 group cash proxy** remains a conditional source-classification sensitivity, not normalized owner FCF. Neither a headline nonimpairment nor a low observed-period profit ratio makes the parent equity cheap or expensive without the underlying post-acquisition cash and current market valuation.
