## WABAG: Yanbu is a contract-finality and risk-budget test

**Issuer events and clocks:**
1. 6 Sep 2024: first Yanbu 300 MLD contract/order announcement, about USD317m / INR2,700 crore [W1]. The issuer's 17 Dec 2024 letter (archived issuer copy on third-party mirror) reports the Saudi customer's 16 Dec cancellation for internal procedure [W2]. This is evidence of project/tender risk, not a substantiated allegation of wrongdoing by WABAG.
2. 24 Jul 2025: new letter of award for the same 300 MLD Saudi Water Authority project at USD272m / headline INR2,332 crore; issuer said contract formalities/effective commencement still to follow [W3].
3. 13 Aug 2025 results call, disclosed 20 Aug 2025: management explicitly reconciled INR2,332 crore inclusive of VAT and **INR2,038 crore excluding VAT** as the comparable sales/order number [W4]. Management also expressed confident expectation of receiving/re-retaining Yanbu. Treat confidence as guidance/hypothesis, not a completed contract or fraud.
4. 5 Nov 2026 issuer H1 FY27 presentation: INR202,071 million = INR20,207.1 crore total order backlog as at 30 Sep, **listing Yanbu among key backlog projects** but not giving a project-specific 30 Sep remaining balance [W5].
5. On the **6 Nov 2026** earnings call, transcript published 16 Nov, DMD stated basic engineering was substantially ready and long-lead equipment procurement initiated **ahead of formal commencement** [W6]. Degree of binding purchase obligation, prepayment, cost recognition and recoverability not disclosed.
6. 14 Nov 2026 exchange filing: Saudi Water Authority notified all bidders via Etimad that **the tender was cancelled for the customer's internal reasons**. Company stated it had signed/submitted contract for customer countersignature and formal commencement. No quantified economic impact or compensation claim is supplied [W7].

**Reconciliation:** The simplistic headline INR2,332 / INR20,207.1 = 11.54% is *not like-for-like*, because the earlier issuer call says INR2,332 includes VAT. Using the issuer-mentioned INR2,038 **ex-VAT** gives **10.09% of the 30 Sep book** if *all* of the INR2,038 was still inside it. The arithmetic remainder would be INR18,169.1 crore, **not a newly published company backlog**. Partial revenue recognition, FX effects or project-specific revisions may change the exposure. Do not treat the total contract value as an immediate loss or zero-recovery impairment. Client-side reasons must not be turned into allegations of WABAG wrongdoing.

**Judgment:** Robust sector demand, some earnings/cash progress, and other outstanding projects do not offset uncertainty about conversion of this particular named backlog item and already-initiated pre-commencement costs. More significant than the 16 Nov price fall (INR2,040.10 to INR1,894.40, -7.14%) is decision quality: what management treated as order-backed and why commitment was incurred before a customer countersignature, especially after a prior cancellation. Do not assign an integrity verdict from these public sources. **WATCH_CONTRACT_LOSS_AND_COST_EXPOSURE; no fresh purchase.**

**Reopen on:** (i) original issuer quantified new order book, net-of-VAT Yanbu outstanding amount, (ii) irreversible procurement/cancelled POs/prepayments and contract damages/claims, (iii) IAS/Ind-AS accounting: costs capitalized, provisions, impairment and recoveries, (iv) evidence of new retender/tender resolution, (v) governance proof bid and commitment controls are enforced, (vi) independently refreshed diluted share/owner cash/valuation at dated price.

## TD Power: strong progress, price/share count require discipline

**Original 12 Nov 2026 result** reports consolidated six-month Sep 2026 revenue INR1,481.3727 crore vs INR824.3639 crore year ago; PAT INR210.0397 crore vs INR110.2668 crore. FY26 consolidated PAT INR238.7749 crore. Arithmetic TTM PAT (FY26 less H1 FY26 plus H1 FY27) ≈ **INR338.5478 crore**, subject to issuer accounting comparability and minority interests [T1].

**Share denominator:** AGM-approved two-for-one subdivision completed around the 24 Aug 2026 record date. Old pre-split 18 Aug price high INR1,597.70 must be divided by two to INR798.85 before comparing to INR635.45 on 16 Nov 2026: drawdown **from that particular August high** ≈ **-20.45%**, not -60.2%. This is NOT the stock's peak-to-current 52-week drawdown: official NSE records a later post-split high of **INR856.10 on 5 Oct 2026**, from which the 16 Nov close is **-25.77%**. Issuer discloses September preferential INR75 crore / 1,250,000 new shares at INR600 and October QIP INR600 crore / 7,741,935 new shares at INR775. After QIP total shares **321,448,795** [T1, T2]. The September balance sheet does *not* yet include October QIP cash.

At the 16 Nov close, post-QIP share count implies market capitalization ≈ **INR20,426.46 crore**; dividing by mechanical TTM consolidated PAT gives approximate **60.34× trailing PAT**. This is a valuation illustration, not fair value. To sustain the observed price at 40× annual group PAT would require ≈INR510.66 crore annual PAT, or ≈51% above the current TTM arithmetic; at 35× it would require ≈INR583.61 crore, or ≈72% above. The business may achieve higher future profit, but that growth is already part of what must be underwritten, not an automatic margin of safety.

H1 group cash from operations ≈INR211.74 crore, simple PP&E/CWIP outlay ≈INR46.10 crore. However trade receivables grew ≈INR209.65 crore, inventory ≈INR96.27 crore, other receivables/assets ≈INR78.70 crore while 'other payable, provision & current/noncurrent liabilities' supplied ≈INR297.65 crore. This constrains inference that one H1 CFO print is sustainable owner FCF. Subsidiary's independent auditor mentions material going-concern uncertainty for an Indian subsidiary while group reviewers state its contribution is immaterial and the group conclusion unmodified. This is a subsidiary-specific watch item, not a modified group audit opinion [T1].

**Reopen on:** QIP use and project-level returns, actual diluted EPS after funding, clean recurring CFO through weak demand cycle, customer retention and independent director/incentive record. **WATCH_VALUATION_AND_CAPITAL_RETURNS; no new buy.**

## Xpro: distinguish standalone sales momentum from group owner returns

Issuer H1 FY27 presentation reports **INR351.1 crore revenue, +32.6%** (standalone/group revenue similar), with standalone **INR19.3 crore PAT, +89.9%** [X1]. The more relevant group P&L says **H1 consolidated PBT INR22.4 crore vs INR22.8 crore, -2.0%**, and **PAT before separately displayed FX adjustment INR15.2 crore vs INR19.3 crore, -21.6%**. This is not an assertion that normalized forward profit will fall, but contradicts a claim of currently proven strong consolidated owner earnings acceleration. Issuer also reports expanding dielectric film products still in customer validation.

Group H1 CFO **INR12.5 crore**; net investing cashflow **-INR68.9 crore**, meaning a combined CFO+investing cash deficit of **-INR56.4 crore**, not a pure maintenance-FCF estimate. September group CWIP INR360.0 crore, non-current/current borrowings INR232.6/41.1 crore and lease liabilities INR40.0/2.3 crore, group cash INR10.0 crore and other bank balances INR223.4 crore. Bank balance availability, security, subsidiary funding, and noncontrolling equity interests must be independently reconciled. No unsupported 'net cash' conclusion [X1]. Price INR1,194.80 (NSE close 16 Nov) is approximately 79.6× third-party TTM EPS, a demanding market expectation even if expansion ultimately succeeds [X2].

**Reopen on:** cash after capex, group vs parent earnings attribution, line yield and customer qualification, incremental ROIC, financing covenants and revised diluted valuations. **WATCH_RAMP_RETURN_ON_CAPITAL; no new buy.**
