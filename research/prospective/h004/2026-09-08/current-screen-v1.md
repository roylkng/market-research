The Sep-7 board-approved 50:50 JV for a 2.4 GW solar-cell facility is strategically material, but H004-R001 requires either a quantified capacity increase relative to existing capacity, first commercial operation, or another listed Grade-3/4 condition. The currently verified disclosure is a planned facility/JV, not commercial operation. Do not manufacture a catalyst grade after seeing the price.

The stock rose around 8% today after being declared L1 bidder. The contract value was not yet sufficiently verified under the H004 order-value thresholds and the price move is already near the pre-momentum boundary. This is a current-news mover, not the type of latent Stage-1 setup H004 is designed to find.

### Solar Industries — excellent company, wrong H004 timing

Its strong result was followed by an immediate roughly 8% result-day move to a lifetime-high area. That fails the spirit of the current pre-momentum opportunity search even if the long-term business remains attractive.

### Aarti Pharmalabs — already repriced

The stock hit a 20% upper circuit on the result reaction. H004 must not count an impossible or already-explosive entry as a pre-momentum success.

### GSFC — does not meet the frozen earnings-inflection rule

Revenue growth was strong, but PAT/EBITDA growth and margin progression did not meet the 4-test H004 earnings anchor. Do not promote it based only on the headline revenue number.

A ~₹903cr contract is positive but must clear the frozen order-value-to-TTM-revenue threshold to become a Grade-3/4 independent H004 catalyst. It is not automatically a signal because the absolute rupee value sounds large.

## What would promote a Stage-1 name to Stage 2

No candidate is promoted merely because this document ranks it highly.

At the formal daily snapshot, an already-qualified Stage-1 name still needs at least **two** of the frozen execution conditions:

1. daily return between +2% and +8%,
2. volume >=2.0x prior-20-session median,
3. delivery ratio >=1.5x prior-20-session median,
4. sector-relative return >=+2 percentage points,
5. close within 5% of its prior 60-session high.

The prior 5-session return must remain <15%, and an upper-circuit/no-offer state cannot be counted as an executable trigger.

For the highest-priority names, the preferred pattern is **not** a 10%-20% jump. It is a modest +2% to +6% recognition day accompanied by abnormal volume and/or sector-relative strength. That preserves lead time before the move becomes obvious.

## Falsification discipline

The separately recorded H004-HR001 manual challenge currently catches obvious information-driven cases such as Entero Healthcare and Bodal Chemicals but misses several unexplained or already-moving weekly winners. A naive small-sample classification is only ~2/5, below H004's frozen >=50% recall gate, but it is not a valid gate estimate because it is a winner-only sample and does not use the full 20-session label/executability reconstruction.

That miss pattern is retained rather than changing H004 thresholds.

The next broad replay must include the full eligible universe and false positives. If H004 cannot clear the frozen gate, it fails. Unexplained price/flow dislocations should then be handled by a separate anomaly experiment instead of contaminating this information-first signal.
