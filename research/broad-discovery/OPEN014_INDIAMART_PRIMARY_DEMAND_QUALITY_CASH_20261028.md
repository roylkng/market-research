The company presents **business enquiries delivered 87 million** and **unique business enquiries 27 million**, which are different definitions. Do not add these as unique transactions. Use industry-specific Silver/Gold/Platinum renewal/churn and paid lead consumption (still unpublished on a source-comparable basis) before inferring market share loss or inevitable subscriber collapse. ARPU is annualised **quarterly revenue / period-end paid supplier** and not proof each continuing supplier individually spends 10% more.

The 27-Oct primary release therefore upgrades OPEN012's already observed net paying supplier decline: **active buyer and lead-generation metrics are deteriorating simultaneously.** This is an adverse leading indicator and **thesis-pressure / risk escalation**, not a proven terminal franchise break or an immediate buy/sell signal.

## 5. Downside sensitivity, not a target

At group reported Q2 EBITDA ₹159cr, annualized mechanical current-quarter run-rate ₹636cr is **not sustainable forecast**. If, hypothetically, sustainable EBITDA were **10% lower**, at an assumed **12×** enterprise EBITDA valuation while treasury/other claims were fixed, the implied EV change would be **−₹763cr**, about **8% of indicative ₹9,572cr equity cap** before transaction costs, dilution or market-multiple change. This is **not calibrated probability**, trading prediction or price target. Changes in number of paid suppliers, revenue per merchant, acquisition spend and retained treasury can lead to substantially different outcomes.

**Disposition:** source-backed **INVESTIGATED_RENEWAL_LEAD_QUALITY_AT_RISK**, **NOT INVESTMENT_READY**. Existing cohort experiment source dates/symbols untouched.
