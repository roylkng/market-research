# HG009 P025. Reality check on the first 300-name NSE observation

**Method review date: October 21, 2026.** The selection and 300 controls were frozen October 14. The first 300-issuer market observation occurred October 15. This diagnostic method was developed **after seeing the October 15 price movements**. No part of P025 can be relabeled an original October 14 preregistered H1 hypothesis or a historical trading signal.

## Objective, not a victory narrative

The actual investment research objective is to identify genuinely undervalued NSE businesses with source-reconciled parent owner cash, dilution, risk, and costs, and to prove that a predeclared method outperforms realistic controls in later independent cohorts.

HG009's selected 100 are not automatically hidden gems. On October 15, the initial source-only daily price test showed underperformance versus both frozen controls, even though the selected group had significantly higher original October 5 growth and pretax margins than the zero-signal arm.

This report asks only: **does the negative first-day comparison survive different transparent, limited descriptive views of the same 300 source observations?**

## Original H1 observation and post-hoc sensitivity

| Selected (2 or 3 signals) | 99 | -1.4103% | -1.4627% | -1.2798% |

The original NSE Nifty500 *price index* changed **+0.5738% open-to-close**. This contrast highlights substantial composition/risk differences; it is **not** a demonstrated tradable shortfall relative to a market-index hedge.

After reading these first-day outcomes, we selected one deliberately minimal post-hoc sensitivity: use **only the original October 5 revenue-growth bands**, with cutoffs negative, 0–15%, above 15%, and unavailable. The selected group's *full original 100-person* population weights are **14%, 26%, 57%, and 3%**, respectively. Reweight each arm's conditional-on-observed H1 bucket means to those same fixed weights. If a comparison arm has zero priced observations in a positively weighted stratum, its standardized mean is **null**, not a reweight of the remaining groups.

| Descriptive H1 sensitivity | Selected | One-signal | Zero-signal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw source-observed mean | -1.4103% | -1.2690% | -1.0763% |
| Same selected-revenue-distribution weighted mean | **-1.4131%** | **-1.1927%** | **-1.2241%** |

The post-hoc selected-minus-control differences remain negative:
- Against one-signal: **-0.1413 pp raw** versus **about -0.2204 pp revenue-standardized**.
- Against zero-signal: **-0.3340 pp raw** versus **about -0.1890 pp revenue-standardized**.

This does **not** isolate the effect of an LLM, because matching coarse turnover and four revenue-growth buckets does not adjust sector, enterprise size, volatility, float, accounting regime, profitability, events or intraday risk. Company observations on one day are correlated. We do not publish bootstrap p-values, a one-session Sharpe ratio, annualized alpha, investor total return or a buy/sell conclusion.

## H5 pre-exit protocol and explicit selection-bias warning

The identical four source-growth buckets and unmodified selected-company original weights are declared on October 21, *before the scheduled October 22 official NSE H5 exit*. The only permitted future H5 source is the separately sealed HG009 P006 H5 market-source report. The source must prove exact complete holding sessions:

October 15, 16, 19, 21, 22. October 20 was the Dussehra NSE holiday.

The same P025 script accepts H5 only after its original NSE sources and P006 signed result are available. Missing intermediate sessions, absent group observations, symbol/ISIN substitutions and nonexistent corporate-action clearance must not become an artificial winner or zero return.

**H5 is not an independent confirmatory sample.** Its first trading day was already visible when these secondary sensitivity rules were fixed, and its cohort is exactly the same 300 companies. H5 can be a useful descriptive update, but cannot validate a profitable annual alpha strategy or erase the negative H1 observations.

## What changes the investment research outcome

Prioritize eventual *independent, future-dated* 90/180/252-session screens, source-complete corporate-action/shareholder-return adjustments, sector/size/beta matched controls, data-capture missingness and after-cost execution. At company level require original repeatable owner cash and a contemporaneous diluted-share/market-depth bridge. Neither H1 nor H5 price-only data can substitute for a margin-of-safety valuation.
