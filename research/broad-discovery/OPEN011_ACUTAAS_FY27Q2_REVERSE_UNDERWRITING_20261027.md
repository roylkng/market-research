## What do the good results need to justify?

**Frozen dated pre-release price reference only:** 26 October NSE close **₹3,348.80** and reported approximate equity market cap **₹27,409cr** ([dated history](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/ACUTAAS/history/)). The October 27 announcement occurred *after that referenced session*, and ₹3,348.80 is **not a guaranteed post-results executable purchase price**. Today's actual free float, fully diluted share count, price and spreads are **not source-verified** for valuation here.

Instead of proposing arbitrary BUY price targets, perform **three-year reverse earnings underwriting**:

Assume, only hypothetically:
- required compounded shareholder equity value appreciation **12% each year over three years**; *not a promise or a return forecast*;
- end-of-three-years valuation multiple of **20×, 25×, 30× or 40×** annual group PAT;
- no dividends, taxes, dilution, buybacks, debt-equity bridge, acquisition/lease claims, changing shares or trading costs (all are *real* missing claims);
- starting profit *diagnostic* of **2 × last reported H1 group PAT = ₹396.2704cr**, **not** the company's FY27 forecast, normalized PAT or parent shareholder cash.

The reference equity value needed three years out = `27,409 × 1.12^3 ≈ ₹38,508cr`.

| Hypothetical year-three P/E | Required year-three annual group PAT ₹cr | CAGR over ₹396.27cr mechanical reference |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20× | **1,925.38** | **69.37% / year** |
| 25× | **1,540.31** | **57.23% / year** |
| 30× | **1,283.59** | **47.96% / year** |
| 40× | **962.69** | **34.43% / year** |

The Oct26 market cap / doubled H1 total PAT reference is **69.17×**, a *mechanical price-to-annualized-half-year-profit ratio*, NOT a twelve-month audited or fully diluted P/E. Applying an ordinary **30×** earnings multiple in three years while achieving a **12% annual equity return** would demand **nearly 48% compounded group PAT growth** starting from that doubled-H1 reference. Higher multiple retention reduces the required growth, but defending 40× across an extended period is itself an aggressive scenario. Present reported +71% YoY quarterly PAT growth does **not** establish a 48% sustainable earnings CAGR over three years or any after-cost investor return.

**Missing owner's economic value:** share-specific EPS denominator, minority loss exposure, fully consolidated after-tax CFO, capex split (maintenance vs expansion), credit/incentive economics, sustainable Korean semiconductor revenue and returns, consolidated gross debt/leases, acquisitions/related-party transfers, and execution taxes/costs. No probability based on management guidance is admissible. The positive reported earnings are genuine as a reported claim, but **no favorable long-horizon margin of safety is demonstrated**.
